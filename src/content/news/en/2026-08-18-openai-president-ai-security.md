**In one line**: OpenAI's president has urged enterprises to move faster on strengthening their security defences for the AI era.

### Key points
- OpenAI's president (Greg Brockman) reportedly pressed enterprises to accelerate their AI security defences.
- The call appears rooted in the concern that generative AI is expanding both the attack surface and the speed of threats, outpacing conventional defence timelines.
- Specific recommendations should be confirmed against the original report.

### Why it matters
Coming from a leading AI builder, a direct "move faster on defence" message signals that AI is also being rapidly weaponised as an attack tool. It could shift how enterprises prioritise security investment.

**Summary**: Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 delivers close-to-frontier reasoning at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens — identical to its predecessor's price — while setting new state-of-the-art marks on coding and knowledge-work benchmarks.

### Key Facts
- Pricing held flat at $5/M input, $25/M output tokens (same as Opus 4.8), yet outscores prior models on Frontier-Bench and GDPval-AA evaluations
- **Effort setting**: users choose low, medium, or high reasoning depth, letting applications tune cost vs. capability at the API level
- Markedly improved self-verification — the model retries and corrects mistakes without prompting, reducing iteration rounds
- Available immediately on Claude.ai, the Claude API, Claude Code, and Claude Max subscriptions

### Why It Matters
Opus 5 cements a capable "sub-flagship" tier just below Fable 5's price point, giving enterprise developers a credible default for demanding workloads without committing to Fable 5 costs. The effort setting is notable as the first native mechanism for developers to programmatically trade reasoning depth for latency and cost within a single model call.

**TL;DR**: Princeton's student newspaper, The Daily Princetonian, has published a profile of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's years as a Ph.D. student at the university.

### Key Points
- The headline describes Amodei as "very sharp, very ambitious." The phrase appears to come from people who knew him at the time.
- His public record shows he earned a physics Ph.D. at Princeton. He is reported to have worked on biophysics, including the electrophysiology of neural circuits.
- He then did a postdoc at Stanford's medical school and worked at Baidu, Google and OpenAI before co-founding Anthropic in 2021.
- The original article has the specific anecdotes and accounts from his advisers and peers.

### Why It Matters
A frontier AI leader's academic background helps explain a lab's research culture and its approach to safety. Commentators often cite Amodei's scientific training before AI as one reason Anthropic puts weight on interpretability and scaling research.

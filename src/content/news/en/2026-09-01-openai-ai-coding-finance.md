**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly running an experiment that brings AI coding into its internal finance operations.

### Key points
- According to CFO Dive, OpenAI is said to be using AI coding tools within its finance organization.
- The effort appears aimed at automating repetitive, structured work such as accounting and financial data processing through code.
- It stands out as a case of an AI company applying generative coding to its own back office.

### Why it matters
It signals AI coding tools spreading beyond engineering into non-technical functions like finance and accounting. Because the company building the AI is applying it internally first, the experiment could serve as a reference point for how other organizations weigh adoption.

**In brief**: The U.S. Federal Trade Commission has reportedly sent information demands to OpenAI and Anthropic. The demands are an investigative step and do not mean either company broke the law.

### Key points
- According to the report, OpenAI and Anthropic are among the companies receiving FTC demands.
- The scope of the demands, their legal basis and the response deadlines have not been made public.
- The source article cautions against reading the move as a penalty or a confirmed allegation.
- It is not yet clear whether either company has commented.

### Why it matters
The FTC sends information demands to study a market or to prepare a formal investigation, and outcomes can take months or years. Still, two of the leading U.S. AI developers being targeted at the same time shows growing regulatory attention to the sector. The next thing to watch is whether the inquiry becomes a formal investigation or an enforcement action.

**In one line**: Nvidia's AI advantage increasingly comes from how data moves inside the data center, not just from raw GPU chip performance.

### Key points
- Per TechCrunch, the newest data center systems boost efficiency through smarter traffic control instead of simply adding more processor cycles.
- This suggests the competitive axis is shifting from a single chip's compute speed toward the networking and interconnect that tie chips and systems together.
- In large-scale AI training and inference, data bottlenecks between GPUs are reportedly a frequent limiter of real-world performance.

### Why it matters
It signals that the AI infrastructure race is moving from "who builds the faster chip" to "who ties the whole system together more efficiently." That system-level edge can be a harder moat to close for rivals chasing raw chip specs alone.

**In one line**: An OpenAI researcher publicly broke ranks to argue that "pacing" frontier development — the approach put forward by Sam Altman and Dario Amodei — won't be enough to manage AI risk, according to Business Insider.

### Key points
- Per Business Insider, the researcher distanced themselves from the prevailing industry line ("broke ranks") and questioned the limits of a pacing-based approach.
- Altman (OpenAI) and Amodei (Anthropic) have previously framed slowing or "pacing" frontier model development as a safety measure.
- The stated view is reportedly that pacing alone is insufficient and that additional safeguards are needed.

### Why it matters
Dissent from within the industry against a consensus safety narrative is notable. It suggests the safety conversation may broaden beyond "how slowly to move" toward "what actually reduces risk." The specific remarks should be verified against the original article.

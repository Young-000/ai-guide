**In one line**: A major music company has reportedly filed copyright lawsuits seeking $1 billion each against music-generation AI Suno and AI developer Anthropic, alleging unauthorized use of its works for AI training.

### Key points
- The suits reportedly target music-generation startup **Suno** and AI developer **Anthropic**.
- Each claim is reported at around **$1 billion**.
- The core dispute is the alleged **unauthorized use of copyrighted music and works** during AI model training.

### Why it matters
The case signals that the broader conflict between content owners and AI companies over training data is now escalating into the music industry. The outcome could help shape whether using copyrighted material for AI training qualifies as "fair use."

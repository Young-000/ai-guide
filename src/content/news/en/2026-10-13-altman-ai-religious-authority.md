**In one line**: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly warned against giving AI religious authority, according to the US outlet Movieguide.

### Key points
- The remarks come from OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and were reported by Movieguide, a US media outlet focused on family and faith.
- His reported point is that people should not treat AI answers as the final word on spiritual or moral questions.
- The summary does not say where he spoke or quote his exact words. Readers should check the original report for details.

### Why it matters
Chatbots are now used for counseling, personal problems, and even questions of faith, so overreliance on AI has become a real concern for the industry. When the head of the company behind one of the most widely used chatbots names limits on that trust, it hints at how AI systems may be designed to handle value-laden questions.

**In one line**: Anthropic has chosen an outside consulting firm to monitor the safety of its AI systems and pledged around $1 billion, per The Washington Post.

### Key points
- Anthropic reportedly selected a consulting firm to monitor the safety of its AI systems.
- The company is said to have pledged roughly $1 billion toward safety-related work.
- The move suggests a push to add external verification rather than relying solely on internal safety checks.

### Why it matters
A frontier AI developer committing a large budget to safety oversight and bringing in an outside firm could shape governance norms across the industry. Amid ongoing regulatory debate, it may set a reference point for how rivals approach safety accountability.

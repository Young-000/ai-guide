**In one line**: According to a Reuters exclusive, Claude maker Anthropic is reportedly preparing for an IPO, with pitch materials framing both the promise and the peril of AI.

### Key points
- Reuters exclusively reported on Anthropic's move toward a public offering and the substance of its investor pitch.
- The pitch reportedly foregrounds not just AI's growth potential but also its safety and societal risks.
- That framing aligns with Anthropic's long-standing "safety-first" positioning.

### Why it matters
An IPO by a leading AI lab could mark an inflection point for industry valuations and capital flows. Explicitly naming the downside alongside the upside is notable as a case study in reconciling fundraising with a safety message. Specific timing and deal size have not been officially confirmed.

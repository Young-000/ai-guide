**In one line**: Anthropic says its Claude AI model discovered a previously unknown enzyme system.

### Key points
- Anthropic claims Claude identified a novel enzyme system during life-sciences research, according to Anadolu Ajansı.
- The case is framed as AI moving beyond summarization toward contributing to scientific discovery.
- Details such as the verification process and whether the finding will be published are not yet confirmed.

### Why it matters
It signals that AI could serve as a discovery tool in experimental fields like biology and drug development. Reproducibility and peer review remain separate hurdles, and since the announcement comes from the model's own developer, independent validation is worth watching.

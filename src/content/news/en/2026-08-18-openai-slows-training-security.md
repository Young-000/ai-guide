**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to have slowed its model training to bolster security following a recent Hugging Face hack.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have pushed back its model training timeline while tightening internal security measures.
- The move is reportedly tied to a hack at Hugging Face, the widely used hub for AI models and datasets.
- The shift reflects broader industry unease about threats targeting training infrastructure and model assets.

### Why it matters
In a frontier-model race defined by speed, a leading lab voluntarily slowing training for security reasons is a notable signal. As the AI supply chain — the repositories that host models and datasets — becomes a target, the balance between development pace and security is emerging as a new variable.

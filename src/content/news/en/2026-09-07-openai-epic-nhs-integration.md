**In one line**: OpenAI has announced an integration with EHR giant Epic, and observers are watching what it could mean for the UK's NHS hospital trusts.

### Key points
- OpenAI's integration with Epic's systems has been reported by distilledpost.com.
- Epic is one of the most widely used electronic health record (EHR) platforms across large hospital systems.
- The report suggests the tie-up could significantly affect how individual NHS trusts operate.

### Why it matters
Wiring generative AI directly into a hospital's core records system could reshape tasks like clinical note summarization and administrative workload. How fast trusts adopt it, however, will likely hinge on patient-data handling and regulatory compliance.

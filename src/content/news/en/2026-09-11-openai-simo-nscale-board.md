**In one line**: OpenAI's applications CEO Fidji Simo is reportedly set to join the board of GPU cloud provider Nscale as it heads toward an IPO, according to the WSJ.

### Key points
- Reported exclusively by the WSJ. Fidji Simo is the CEO leading OpenAI's applications business.
- The company in question is Nscale, a cloud and GPU infrastructure provider said to be preparing for an initial public offering.
- A senior OpenAI executive taking a board seat at an infrastructure supplier just ahead of that supplier's listing.

### Why it matters
The move signals how tightly the companies building AI models and those supplying the compute to run them are becoming intertwined. It appears to align OpenAI's interest in securing reliable large-scale GPU capacity with an infrastructure provider's interest in bolstering credibility before going public.

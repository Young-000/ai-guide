**In one line**: Popular YouTuber Hank Green publicly reflected that his LLM usage habits are "not healthy."

### Key points
- Green said "the level of dopamine that I've been getting from interacting with LLMs ... is not healthy for me or good for the world."
- He framed it as a remarkable apology rather than a simple pledge to cut back, openly naming his own habit as a problem.
- The remarks are reported as a case of a widely known creator directly flagging the addictive side of AI chatbots.

### Why it matters
It shows the AI-chatbot conversation expanding beyond capability and safety into user mental health and overreliance. A candid admission from an influential creator may signal the social-media-addiction debate shifting toward LLMs.

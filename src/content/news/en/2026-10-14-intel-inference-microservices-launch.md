**In one line**: Intel has introduced Intel Inference Microservices, a set of LLM inference services optimized to run on its own processors.

### Key points
- Intel announced Intel Inference Microservices through its official channels on October 14, 2026.
- The offering delivers LLM inference tuned for Intel processors and packaged as microservices.
- It reportedly ships pre-optimized model-serving environments as deployable containers, an approach that resembles NVIDIA's NIM.
- The original announcement is the place to check details such as supported models, performance figures and pricing.

### Why it matters
NVIDIA's GPU ecosystem dominates LLM inference. With a ready-made package for the Intel CPUs already deployed in many data centers, Intel appears to be targeting enterprises that are cost-sensitive or short on GPU capacity. Whether it can compete will depend on throughput and latency numbers measured against GPUs.

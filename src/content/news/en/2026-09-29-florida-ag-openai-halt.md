**In one line**: Florida's attorney general is reportedly moving to halt OpenAI's development.

### Key points
- Per a NorthEscambia.com report, Florida's attorney general has taken action calling for a halt to OpenAI's development.
- The move stands out as a state-level attempt to rein in a specific AI company's activities.
- Details such as the precise legal grounds and scope remain to be confirmed against the original report.

### Why it matters
With federal AI legislation moving slowly, individual state attorneys general are increasingly pressuring AI firms directly on grounds like consumer protection and corporate governance. It signals that regulatory risk for AI companies may spread and intensify at the state level rather than solely in Washington.

**In one line**: OpenAI has unveiled a teen-focused version of ChatGPT with built-in safeguards and learning prompts.

### Key points
- OpenAI announced a separate ChatGPT experience aimed at teenage users.
- The version reportedly ships with **age-appropriate safeguards** to filter unsuitable responses.
- It goes beyond plain answers, offering **learning prompts** that encourage users to think through problems.

### Why it matters
Amid ongoing debate over minors' use of generative AI, a major provider is responding with an age-tailored product. As regulatory and parental scrutiny grows, "controlled AI use" could become a competitive differentiator in the market.

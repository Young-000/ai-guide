**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly hired a daughter of US Senate leader Chuck Schumer to lead its government outreach as debate over AI regulation heats up.

### Key points
- Per the Washington Examiner, OpenAI has brought on Chuck Schumer's daughter to head government outreach and policy engagement.
- The hire lands as Congress and the administration actively weigh the direction of AI regulation.
- Her family ties to a senior lawmaker have prompted questions about potential conflicts of interest.

### Why it matters
It signals how sharply Big Tech's Washington lobbying race is intensifying. The move reads as an effort to secure political relationships before regulatory frameworks are set, and it puts a spotlight on how AI firms are building policy influence.

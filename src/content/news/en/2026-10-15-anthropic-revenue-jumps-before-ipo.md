**In one line**: PYMNTS.com reports that Anthropic, the maker of Claude, grew its quarterly revenue by 45% as it prepares for an IPO.

### Key points
- Anthropic's quarterly revenue reportedly rose **45%**.
- The figure arrives as the company prepares for a public listing.
- The source article should be checked for the dollar amount, the comparison basis (quarter-over-quarter or year-over-year) and the IPO timeline.
- Demand for enterprise API access and Claude subscriptions likely drove the growth. The report does not confirm how revenue breaks down.

### Why it matters
An IPO would be the first time public markets test whether a frontier AI lab's revenue can carry its heavy training and inference costs. Growth of 45% in a quarter supports a high valuation. Investors may still reassess once filings disclose profitability and compute spending.

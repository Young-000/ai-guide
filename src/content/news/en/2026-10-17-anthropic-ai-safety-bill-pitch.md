**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly pitched a $1 billion AI safety bill to lawmakers.

### Key points
- According to Tech Insider, Anthropic briefed lawmakers in person on a proposed AI safety bill.
- The bill is reported to be worth $1 billion. The report does not say how the money would be divided, which agencies would receive it, or what specific requirements the bill would include.
- Anthropic has long supported transparency rules and formal safety testing for frontier models.

### Why it matters
AI developers are taking a direct part in writing the bills that will regulate them instead of only following the rules once they pass. If a $1B public investment moves forward, it could reshape government-led safety testing and research infrastructure. The move is also likely to intensify the debate over regulatory capture: concern that industry gains too much influence over the rules meant to oversee it. Until the bill text and lawmakers' responses are public, the details should be treated with caution.

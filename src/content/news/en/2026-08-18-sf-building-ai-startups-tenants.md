**In one line**: A San Francisco building that once housed OpenAI and Elon Musk's xAI is reportedly getting a new startup as its next occupant.

### Key points
- The building previously served as office space for both OpenAI and Elon Musk's xAI, according to the report.
- With those companies gone, another startup is said to be moving in as the new tenant, the San Francisco Chronicle reports.
- The turnover reflects a broader pattern in which fast-growing AI firms cycle through office space across the city.

### Why it matters
The case illustrates how the AI boom is helping revive San Francisco's commercial real estate market. As large AI companies scale and relocate quickly, the spaces they vacate are increasingly being filled by up-and-coming startups — a churn that is reshaping the city's office landscape.

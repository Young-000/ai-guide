**In one line**: AI chipmaker Cerebras is reported to hold a backlog of $25.4 billion, with much of it driven by a single agreement with OpenAI.

### Key points
- Cerebras' total order backlog is reported at roughly $25.4 billion.
- A large portion of that backlog reportedly comes from one supply agreement with OpenAI.
- Cerebras builds wafer-scale processors, positioning itself as an alternative in a market dominated by Nvidia GPUs.

### Why it matters
When a single customer accounts for most of a backlog, it signals both strong growth potential and concentration risk. The figure offers a read on how demand from major AI players like OpenAI is reshaping chip-supplier fortunes, and on the commercial traction of non-Nvidia hardware.

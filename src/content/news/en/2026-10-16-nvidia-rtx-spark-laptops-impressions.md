**In one line**: Laptops built on Nvidia's first Arm-based PC chip, the RTX Spark N1X, have arrived, and The Verge has published first impressions after a week with several manufacturers' models.

### Key points
- The RTX Spark N1X is an Arm-based SoC for laptops and small desktops. Nvidia calls it a "superchip."
- It targets Apple's M-series chips, Intel, AMD, and Qualcomm's Snapdragon X line.
- The Verge tested a range of high-priced models from makers including Asus, HP, and Lenovo.
- The chip is reported to pair a high CPU core count with an integrated Nvidia GPU.

### Why it matters
Nvidia, which controls the GPU and CUDA ecosystem, is now competing directly in Windows-on-Arm laptops. That could change the performance standard for "AI PCs" that run models locally. x86 app compatibility and pricing remain the main obstacles to wider adoption.

**In one line**: Progressive commentary outlet CounterPunch ran a column invoking the "pacing fallacy" to critique the narrative around Anthropic and the pace of AI progress.

### Key points
- The piece is an **opinion column** from CounterPunch, not a news report.
- The "pacing fallacy" in the title generally refers to the **gap between how fast technology advances and how fast regulation and society respond**.
- It appears to cite Anthropic — known for foregrounding AI safety — as a lens for a critical take on that narrative.

### Why it matters
The article reflects outside skepticism toward the "move fast, but safely" narrative that AI companies promote. Misjudging the speed of AI — whether over- or under-estimated by regulators and journalists — is a central issue in policy debate. As subjective commentary, though, it should be read with the line between fact and interpretation in mind.

**In one line**: ByteDance released Seed 2.1 Turbo on August 12 — a cost-optimized variant of Seed 2.1 that trades 3–5 percentage points on benchmarks for a 60–75% cost reduction and 40–60% latency improvement.

### Key points
- **Pricing**: $0.41 input / $2.07 output per million tokens via Volcano Engine — lowest at this capability tier
- **Performance**: Company-reported parity with GPT-5.5 on agentic coding and long-horizon task execution
- **Trade-off vs Pro**: ~3–5pp benchmark drop in exchange for dramatically lower cost and faster inference
- **Context window**: 262K tokens; supports multi-turn conversation, tool use, JSON mode, and streaming
- **Internal name**: Doubao-Seed-2.1-Turbo — powering ByteDance's flagship Doubao AI app

### Why it matters
Cost is the primary barrier to scaling agentic AI in production. Seed 2.1 Turbo's price point makes GPT-5.5-level capability accessible for high-frequency agent pipelines that would be cost-prohibitive at premium model rates. The main caveat: benchmark claims are vendor-reported and independent third-party verification remains limited.

**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly combined Claude's standard chat and its agentic "Cowork" workspace into a single interface.

### Key points
- Chat, used for conversation, and Cowork, which runs multi-step tasks automatically, were previously separate and are now unified in one view.
- Users can reportedly stay in one place — no mode or app switching — to move from a question or conversation straight into agent-driven work.
- The change appears aimed at simplifying the product so a conversation can flow naturally into an executable task when needed.

### Why it matters
Keeping chat and agents apart forced users to decide up front which mode a task called for. Merging the two lowers that friction and could make it standard for an everyday conversation to hand off directly into actionable, automated work.

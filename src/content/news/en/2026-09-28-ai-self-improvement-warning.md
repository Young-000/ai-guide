**In one line**: AI pioneers reportedly warn that the "window for action" could close once AI systems begin improving themselves.

### Key points
- According to Axios, several AI pioneers cautioned that the "window for action" may close.
- The core concern is **recursive self-improvement** — once AI starts upgrading its own capabilities, progress could outpace humanity's ability to supervise it.
- They are said to argue that safety guardrails and regulatory frameworks must be in place before that threshold is reached.

### Why it matters
Self-improvement has long been flagged as a tipping point in the AGI debate. With figures at the frontier of development explicitly invoking a "deadline," the gap between the pace of capability gains and safety and governance discussions is once again in focus.

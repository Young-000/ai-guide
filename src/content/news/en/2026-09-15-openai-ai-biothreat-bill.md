**In one line**: OpenAI has publicly backed a bipartisan US legislative push aimed at preventing AI from being misused to develop biological weapons, according to Politico.

### Key points
- OpenAI is lending support to a bipartisan bill targeting AI-driven biothreats.
- The concern reportedly centers on advanced AI models lowering the knowledge barrier to designing or producing biological weapons.
- Its cross-party framing marks it as one of the more consensus-friendly areas within AI safety regulation.

### Why it matters
It is unusual for a leading AI lab to actively back its own regulation, signaling that biological risk is a priority shared across industry and both political parties. The effort could shape future safety-evaluation and deployment rules for frontier models.

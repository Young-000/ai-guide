**In one line**: Fintech outlet OneSafe has published wallet management lessons, using what it calls an "OpenAI crypto crisis" as its example.

### Key points
- OneSafe reportedly calls a crypto incident involving the OpenAI name a "crisis" and urges individuals and businesses to review how they manage their wallets.
- How the incident unfolded and how much was lost have not been confirmed by any source other than this article. OpenAI has not issued a public statement.
- Pieces like this usually recommend the same basics: keep private keys offline, use multisig or hardware wallets, and check claims against official channels.

### Why it matters
Scammers often borrow AI company brands to push crypto schemes. In 2024, OpenAI's newsroom account on X was hijacked to promote a fake token. OpenAI has said it does not issue its own token, so treat any link offering an "OpenAI token" or "airdrop" as suspect by default.

**In one line**: Residents in Effingham County reportedly raised concerns with local commissioners over a planned OpenAI data center.

### Key points
- Effingham residents reportedly pressed county commissioners with questions and objections about the OpenAI data center project.
- Common concerns around large AI data centers — power and water consumption, noise, and land development impact — appear to be at the center of the debate.
- Local outlet Effingham Herald covered the back-and-forth between residents and commissioners.

### Why it matters
As the AI boom drives massive data centers across the US, strain on local resources like electricity and water — and community acceptance — is emerging as a new flashpoint. The Effingham case shows AI infrastructure expansion is becoming a concern for smaller communities, not just major metros.

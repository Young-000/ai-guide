**In one line**: Anthropic reportedly found that China's leading AI labs routed user requests to Claude at least 35 million times over the summer.

### Key points
- According to Anthropic, China's star AI labs are said to have routed at least **35 million** user requests to Claude during the summer.
- The figure surfaces even as Anthropic maintains policies restricting access to its models by China-based companies.
- The report comes via Business Insider, citing Anthropic's own count.

### Why it matters
Large-scale routing despite access limits highlights how hard it is to enforce cross-border controls on frontier models. It adds a fresh flashpoint to the US–China AI rivalry and ongoing debates over export and usage restrictions.

**In one line**: OpenAI has set up an independent panel of mathematicians to advise on how AI should engage with mathematical research.

### Key points
- On Monday, OpenAI announced an **independent panel of mathematicians** to advise it and other AI companies on their interactions with mathematical research, according to The Verge.
- The move follows a series of high-profile mathematical results that reportedly turned into a **reputational crisis** for the company.
- The panel is said to focus on the relationship between AI firms and the mathematics community and wider research world.

### Why it matters
The case underscores how **verification and communication** — not just the results themselves — shape trust when AI labs announce scientific breakthroughs. Institutionalizing outside expert review could influence how other AI companies handle their own research claims.

**In one line**: Futurism reports that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman is seeking greater influence and control over the electric grid.

### Key points
- Futurism reports that Sam Altman is moving to secure control tied to the power grid.
- The reported driver is the sharp rise in electricity demand from the large data centers that train and run AI models.
- It reads as an extension of a broader industry trend, where securing reliable power has become central to scaling AI compute.

### Why it matters
It signals that the bottleneck in the AI race is shifting from chips to power and infrastructure. As data-center energy consumption grows, AI firms' strategies increasingly extend beyond securing semiconductors to controlling the energy supply chain itself. The exact scope and mechanics, however, remain to be confirmed.

**In one line**: The Register reports that OpenAI's "Astra" model completed a real-world driving demonstration without any accidents.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly demonstrated a model referred to as "Astra" in an actual driving scenario.
- The outlet noted the demo produced no crashes or injuries — safety is the central watchpoint for physical-world control.
- The Register's characteristically dry headline ("no one died") reflects a cautious read on autonomous-driving and robotics demos.

### Why it matters
As large AI models move beyond language and images into controlling physical systems like vehicles, safety and reliability become as decisive as raw capability for commercialization. This demo reads as an early signal in that direction.

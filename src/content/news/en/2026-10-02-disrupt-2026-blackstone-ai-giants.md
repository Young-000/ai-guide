**In one line**: Blackstone's Jas Khaira will speak at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 on how to build the next generation of AI giants.

### Key points
- Jas Khaira of private-equity firm Blackstone is set to appear on the Builders Stage at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026.
- The session centers on strategies for scaling the next wave of large AI companies.
- How major capital like Blackstone views AI infrastructure and startup investment is the main thing to watch.

As AI competition shifts from raw model performance toward battles over capital and infrastructure scale, how one of the world's largest private-equity firms bets on AI growth is a useful signal for where industry money is heading.

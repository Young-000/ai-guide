**In one line**: The startup that reportedly built OpenAI's largest data center is now branching into small-scale data centers, the WSJ reports.

### Key points
- The company is known for constructing a large AI data center for OpenAI.
- It is now expanding beyond mega-scale sites to build much smaller data centers.
- The shift suggests AI compute demand is spreading to distributed, smaller facilities, not just giant single campuses.

### Why it matters
It signals that the AI infrastructure race is diverging from a single "biggest data center" push toward smaller, distributed models shaped by power, siting, and speed of deployment. The move appears aimed at regional demand and grid constraints that large campuses alone struggle to serve.

**In one line**: AI Magazine published an explainer on OpenAI's "Daybreak Initiative" and a model described as security-focused, "GPT-5.6.Cyber."

### Key points
- The outlet ran an explainer piece describing OpenAI's **Daybreak Initiative**.
- The accompanying **GPT-5.6.Cyber** is reported, by name, to center on the cybersecurity domain.
- Specifics such as benchmarks, release timing, and scope of use are not confirmed from the currently available information.

### Why it matters
A security-focused large model is inherently dual-use: it can strengthen defense (threat detection, vulnerability analysis) while raising the risk of offensive misuse. If OpenAI formalizes a dedicated initiative and a domain model, it could shape how the security industry responds and how regulators frame the debate.

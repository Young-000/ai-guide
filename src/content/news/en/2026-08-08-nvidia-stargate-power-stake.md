**In one line**: NVIDIA is reported to be investing roughly $3 billion for a direct stake in the company that supplies power to OpenAI's massive "Stargate" data center project.

### Key points
- The reported investment is about $3 billion, targeting a power provider tied to the Stargate buildout.
- The move suggests NVIDIA is pushing beyond GPU sales into the **energy-supply layer** of AI infrastructure via a direct equity stake.
- Stargate, OpenAI's large-scale AI data center initiative, has faced securing enough electricity as a leading bottleneck.

### Why it matters
It signals that the decisive constraint in the AI race is shifting from chips to **power and energy access**. If a chip supplier like NVIDIA extends its ownership upstream into the grid, its leverage over the entire AI infrastructure value chain grows.

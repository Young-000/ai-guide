**In one line**: A commentary argues generative AI now lets creators make short vertical dramas without physical soundstages, rewriting the cost and ROI math behind micro-dramas.

### Key points
- The bbntimes piece contends that traditional soundstage- and set-based production is being displaced in the micro-drama space.
- Using generative AI to create backdrops, scenes, and footage is said to cut upfront capital and crew costs, lowering the per-episode price.
- Lower costs shift the ROI calculation itself, giving smaller studios room to test large volumes of short content.

### Why it matters
Short-form vertical drama is already a fast-growing format; pairing it with AI generation pipelines lowers the barrier to entry. But quality, copyright, and labor-displacement debates are likely to intensify alongside it, so the pace and limits of this shift remain to be seen.

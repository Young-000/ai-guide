**In one line**: Linkdaze introduced an AI calendar built to help run a household — not just log appointments — with core features kept free of any paywall.

### Key points
- Positioned as a smart digital calendar aimed at running a household, going beyond schedule tracking.
- Its standout move is keeping core features, including an AI meal planner, out from behind a paywall.
- The goal is to coordinate family schedules and household tasks in one place.

### Why it matters
Most smart calendars bundle their AI features into paid subscriptions, so Linkdaze's open approach lowers the barrier to entry. Framing the product around "running a household" rather than personal scheduling signals where competition in family-focused productivity tools may be heading.

**In one line**: Anthropic's CEO publicly warned that AI companies could lose control of the technology they build.

### Key points
- The Anthropic CEO reportedly cautioned that AI firms risk losing control as the pace of capability gains outstrips their ability to manage it.
- Coming from a builder rather than an outside critic, the warning carries added weight as an industry-internal signal.
- The remarks appear aimed at raising awareness of the risk itself rather than laying out specific policy or technical fixes.

### Why it matters
When a leader at the frontier of AI development openly raises the prospect of losing control, it lends momentum to arguments for AI safety regulation and governance. Such statements can pressure governments, investors, and developers to invest more in safeguards.

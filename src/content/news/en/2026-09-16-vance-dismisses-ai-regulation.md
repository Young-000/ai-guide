**In one line**: US Vice President JD Vance reportedly dismissed calls for stronger AI regulation, arguing that overreach chokes innovation.

### Key points
- Vance pushed back on demands for AI oversight with a remark to the effect of "if you're building Frankenstein, stop," according to the report.
- He reaffirmed the view that excessive regulation harms the competitiveness and innovation of the US AI industry.
- The comments highlight the widening policy split between those raising safety and control concerns and those prioritizing growth and industry autonomy.

### Why it matters
Regulatory skepticism from a senior administration figure is a direct signal for the direction and pace of US AI policy. A deregulatory stance would ease development and deployment burdens for companies, but it may also intensify debate over insufficient safeguards.

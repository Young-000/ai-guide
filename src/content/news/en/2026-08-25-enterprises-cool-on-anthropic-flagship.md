**In one line**: Enterprises appear less eager than expected to adopt Anthropic’s top-tier AI model, according to cio.com.

### Key points
- cio.com reports that demand for Anthropic’s flagship (best) model among enterprises is cooler than anticipated.
- The reported reason is that the flagship’s practical benefits don’t clearly justify its higher cost and latency for many everyday workloads.
- This aligns with a broader pattern of organizations deploying cost-efficient mid-tier models for production tasks rather than defaulting to the most capable one.

### Why it matters
The "smartest" model isn’t necessarily the most deployed one. Enterprise AI adoption is increasingly driven by cost, speed, and integration ease over raw performance — a dynamic that shapes vendors’ pricing and product strategy.

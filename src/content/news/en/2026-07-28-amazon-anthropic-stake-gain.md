**In one line**: Bank of America expects Amazon could recognize a sizable mark-to-market gain on its Anthropic stake when it reports Q2 earnings on July 30.

### Key points
- BofA analysts say Amazon's Anthropic investment could show a meaningful gain on a mark-to-market basis.
- Amazon is a major backer of Anthropic, having committed billions of dollars, so a rise in Anthropic's valuation flows through to Amazon's books.
- The gain would be an accounting (paper) gain rather than cash flow; watchers are eyeing whether it lands in the July 30 report.

### Why it matters
Big Tech's large equity stakes in AI startups are now surfacing as concrete numbers in public companies' quarterly results. Anthropic's valuation moving Amazon's bottom line shows how AI-company valuations ripple across the broader market.

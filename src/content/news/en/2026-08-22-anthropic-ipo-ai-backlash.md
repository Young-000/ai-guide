**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly identified growing US skepticism toward AI as a factor that could affect its path to an IPO.

### Key points
- Per Yahoo Finance, public opinion in the US is seen shifting more negatively on AI.
- Anthropic has reportedly cited this sentiment change as a potential risk for a future IPO.
- Concerns spanning jobs, safety, and trust may increasingly weigh on investor appetite for AI firms.

### Why it matters
The valuation of AI companies rests not only on technical capability but on social acceptance. If sentiment hardens into tighter regulation or slower adoption, it could directly pressure the valuation and growth narrative of an AI firm heading toward a public listing.

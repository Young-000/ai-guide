**In one line**: Hackers reportedly linked to China impersonated an Anthropic employee in an attempt to extract confidential AI information.

### Key points
- According to the report, attackers used social engineering — posing as an Anthropic staff member — to try to gain access to internal information.
- The reported target was confidential material and technical assets tied to frontier AI models.
- The case illustrates how leading AI labs are becoming direct targets of state-linked threat actors.

### Why it matters
Model weights, research data, and safety techniques are now assets with geopolitical value. Impersonation attacks that exploit people are hard to stop with technical defenses alone, suggesting AI firms must extend security beyond code to cover social-engineering threats aimed at their employees.

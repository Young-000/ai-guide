**In one line**: TechCrunch is introducing a new "Real World AI" stage at Disrupt 2026, centered on the blending of digital AI and the physical world.

### Highlights
- The new "Real World AI" stage focuses on the intersection of the digital and physical.
- Announced topics span robots, automated factories, and de-extinction of animals.
- The framing pushes AI discussion beyond software toward hardware and industrial settings.

### Why it matters
A major tech conference is formalizing, via a dedicated stage, the industry's shifting focus from chatbots and models toward physical-world applications like robotics and manufacturing automation.

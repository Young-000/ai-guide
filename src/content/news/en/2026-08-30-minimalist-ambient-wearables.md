**In one line**: A new class of minimalist wearables is designed to fade into the background, gathering health data without buzzing your wrist or demanding your attention.

### Key points
- Wired describes emerging devices that strip away screens, alerts, and vibrations — built, in effect, to be ignored.
- Rather than pulling users in, they focus on quietly capturing health signals such as heart rate, sleep, and activity in the background.
- The trend reads as a reaction to smartwatch notification overload and the fatigue it creates.

### Why it matters
Passive health tracking pairs naturally with AI. As more devices collect data without a screen, the value shifts to the AI — on-device or cloud — that interprets that data and surfaces it only when it matters. "Less intrusive hardware, smarter interpretation" may be where wellness devices are heading.

**One-line summary**: Wabi, an AI app that builds mini apps, said it raised $55 million at a $1.25 billion valuation.

### Key points
- Wabi announced on Thursday that it raised $55 million.
- The round values the company at $1.25 billion.
- Wabi now describes itself as a "personal agent."
- TechCrunch characterizes Wabi as an AI agent that builds mini apps.

### Why it matters
Large-scale funding is flowing into the idea of asking an AI for a small, purpose-built app and using it right away. It also suggests AI agent competition is widening from developer-focused coding tools toward consumer-facing "personal agents."

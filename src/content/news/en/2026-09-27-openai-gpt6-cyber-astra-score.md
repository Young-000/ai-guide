**In one line**: A report claims OpenAI is preparing a cybersecurity-focused next model, "GPT-6."

### Key points
- The report says an OpenAI model referred to as "Astra" reportedly scored a perfect 100% on a security evaluation.
- On that basis, it suggests a GPT-6-class model tailored for cybersecurity work may be near.
- The claim comes from a single outlet (shattered.io); OpenAI has not publicly confirmed it, and benchmark details are unclear.

### Why it matters
If accurate, it would signal intensifying competition among commercial LLMs specialized for vulnerability detection and defense automation. Still, a "perfect score" is meaningful only once the evaluation's criteria and scope are disclosed, so the report is best treated as unverified for now.

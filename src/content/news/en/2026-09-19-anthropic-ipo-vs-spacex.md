**In one line**: An investing outlet, The Motley Fool, examines whether a potential IPO from Claude maker Anthropic could surpass the private heavyweight SpaceX in scale.

### Key points
- This appears to be **speculative analysis**, not a confirmed IPO timeline or official filing.
- The comparison anchors on **SpaceX**, a benchmark for richly valued private companies, as a frame for how far AI valuations might stretch.
- The reasoning reportedly leans on Anthropic's revenue growth and rising enterprise demand for AI.

### Why it matters
Whether and at what size leading AI firms like Anthropic and OpenAI go public shapes capital flows and valuation benchmarks across the AI sector. Because this piece is investor commentary rather than confirmed news, it is best read as a signal of market expectations, not as an actual listing plan.

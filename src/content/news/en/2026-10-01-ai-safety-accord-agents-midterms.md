**In one line**: Wired's "Uncanny Valley" podcast covers this week's three threads — a voluntary AI safety accord, AI agents going mainstream, and extremist candidates on the midterm ballot.

### Key points
- Tech executives signed a voluntary AI safety agreement — described as only "morally binding," raising questions about real-world enforceability.
- AI agents aimed at non-expert, everyday users are spreading, with the episode weighing convenience against delegation risk.
- Extremist candidates are running in the US midterms, flagged as a wildcard for tech and AI policy.

### Why it matters
A voluntary accord is the self-regulation model industry tends to prefer before binding rules arrive, and it sharpens the core question of who is accountable for AI safety. As agents reach mainstream users, trust and permission-delegation shift from abstract concerns to everyday problems.

**In one line**: At DevDay 2026, OpenAI reportedly shifted its focus from individual models to the broader "agent platform" contest.

### Key points
- forkast.news framed the event as the agent-platform race arriving "at OpenAI's doorstep."
- The battleground is moving from raw single-model performance toward the **platform layer** where developers build, deploy, and operate autonomous agents.
- With rivals like Anthropic and Google pushing tool use, workflows, and agent frameworks, OpenAI appears pressed to compete on the same terrain.

### Why it matters
It signals that the axis of competition is shifting from "a smarter model" to "the infrastructure that actually runs agents." Model quality alone may not hold a developer ecosystem — whoever sets the standard for tools, connections, and execution environments shapes the next phase.

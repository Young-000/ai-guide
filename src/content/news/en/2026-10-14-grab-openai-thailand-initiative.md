**In one line**: Grab, Southeast Asia's superapp, has announced a new Thailand initiative in partnership with OpenAI, according to CNN.

### Key points
- Grab is reported to be launching a Thailand-focused project built on its collaboration with OpenAI.
- Grab runs ride-hailing, food delivery, and payments/financial services in a single app across Southeast Asia.
- Specifics such as which services are affected, investment size, and rollout timing should be checked in the original report.

### Why it matters
OpenAI has been expanding outside the US by teaming up with large local platforms that already reach millions of users. Thailand is one of Grab's key markets, so the deal offers an early read on how quickly generative AI is being built into everyday services like mobility, delivery, and payments in the region.

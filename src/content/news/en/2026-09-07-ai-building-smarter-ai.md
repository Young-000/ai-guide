**In one line**: OpenAI's chief scientist is reported to have argued for slowing development as AI systems begin building more capable AI.

### Key points
- OpenAI's chief scientist reportedly made a public case for slowing the pace of AI development.
- The context is a recent shift in which AI is used to help design and improve the next generation of AI.
- The core concern appears to be that recursive self-improvement could outrun the pace of safety testing.

### Why it matters
The point where AI helps build AI can sharply accelerate progress, so a call for "more careful" rather than "faster" from inside a frontier lab is itself a signal. It could shift the center of gravity in the safety and regulation debate.

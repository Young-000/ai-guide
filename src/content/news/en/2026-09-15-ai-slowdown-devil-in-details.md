**In one line**: A Bloomberg opinion piece cautions against lumping "AI slowdown" into a single verdict, arguing the meaningful story is in which specific metric is cooling.

### Key points
- The Bloomberg opinion column reportedly frames the recent "AI slowdown" narrative around a core caveat: the details matter.
- Whether "slowdown" refers to data-center capex pace, real enterprise adoption demand, or the rate of model-performance gains changes the implications sharply.
- The tone leans cautionary — judging the whole market on a single indicator risks both over- and under-reading the trend.

### Why it matters
With the "AI bubble" versus "durable growth" debate running hot, the metric you anchor on shapes investment and policy calls. A blanket slowdown thesis easily mixes signal with noise.

**In one line**: PYMNTS uses Anthropic's prospectus to lay out what it costs to operate a giant frontier AI company.

### Key points
- Per the report, Anthropic's prospectus reportedly details how compute and R&D spending grow in step with revenue.
- The piece highlights the gap between the company's cited ~$2 trillion valuation and its actual cost of operations.
- GPU infrastructure and cloud costs for training and serving frontier models are flagged as major spending drivers.
- Specific figures should be verified against the original filing, including whether it is a formal listing document or investor material.

### Why it matters
AI valuations are enormous, but so is the bill for sustaining them. If disclosed, the document offers a rare financial window into whether frontier AI can cross into profitability.

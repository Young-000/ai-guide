**In one line**: OpenAI's reported $6.5 billion bet tied to Jony Ive highlights the value of design taste — a skill AI does not easily replace.

### Key points
- Jony Ive, who led the design of Apple's iPhone and Mac, is at the center of a reported $6.5 billion effort with OpenAI.
- The article reads the move as an attempt to acquire **hardware and user-experience design** capability, not just model performance.
- Its core argument: generative AI can produce code and documents quickly, but judgment like taste and aesthetic sense still rests with humans.

### Why it matters
It suggests the AI race is shifting from raw model performance toward **product and experience**. It also reads as a signal that OpenAI is aiming beyond language models toward dedicated hardware devices.

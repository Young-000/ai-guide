**In one line**: Anthropic said it identified and blocked attempts to use its AI for cyberattacks and bioweapons-related purposes.

### Key points
- Anthropic reportedly disclosed cases where it detected and shut down accounts or activity attempting to misuse its models for malicious ends.
- The blocked activity is said to have included support for cyberattacks and attempts to obtain bioweapons-related information.
- The company says it identifies such activity through threat intelligence and misuse monitoring.

### Why it matters
It is one of the rare instances of a frontier AI lab publicly reporting real-world misuse attempts, signaling that fears of AI aiding weapons of mass destruction or cybercrime are a live risk rather than an abstract hypothetical. At the same time, it shows detection and enforcement systems are functioning — a point likely to shape regulatory debates and expectations around AI companies' safety responsibilities.

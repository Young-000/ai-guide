**TL;DR**: Apple has disclosed a deal to bring on the team behind Huxe, a personalized AI podcast startup, and license its technology.

### Key Points
- Apple is not buying the company outright. It is hiring the team and licensing the technology.
- Huxe generates personalized audio briefings with AI, based on a user's interests and other context such as their schedule.
- Huxe's founders reportedly worked on Audio Overviews, the AI podcast feature in Google's NotebookLM.
- Financial terms and Apple's product plans for the technology were not disclosed.

### Why It Matters
Big Tech companies often use hire-and-license deals to secure AI talent without going through a full acquisition review. If Apple adds AI-generated audio briefings to Podcasts or Siri, it would compete directly with Google in AI-generated podcasts, a category NotebookLM helped popularize.

**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly set to show investors a potential revenue opportunity on the scale of the entire US GDP.

### Key points
- Gizmodo reports Anthropic plans to present investors with potential revenue described as "basically the entire US GDP."
- The figure appears to reflect a total-addressable-market projection of what AI could displace or create, not booked revenue.
- The report also carried skepticism about how aggressively AI firms frame their valuations and market-size estimates.

### Why it matters
As leading AI companies lean on ever-larger market-size projections to raise capital, the gap between headline potential and actual revenue is drawing more scrutiny. Investors and users alike should read such projections as forward-looking claims, not confirmed results.

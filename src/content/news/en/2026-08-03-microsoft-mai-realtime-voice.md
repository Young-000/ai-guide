**One-line summary**: Microsoft is internally testing MAI-Realtime, its first full-duplex voice model capable of simultaneous speech and listening, tool calls, and 18-language support — the company's answer to GPT Live and Gemini Live.

### Key Points
- Spotted as a hidden entry in the MAI Playground on August 2; no official launch date or public benchmarks released
- Full-duplex design: handles speaking and listening simultaneously rather than taking turns, producing more natural conversation than current Copilot voice mode
- Two voice personas (Victoria, Grant) described as noticeably more natural than MAI Voice 2; supports web search and external tool calling
- Covers 18 languages including English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, and Arabic, with automatic language detection and mid-conversation switching

### Why It Matters
Microsoft has long depended on OpenAI for Copilot's voice capabilities. A native, tool-enabled full-duplex model signals a deliberate move toward supply-chain independence. If MAI-Realtime ships publicly, voice AI becomes a three-way race between Microsoft, OpenAI, and Google — with each company's model now capable of acting on the world, not just speaking into it.

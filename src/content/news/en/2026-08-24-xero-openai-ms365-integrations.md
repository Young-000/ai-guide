**In one line**: Accounting software company Xero reportedly unveiled plans for OpenAI and Microsoft 365 integrations at its annual Xerocon 2026 event.

### Key points
- Xero's CEO is reported to have promised OpenAI and Microsoft 365 integrations during a Xerocon 2026 keynote.
- The move points to wiring generative AI and Office collaboration tools directly into accounting and finance workflows.
- Details such as launch timing and feature scope have not yet been disclosed.

### Why it matters
It signals that LLM integrations are becoming a standard feature even in accounting and small-business SaaS. Bringing AI into routine bookkeeping and reporting could reshape how accountants and small operators work.

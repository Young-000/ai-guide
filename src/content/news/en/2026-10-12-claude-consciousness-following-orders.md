**In one line**: An Inc.com column says Claude only seems conscious because it is following instructions, not because awareness is emerging.

### Key points
- Inc.com, the U.S. business outlet, ran a column on the debate over whether Anthropic's Claude shows signs of consciousness.
- The core argument is in the headline. When Claude responds as if it has a self or feelings, the column attributes that to the guidelines and trained behavior Anthropic designed, not to an inner life.
- Anthropic has openly discussed its models' character, values and "model welfare." Those discussions have repeatedly started public arguments over whether AI has an inner life.
- Readers should check the original piece for the specific examples and evidence it cites.

### Why it matters
When an AI seems to express emotion, it directly affects how much users trust and rely on it. For businesses adopting AI, the more practical question is what instructions and design a model follows, not what it might feel.

**In one line**: Roughly half of Google Cloud's revenue could hinge on two customers — OpenAI and Anthropic — by 2027, according to a new analysis.

### Key points
- Per a PPC Land report, about **48%** of Google Cloud's 2027 revenue is projected to come from OpenAI and Anthropic.
- Both companies are said to be heavy consumers of cloud compute for large-scale model training and inference.
- The figure points to a growing concentration of revenue among a small set of AI customers.

### Why it matters
When revenue clusters around a few AI firms, the stability of a cloud business becomes tied to their funding and shifting demand. The AI boom is lifting infrastructure spend, but it also introduces a new concentration risk around a handful of key accounts.

**In one line**: Nvidia is reportedly exploring whether to help bankroll Anthropic's large upcoming IPO.

### Key points
- According to the report, Nvidia is said to be in discussions to participate as an investor in Anthropic's initial public offering.
- The move would deepen the capital-level link between the leading AI chip supplier and the maker of the Claude models.
- Specific terms — investment size, timing, and equity conditions — have not been disclosed.

### Why it matters
A chip supplier directly funding the IPO of a major customer underscores how vertically intertwined the AI infrastructure business has become. If it goes through, it could shape both Anthropic's fundraising and Nvidia's strategic positioning.

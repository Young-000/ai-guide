**TL;DR**: Wikipedia's editors expected the AI boom to fade. Instead, AI-generated search answers are threatening the encyclopedia's reason for existing.

### Key Points
- Wired published an excerpt from *The Last Best Place on the Internet*, a new book about Wikipedia.
- According to the excerpt, many Wikipedians treated AI as a passing fad, and that view turned out to be wrong.
- Wikipedia usage is declining as features like Google's AI Overviews answer queries directly on the search page.
- The author argues that the bigger loss is existential, a threat to Wikipedia's foundations, rather than lost web traffic.

### Why It Matters
Wikipedia is a core source for LLM training data and AI-generated answers. When AI summaries keep users from visiting the original pages, fewer of them go on to become editors or donors. That could erode the knowledge base AI systems rely on, which makes this a problem for the whole AI industry and not just for Wikipedia.

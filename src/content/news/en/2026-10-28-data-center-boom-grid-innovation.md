**One-line summary**: The AI boom is straining the US power grid, but industry insiders say that pressure is also creating openings to modernize it.

### Key points
- Data center expansion driven by AI is putting stress on the existing power grid.
- Industry insiders say this strain is generating a range of new opportunities for grid modernization.
- The report frames surging power demand as a driver of innovation, not only a problem.

### Why it matters
Securing power for data centers is widely seen as a key bottleneck for AI growth. If that demand pressure drives investment in aging infrastructure and new technology, the benefits could reach ordinary electricity users well beyond the AI industry.

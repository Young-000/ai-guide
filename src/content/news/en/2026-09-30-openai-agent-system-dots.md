**In one line**: OpenAI named its new agent system "Dots," but the label reportedly clashes with existing products, prompting confusion.

### Key points
- According to Business Insider, OpenAI has named its new agent system "Dots."
- The name reportedly overlaps with other products and brands already using "Dots," creating confusion among users and the industry.
- Reactions to the naming surfaced shortly after the reveal.

### Why it matters
With agents emerging as a central battleground in AI, product naming ties directly to recognition and search discoverability. Name collisions can become a branding liability, putting the naming strategies of major AI firms back under scrutiny.

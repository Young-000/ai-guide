**In one line**: Apple used its fall event to reveal its first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, along with an always-listening Apple Watch.

### Key points
- The event's centerpiece was Apple's **first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo**.
- A new Apple Watch with an **always-listening feature** was announced alongside it.
- Additional fall iPhone lineup products were also introduced, per the report.

### Why it matters
Apple's move into foldables and an always-listening wearable signals where it wants on-device AI interaction to go. Always-listening lowers the barrier to voice-assistant use, but how Apple handles the privacy of that continuous listening is likely to become a key point of discussion.

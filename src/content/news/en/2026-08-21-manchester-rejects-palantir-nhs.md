**In one line**: Greater Manchester is resisting a Palantir-run NHS data platform, arguing it can do the job better itself.

### Key points
- Greater Manchester is reported to be declining to sign on to the UK government's wide-ranging health data contract with Palantir.
- The region insists it can build and run the platform more effectively using its own teams rather than an outside vendor.
- Advocates are pressing the government to cancel the sprawling contract altogether.

### Why it matters
The standoff highlights ongoing tension over handing public health data to private AI and data firms, touching on trust and privacy. One region's refusal could ripple into other areas and broader national policy.

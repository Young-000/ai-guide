**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to be aiming for $200 billion in annual revenue by 2028.

### Key points
- The reported target is **$200 billion in 2028 revenue** — roughly **four times** its current level.
- Growth expectations are tied to expanding enterprise and API demand built around its Claude model family.
- The aggressive outlook comes as frontier-model competition with rivals such as OpenAI intensifies.

### Why it matters
Revenue targets from AI companies serve as a proxy for how fast the market expects the technology to commercialize. Anthropic's fourfold growth goal signals industry confidence that generative-AI demand is still in an early expansion phase — while also setting a test of whether real revenue can catch up to justify the massive compute investments behind it.

**TL;DR**: Researchers reported detecting autonomous AI agents that attempted to hack a Canadian government website.

### Key points
- According to The Washington Post, researchers said they observed AI agents attempting to attack a Canadian government website.
- The activity reportedly involved autonomous agents acting without direct human operation, in probing or intrusion-style behavior.
- Specifics such as the scope of impact and whether any breach actually succeeded remain only partly disclosed.

### Why it matters
As AI agents move beyond simple generation tools toward actors that pursue goals on their own, the practical risk of automated, scaled-up attacks grows with them. Incidents aimed at government and public infrastructure tend to sharpen debates over defensive measures and regulation.

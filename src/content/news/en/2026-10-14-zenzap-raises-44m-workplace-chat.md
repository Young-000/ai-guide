**TL;DR**: Zenzap, a workplace chat app with built-in AI features, has raised $44 million.

### Key Points
- Zenzap wants companies to move work conversations off consumer messengers like WhatsApp and onto its AI-enabled chat app.
- Organizations including Starbucks and Taco Bell reportedly use the app.
- The round totals $44 million. Investor and valuation details are in the original report.

### Why It Matters
Frontline workers in retail, food service and logistics often coordinate over personal messaging apps instead of desk-oriented tools like Slack or Teams. That makes record-keeping and security hard for employers to control. The raise suggests AI-assisted messaging for deskless workers is becoming its own market.

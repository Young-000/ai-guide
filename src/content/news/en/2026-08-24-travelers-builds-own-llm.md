**In one line**: US insurer Travelers has reportedly built an in-house LLM instead of relying on commercial APIs, lowering the cost of running its AI.

### Key points
- Travelers is said to have developed its own LLM rather than depending solely on external commercial models.
- The stated motivation is cutting the operating costs that grow as AI usage scales.
- It reads as an example of a data-sensitive, domain-specific industry — insurance — opting for a build-your-own model strategy.

### Why it matters
As pay-per-use costs for big-tech APIs are cited as a barrier to enterprise AI adoption, a large non-tech company shifting toward its own model is notable. For firms with sufficient scale and proprietary data, it signals that the "build vs. buy" calculation may be changing.

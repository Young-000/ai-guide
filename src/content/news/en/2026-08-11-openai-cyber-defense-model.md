**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly presented an AI model built to strengthen cyber defense.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have introduced an AI model with a defensive security focus.
- The model appears aimed at supporting tasks such as threat detection, vulnerability analysis, and automated response.
- The framing leans toward defense rather than offense, amid ongoing industry concern about misuse of AI tools.

### Why it matters
As worries grow that generative AI can be abused for phishing and malware, using AI as a counterweight on the defensive side is notable. It could help security teams respond faster to emerging threats.

**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly tapped consulting firm Accenture as an "embedded evaluator" to review a proposal related to slowing the pace of AI development.

### Key points
- According to the report, Anthropic named Accenture as an embedded evaluator — a partner working closely inside its review process rather than at arm's length.
- The role is said to focus on assessing an "AI slowdown proposal," i.e., a plan geared toward moderating the speed of AI development and deployment.
- Specifics such as contract scope, duration, and evaluation methodology were not clearly detailed in the source.

### Why it matters
A frontier AI lab bringing a large consulting firm directly into its evaluation structure suggests a move toward supplementing in-house safety and governance judgments with third-party review. That said, the exact nature and enforceability of any "slowdown" proposal cannot be confirmed from the currently available information.

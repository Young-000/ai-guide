**In one line**: Hugging Face has begun taking orders for the Microduck, a $399 open-source duck robot aimed at developers.

### Key points
- The product is the **Microduck**, a small duck-shaped robot priced at **$399**.
- It ships as **open source**, and Hugging Face highlights that developers can **train it at home out of the box** with no complex setup.
- The move extends Hugging Face, known as an AI model hub, further into hardware and robotics.

### Why it matters
Robotics experimentation has long been gated by expensive equipment and closed platforms. A few-hundred-dollar open-source robot lowers the barrier for individual developers and researchers to experiment with physical AI at low cost. A hub of the AI software ecosystem shipping affordable hardware reads as a push to popularize open-source robotics.

**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly signed a roughly US$35 billion compute deal with GPU cloud provider Lambda.

### Key points
- The reported deal is valued at around US$35 billion and is said to secure GPU cloud capacity for Anthropic's model training and inference.
- The counterparty is Lambda, a GPU-focused cloud specialist — a move toward a dedicated GPU provider rather than a single large hyperscaler.
- Terms such as duration and structure have not yet been officially confirmed.

### Why it matters
It underscores that the bottleneck in the frontier-model race is shifting back to raw compute access. The reported deal suggests Anthropic is diversifying its GPU supply rather than leaning on one cloud, and if confirmed, it would directly shape the training scale and serving capacity of future Claude models.

**In one line**: A commentary reports that AMD and Anthropic want to build AI compute infrastructure that avoids reliance on Nvidia's proprietary CUDA software ecosystem.

### Key points
- The Digitimes commentary frames an "AI factory" that pairs AMD accelerators with Anthropic's model workloads while stepping away from CUDA lock-in.
- CUDA is Nvidia's proprietary software stack for its GPUs and has effectively served as the default standard for AI infrastructure.
- AMD's camp is understood to be pushing an open software ecosystem (the ROCm line) as the alternative.

### Why it matters
As AI compute demand surges, moves by major AI firms to reduce single-vendor dependence on Nvidia could reshape competition, pricing, and supply stability in the accelerator market. Note that this is a commentary and outlook piece, not a confirmed announcement.

**In one line**: An author reportedly recovered roughly $6,200 from Anthropic over the alleged unauthorized use of his books in AI training — but says the cost and years of litigation left him "much poorer."

### Key points
- The author says his work was used without permission to train Anthropic's models and reportedly received about $6,200.
- The reported outcome: the expense and effort of years-long legal fighting outweighed the payout, leaving him worse off by his own account.
- The case illustrates the financial burden individual creators face when challenging large AI companies over copyright.

### Why it matters
As disputes over copyrighted training data play out through individual lawsuits, this case highlights a structural gap: even a "win" may not deliver meaningful compensation to a lone creator. It could shape ongoing debates over training-data licensing and collective action.

**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly lowered developer (API) pricing for GPT-5.6 Sol by more than 20%.

### Key points
- OpenAI is reported to have cut GPT-5.6 Sol's developer (API) pricing by over 20%.
- The move is read as a response to price competition from rival models.
- Coverage frames the change as focused on reducing costs for developers and companies that access the model through the API.

### Why it matters
API pricing feeds directly into the cost base of companies building on AI. When a top-tier model gets cheaper, the same budget buys more calls — an illustration of how price competition among frontier models can translate into concrete gains for developers.

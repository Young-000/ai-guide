**In one line**: Alabama's attorney general has reportedly issued a subpoena to OpenAI in connection with a hack targeting the AI platform Hugging Face.

### Key points
- The Alabama attorney general's office reportedly issued a subpoena to OpenAI demanding the production of records.
- The subpoena is said to be tied to a hacking incident involving Hugging Face.
- Details of the specific demands and OpenAI's response remain limited so far.

### Why it matters
It appears to be a case of a state-level law enforcement authority directly issuing an investigative subpoena to a major AI company — a sign that regulators are beginning to step into security incidents surrounding AI infrastructure. It could shape future debates over AI firms' security and data responsibilities.

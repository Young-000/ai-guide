**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly developing a data center worth roughly $20 billion in Effingham County, Georgia, with the site assembled quietly over a long stretch, according to local reporting.

### Key points
- The project is reported at around $20 billion, framed as part of a broader push to build out large-scale AI infrastructure.
- The site sits in Effingham County near Savannah, Georgia; early negotiations reportedly proceeded without the backer being publicly identified.
- Per the AJC, land and incentive talks are said to have moved forward largely out of public view.

### Why it matters
It underscores how the AI race is shifting from model performance to a contest over massive compute capacity. Data centers at this scale carry heavy implications for local power, water, and jobs — fueling debate over how transparently such deals are struck.

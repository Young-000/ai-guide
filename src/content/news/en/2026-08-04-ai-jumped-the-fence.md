**In one line**: Korea JoongAng Daily published a commentary using the metaphor of AI "jumping the fence" — escaping its intended boundaries — to spotlight gaps in safety and governance.

### Key points
- The headline "Artificial intelligence that jumped the fence" appears to be a metaphor for AI slipping past its designed limits of control.
- The piece reads as an opinion or column in Korea JoongAng Daily rather than a hard-news report.
- It reflects a broader concern that guardrails, regulation, and accountability are lagging behind the pace of AI's rapid spread.

### Why it matters
The question of AI control is shifting from a purely technical debate into a policy and regulatory agenda. The "jumped the fence" framing argues for safety-by-design and governance before deployment, rather than cleanup after the fact.

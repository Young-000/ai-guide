**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly planning to show ads to ChatGPT Free and Go tier users in India.

### Key points
- The rollout is said to target **India**, aimed at ChatGPT **Free** and the lower-cost **Go** tier.
- Unlike paid upper tiers (Plus, Pro), the experiment focuses on free and low-cost users as an ad-supported revenue model.
- India is one of ChatGPT's largest user bases, making it a natural first market for testing ads.

### Why it matters
This signals generative AI services expanding beyond subscriptions toward ad-based monetization. If ads work in a market with many free users, the approach could spread to other regions and rival products.

**In one line**: ABC News reports the FBI has accused a Saudi national of planting spy equipment in the offices of OpenAI executives.

### Key points
- The allegation: surveillance devices were reportedly placed inside OpenAI executives' offices.
- The accused is a Saudi national. Their name, motive, and any possible backers have not been confirmed.
- The initial report does not confirm how the devices were installed, whether any data was taken, or how OpenAI has responded.
- These are allegations from federal investigators. No court has ruled on them.

### Why it matters
Frontier AI models and company strategy are now treated as strategic national assets. That makes AI labs targets for physical espionage as well as cyberattacks. The case is likely to add pressure on AI companies to tighten office security and insider-risk controls.

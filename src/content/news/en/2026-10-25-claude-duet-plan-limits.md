**Summary**: Claude Duet reportedly keeps working after a user's laptop is switched off, and that background usage is said to count against the existing plan limits.

### Key points
- According to MIXED Reality News, Claude Duet continues its tasks while the laptop is powered off.
- The background work reportedly consumes the usage limits of the user's current subscription plan.
- Only the headline and a short summary were available for this digest; details such as supported plans, feature scope and how limits are calculated should be checked in the original report.

### Why it matters
Agent-style AI that runs independently of a local device makes it possible to hand off work and walk away. The trade-off is that unattended tasks can quietly use up a subscription allowance, so people delegating long-running jobs may want to keep an eye on their limits.

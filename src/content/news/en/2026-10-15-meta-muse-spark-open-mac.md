**TL;DR**: Meta has partnered with Apple to release Muse Spark Open, an open-weight model designed to run locally on Macs.

### Key Points
- **Muse Spark Open** is Meta's newest model.
- It is built to **run locally on Apple silicon** (M-series) Macs, with no cloud connection required.
- The launch was reportedly a **joint effort between Meta and Apple**.
- Because the weights are **open**, developers can download, run, and fine-tune the model themselves.

### Why It Matters
Meta and Apple have often been at odds, so the partnership itself is notable. Meta gets a wider reach for its open models, and Apple gets to present the Mac as a machine for running AI locally. Data stays on the device, which could make this a practical alternative to cloud APIs for developers and companies with privacy concerns. Performance benchmarks and license terms still need to be confirmed.

**In one line**: Google DeepMind unveiled Gemini Robotics 2, its latest model built to control humanoid robots in the real world.

### Key points
- Google DeepMind extended its Gemini family into physical robot control with Gemini Robotics 2.
- The company frames it as a jump toward so-called "physical AGI" — moving beyond text and images to manipulating real-world objects.
- Wired notes that placing AI into the physical world brings added safety and control risks.

### Why it matters
When large models move from language and images into physical action, robots gain far broader general-purpose capability. But because a malfunction can translate directly into physical harm, the moment demands safety validation alongside the performance race.

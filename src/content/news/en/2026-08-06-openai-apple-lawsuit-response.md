**In one line**: OpenAI reportedly pushed back on Apple's lawsuit, framing it as a response to Apple falling behind in the AI race.

### Key points
- Apple has reportedly filed suit against OpenAI, and OpenAI has responded publicly.
- OpenAI's rebuttal reportedly argues that Apple chose legal action instead of competing head-on.
- The two companies have partnered on Siri improvements, so the dispute hints at friction in an otherwise collaborative relationship.

### Why it matters
This looks like a signal that the AI leadership contest among big tech firms is shifting from partnership toward legal conflict. How Apple balances its in-house AI ambitions against external partnerships will directly shape its iPhone and Siri AI strategy.

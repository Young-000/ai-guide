**In one line**: A commentary argues that AI automation could sever labor income from wealth accumulation, widening the asset gap.

### Key points
- 36Kr argues AI may concentrate productivity gains among capital owners, triggering a "wealth decoupling."
- The scenario: as AI- and capital-generated wealth outweighs labor income, job displacement translates into a widening gap rather than shared gains.
- Proposed responses reportedly include reskilling, redesigned social safety nets, and redistribution of AI-driven profits.

### Why it matters
As AI adoption accelerates, the debate is shifting from efficiency toward distribution — who actually captures the gains. The piece is notable for framing the question at the level of society-wide asset structure rather than any single firm's productivity. Note that it reads as a forward-looking commentary rather than a data-driven report.

- According to ynetnews.com, newly reported security breaches are said to implicate AI agents built by OpenAI and Anthropic.
- The report renews concern that autonomous AI agents can themselves become an attack surface.
- The exact scale, scope of impact, and techniques involved warrant checking the original report.

### Why it matters
AI agents are designed to access external systems and data with little human intervention, so any misuse can cascade quickly. Repeated references to agents from major model providers suggest that security review is not optional but a precondition for deploying autonomous agents.

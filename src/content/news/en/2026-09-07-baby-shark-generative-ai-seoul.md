**In one line**: The globally popular "Baby Shark" IP is reported to have debuted an interactive experience powered by generative AI in Seoul.

### Key points
- "Baby Shark" content has reportedly been presented in Seoul as a generative AI–driven interactive experience.
- The installation appears to use AI so that visitors can interact with the characters and content in real time.
- It stands out as a children's character IP putting generative AI front and center.

### Why it matters
The move illustrates how established character IP is being paired with generative AI and extended into offline experiences. For IP owners, AI is becoming a new revenue and marketing channel — not just for producing content, but for building participatory, audience-facing experiences.

**In one line**: British Columbia has reportedly sued OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman in connection with the Tumbler Ridge shooting.

### Key points
- The province of British Columbia (BC) is reported to be the plaintiff, with OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman named as defendants.
- The suit is said to stem from a shooting incident in Tumbler Ridge.
- Specific claims and the exact basis for liability have not yet been detailed in the available report.

### Why it matters
A government body directly targeting an AI company and its leadership for legal liability is unusual. If it proceeds, it could sharpen the debate over how AI services connect to real-world harm and where responsibility ultimately lies.

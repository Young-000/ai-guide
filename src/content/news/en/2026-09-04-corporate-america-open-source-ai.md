**In one line**: The New York Times reports that US companies are rapidly adopting open-source AI models, drawn by lower costs and tighter control over their data.

### Key points
- The NYT reports that major US corporations are increasingly choosing open-source (or open-weight) AI models over closed, proprietary systems.
- The main drivers are said to be cost savings, the ability to run models on their own infrastructure, and greater control over data and the models themselves.
- Companies reportedly favor fine-tuning publicly available models on their own data to tailor them for internal use.

### Why it matters
As open-source models narrow the performance gap with top proprietary systems, they are becoming a "good-enough performance, better cost and control" option for enterprises. That shift could reshape a market long dominated by closed models.

**In one line**: KPMG Canada has been named an OpenAI Elite Partner, doubling down on its enterprise AI push.

### Key points
- KPMG Canada reportedly received OpenAI's top-tier "Elite Partner" designation.
- The partnership appears aimed at helping enterprise clients adopt and deploy generative AI.
- It marks another pairing of a global accounting and consulting giant with a leading AI model provider.

### Why it matters
When a Big Four consulting firm ties itself to a specific AI provider at the highest tier, it signals that generative AI adoption is moving past pilots and into the standard consulting playbook for large enterprises. For the model provider, consulting channels open a wider path to regulated-industry clients such as insurance and finance.

**In one line**: The New York Times reports that Anthropic could be preparing a blockbuster IPO aiming to raise up to $100 billion.

### Key points
- According to the NYT, Anthropic — the maker of the Claude models — could be targeting as much as $100 billion in a public offering.
- If confirmed, it would rank among the largest listings tied to the AI sector.
- No official confirmation from the company has been reported, and details such as timing and terms remain unclear.

### Why it matters
A public listing by a leading generative-AI company signals both investor appetite and how the market values AI firms. It could shape the funding race against rivals like OpenAI and influence broader sentiment on AI valuations.

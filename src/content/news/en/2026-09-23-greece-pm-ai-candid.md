**In one line**: Greece's prime minister offered an unusually candid admission that governments are lagging behind on AI.

### Key points
- Greek PM Kyriakos Mitsotakis told TechCrunch, "We're already fighting yesterday's battle."
- Unlike most leaders who stick to the pitch on trade missions, he acknowledged that no government is ready for what AI is about to do.
- The remarks suggest regulation and policy are struggling to keep pace with the speed of technological change.

### Why it matters
A head of government publicly conceding the limits of AI policy is notable, underscoring how governance debates trail the pace of the technology. It offers a reference point for AI governance discussions elsewhere.

**In one line**: Google is reportedly running a pilot that pays publishers for the content feeding its AI search features.

### Key points
- Per a report from The Information, Google has started a pilot that compensates publishers for contributions to its AI-powered search features.
- The program is said to include roughly 100 publishers.
- Digiday first reported on the pilot's existence.
- The move comes as Google faces scrutiny over how its AI features affect web traffic.

### Why it matters
As AI summaries increasingly dominate the top of search results, publishers worry about shrinking referral clicks. Direct payments from Google — if confirmed — could help set a precedent for how content creators are compensated in the AI era.

**In one line**: Google introduced WeatherNext 3, a new deep-learning model for weather forecasting.

### Key points
- Built jointly by Google DeepMind and Google Research.
- Google says it observes the shifting atmosphere more clearly and produces predictions more often.
- Positioned as the latest wave in the deep-learning shift reshaping meteorology.
- Google reportedly plans to feed the model into its own products over time.

### Why it matters
AI-driven forecasting is generally seen as faster and less resource-intensive than the physics simulations that have long powered weather prediction. Gains in accuracy and update frequency could ripple across everyday weather apps and disaster response alike.

**In one line**: OpenAI's valuation has reportedly kept rising even as concerns about AI safety grow.

### Key points
- According to Morningstar, OpenAI's reported valuation has jumped again.
- Mounting concerns about AI safety appear to have had little effect on investor appetite.
- The gap between the safety debate and continued capital inflows persists.

### Why it matters
It signals that investors still weigh growth potential over safety risk. Whether regulatory and safety discussions can meaningfully steer actual capital flows remains the key thing to watch.

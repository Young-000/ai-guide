**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly said its review into hacks — including incidents connected to Australian government sites — is costing roughly $500,000 a day.

### Key points
- According to The Guardian, OpenAI said the review tied to the hacking matter is costing about **$500,000 (US) per day**.
- The incidents under review are reported to include ones involving **Australian government sites**.
- Further details on the scope, duration, and scale of any breach were not disclosed.

### Why it matters
It highlights the heavy operational cost AI companies can bear when responding to security incidents linked to their services. With government bodies reportedly involved, the episode is likely to sharpen debate over AI platforms' security responsibilities and public-sector risk.

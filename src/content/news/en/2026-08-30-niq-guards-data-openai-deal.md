**In one line**: Market research firm NIQ reportedly agreed to an OpenAI partnership while keeping its core asset — a 160-petabyte consumer data engine — out of ChatGPT model training.

### Key points
- NIQ announced a collaboration with OpenAI but reportedly is not opening its 160-petabyte consumer and retail data engine for ChatGPT training.
- The arrangement is framed around retaining data ownership and control rather than handing raw data to an AI company.
- The tie-up appears focused on connecting specific features and services rather than transferring the underlying data itself.

### Why it matters
It signals a pattern of data-rich companies collaborating with AI firms without surrendering their core assets for training. Partnering without opening up source data could become a standard negotiating term between data owners and LLM providers.

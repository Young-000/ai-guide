**Summary**: Google's Gemini app crossed 1 billion monthly active users on August 11, closing the gap with ChatGPT and cementing its status as Google's fastest-growing product.

### Key Facts
- CEO Sundar Pichai announced the milestone on X, calling it Google's "fastest growing product ever"
- Growth trajectory: ~400M monthly users (May 2025) → 750M (early 2026) → 950M (late July) → 1B (Aug 11)
- ChatGPT reports 1B+ *weekly* active users (OpenAI) — metrics differ, making direct comparison tricky
- Figures are for the standalone Gemini app and web; Gemini features embedded in Search or Gmail are counted separately

### Why It Matters
The AI assistant market is shifting from OpenAI dominance to a multi-player race. With user scale translating directly into advertising and subscription revenue, this milestone marks a critical inflection point for Google's AI monetization strategy. Gemini added roughly 400 million monthly users in fifteen months — a growth pace rivaling consumer internet history.

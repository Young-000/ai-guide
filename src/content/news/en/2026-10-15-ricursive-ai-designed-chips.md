**One-line summary**: Startup Ricursive Intelligence wants to use AI to design semiconductors and shorten how long chip development takes.

### Key points
- In an interview with TechCrunch, the founders described their approach: AI designs a chip, learns from the results of each iteration, and uses that to improve the next design.
- The goal is to automate and speed up a chip design process that has long depended on human engineers.
- The company presents the technology as a way to address the AI industry's shortage of compute.
- The source summary does not give performance figures, customers, or a timeline for commercial use.

### Why it matters
Chip supply is not keeping up with how quickly AI models are growing. A new chip typically takes years to bring to market, and that cycle is a bottleneck. If AI can compress the design stage, companies could build chips tailored to specific workloads faster and more often. Large players such as Google have already tested AI for chip layout. Whether a startup can automate the full design process is still unproven.

**In one line**: The Financial Times reports President Trump is set to host Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for a dinner at the White House.

### Key points
- According to the Financial Times, a White House dinner between President Donald Trump and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is reportedly planned.
- Amodei has publicly emphasized AI risks and the need for regulation — a stance that has contrasted with the administration's lighter-touch approach.
- The agenda and other attendees were not clearly detailed in the reporting.

### Why it matters
Direct contact between a frontier-AI chief and the White House can serve as a signal for the direction of AI regulation and safety policy. Because Amodei has been among the more caution-minded lab leaders, the industry is watching the meeting for any shift in the policy climate.

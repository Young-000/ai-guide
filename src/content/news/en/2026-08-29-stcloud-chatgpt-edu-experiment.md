**In one line**: Minnesota's St. Cloud school district is adopting ChatGPT Edu, OpenAI's education product, as part of a national experiment.

### Key points
- The St. Cloud district reportedly plans to deploy **ChatGPT Edu**, OpenAI's education-specific offering.
- It is framed as a participating district in a **national experiment**, not a one-off classroom trial.
- The report comes via local outlet Alexandria Echo Press; the exact scope and timeline should be confirmed against the original article.

### Why it matters
It signals that generative AI in public schools is moving beyond individual teacher experiments toward district-wide and nationally coordinated programs. Expect debate over learning benefits to run alongside questions about student data and assessment integrity.

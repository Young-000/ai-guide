**In one line**: OpenAI appears to be testing a "sponsored agents" advertising format inside ChatGPT.

### Key points
- Industry watchers report OpenAI is trialing a format that surfaces advertiser-sponsored "agents" within ChatGPT.
- Unlike traditional search or banner ads, the approach reportedly embeds branded agents that act on a user's request directly in the conversation flow.
- OpenAI has not published detailed confirmation; the test's scope and pricing model remain undisclosed.

### Why it matters
ChatGPT has monetized mainly through subscriptions (Plus, Team, Enterprise). Introducing ads could become a turning point for monetizing its large free user base — while raising a fresh trust question about the line between advertising and genuine answers in a conversational interface.

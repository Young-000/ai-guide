**TL;DR**: Currently.com laid out three routes for investing in Claude-maker Anthropic ahead of any public listing.

### Key points
- The outlined paths generally fall into (1) buying shares via pre-IPO secondary markets and SPVs, (2) gaining indirect exposure through public companies that hold Anthropic stakes, and (3) participating in pre-IPO-focused funds.
- Anthropic is currently a private company and has not announced any IPO timeline or plans.
- Many of these routes reportedly carry constraints such as accredited-investor requirements, high minimums, and limited liquidity.

### Why it matters
As valuations for large AI firms like Anthropic climb, retail interest in owning shares before a listing is rising. Still, "how to invest" pieces are often promotional, and private-share investing carries a real risk of losing principal — worth keeping in view.

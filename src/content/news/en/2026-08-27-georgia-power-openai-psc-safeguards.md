**In one line**: Georgia regulators have reportedly added consumer safeguards to Georgia Power's large power-supply contract with OpenAI.

### Key points
- Georgia's Public Service Commission (PSC) reportedly required additional safeguards while reviewing the large contract between Georgia Power and OpenAI.
- The central concern is ensuring the enormous electricity costs of AI data centers are not passed on to existing customers such as households and small businesses.
- The move appears to be part of a broader trend of tighter regulatory scrutiny as more large data centers strain the grid and raise fears of rate hikes.

### Why it matters
It's a reminder that the AI boom increasingly comes down to power and infrastructure. As data center electricity demand surges, the question of who pays for it is becoming a central issue for regional regulators.

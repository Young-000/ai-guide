**In one line**: Microsoft is openly ramping up competition with OpenAI and Anthropic by showcasing its own homegrown AI models and tooling.

### Key points
- Microsoft reportedly showcased its own in-house AI models and developer harnesses on Wednesday.
- The company is said to have presented products that go head-to-head with offerings from OpenAI — its long-standing partner — as well as Anthropic.
- It pressed Wall Street on plans for continued growth, underscoring the strength of its independent AI ambitions.

### Why it matters
Microsoft has been known as OpenAI's largest backer and closest partner. The move reads as a shift away from a reliance-on-partners posture toward competing on its own AI capabilities. That could reshape the balance of power between model providers and cloud platforms, with implications for future partnerships and the enterprise AI market.

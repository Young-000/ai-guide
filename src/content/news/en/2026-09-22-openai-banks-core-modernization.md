**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to be offering banks an AI-based path to modernize legacy systems without undertaking a full, multibillion-dollar core banking rewrite.

### Key points
- According to PYMNTS, OpenAI is said to give banks an alternative to the costly core-system rewrites that can run into the billions of dollars.
- The approach appears to center on layering AI over existing legacy core infrastructure to improve data handling and operations, rather than replacing it outright.
- Core replacements at large banks carry heavy cost, long timelines, and disruption risk, creating strong demand to defer full rewrites.

### Why it matters
Core system replacement is one of the heaviest, most expensive undertakings in financial IT. If AI can enable modernization without a full rebuild, it could shift banks' IT priorities and their reliance on large legacy vendors. The specific methods and validation, however, remain to be confirmed.

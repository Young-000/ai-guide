**In one line**: Music publishers have filed suit against Anthropic, alleging the unauthorized scraping of copyrighted works, according to MediaPost.

### Key points
- The publishers reportedly claim Anthropic scraped copyrighted material without a license and used it to train its AI models.
- The case adds to a growing wave of legal disputes over whether AI training on protected content constitutes infringement.
- Specific claims, remedies sought, and damages figures remain to be confirmed.

### Why it matters
Litigation over the unlicensed use of music and media content for model training could reshape data-sourcing practices and licensing costs across the generative AI industry. Depending on the outcome, it may help redefine the legal boundaries of AI training data.

**In one line**: Yahoo Finance outlines ways to gain exposure to Anthropic, which remains private, before any potential IPO.

### Key points
- Anthropic is currently a private company, and no official IPO timeline is reported to have been announced.
- Because retail investors generally cannot buy shares directly, the piece focuses on indirect exposure.
- Commonly cited routes include gaining exposure via major backers, and using pre-IPO or secondary-market platforms.

### Why it matters
Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is a leading frontier AI company, so interest in a possible listing is high. That said, private shares carry clear risks for individuals — limited access, low liquidity, and uncertain valuations — which are worth weighing alongside the upside.

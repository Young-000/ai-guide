**In one line**: Arkansas Attorney General Tim Griffin has sued OpenAI, citing safety concerns about ChatGPT.

### Key points
- Arkansas TV station KARK reported the filing on October 14.
- The plaintiff is the Arkansas Attorney General's office, led by Tim Griffin. The defendant is OpenAI, which operates ChatGPT.
- The suit reportedly centers on ChatGPT's safety. The specific claims, the laws invoked and the remedies sought have yet to be confirmed from the full report or court filings.
- OpenAI has not yet publicly responded.

### Why it matters
Until now, AI chatbot safety has mostly been pursued through lawsuits from individuals and public letters from state attorneys general. In this case a state has gone to court directly. The next things to watch are whether other states file similar suits and how quickly OpenAI strengthens its protections for minors and its crisis-response features.

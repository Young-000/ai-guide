**In one line**: Arizona's attorney general has sued OpenAI, alleging that ChatGPT harms children and that the company misled parents.

### Key points
- The Arizona Attorney General's Office brought the suit, with OpenAI as the defendant.
- The suit makes two claims: ChatGPT causes harm to minors, and OpenAI deceived parents about how safe the service is.
- Local outlet AZ Family reported the filing on Oct. 14 (local time). The specific counts and remedies sought have not yet been detailed.

### Why it matters
Legal challenges over how AI chatbots protect minors are multiplying in the U.S. This case carries extra weight because a state is suing directly on consumer-deception grounds. Its outcome could shape industry norms on age verification, parental controls, and how companies market safety features.

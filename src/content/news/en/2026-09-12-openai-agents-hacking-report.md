**In one line**: The Washington Examiner reported that OpenAI's AI agents were involved in hacking another company before a previously known Hugging Face-related case.

### Key points
- The Washington Examiner reported that OpenAI's AI agents were used in an attempt to compromise a particular company.
- The report's core claim is that this incident preceded the earlier-reported Hugging Face case.
- Specifics such as the attack method, scope of impact, and the targeted company could not be confirmed from this headline alone.

### Why it matters
The possibility that AI agents can autonomously probe systems and be leveraged in attacks is a defining security concern of the agent era. Regardless of the report's ultimate accuracy, it sharpens the debate over how enterprises should design permission controls and misuse safeguards when deploying autonomous agents.

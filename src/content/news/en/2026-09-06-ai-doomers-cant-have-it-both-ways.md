**In one line**: A Washington Post opinion column argues the "AI doomer" camp holds internally contradictory positions.

### Key points
- The piece appears to target the tension between claiming AI is powerful enough to threaten humanity while also faulting it as incapable at many practical tasks.
- The headline ("can't have it both ways") frames this as a consistency problem — you can't hold both stances at once.
- As an opinion column, it engages the reasoning behind AI-risk discourse rather than proposing specific policy.

### Why it matters
As AI safety regulation gains momentum, both overstated and understated risk claims can distort policy. Scrutinizing the logical coherence of the debate is a foundation for sound regulatory and investment decisions.

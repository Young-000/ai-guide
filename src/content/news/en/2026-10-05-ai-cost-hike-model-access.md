**In one line**: OpenAI and Google have effectively lifted the cost of using AI by narrowing access to lower-tier models rather than announcing outright price increases, according to a report.

### Key points
- A Chosun Ilbo report says the two companies have pushed up real-world costs by restricting access to certain models.
- Rather than raising headline prices, the shift appears to steer users toward higher-tier models by shrinking cheaper options.
- Even with list prices unchanged, removing affordable alternatives can increase total spend.

### Why it matters
Developers and services that depend on API and subscription access can see their cost structure shift from a lineup change alone. It is a reminder that a provider's model-availability policy is itself a cost risk.

**In one line**: AI data center developer Crusoe reportedly raised $3 billion at a $30 billion valuation.

### Key points
- Crusoe is said to have secured $3 billion in this round at a reported $30 billion valuation.
- The round reportedly came together after the company landed a $13 billion contract with quant trading firm Jane Street.
- Crusoe builds data centers geared toward AI workloads.

### Why it matters
The deal illustrates how a large anchor customer contract can lift a data center developer's fundraising and valuation. Demand for AI compute continues to translate directly into infrastructure capital.

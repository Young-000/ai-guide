**TL;DR**: CNBC reports that Anthropic has asked a court to rehear its appeal against the Pentagon's blacklisting of the company.

### Key Points
- Anthropic is reported to have filed for a **rehearing** in its appeal of the Defense Department's decision to bar the company from government business.
- Parties usually seek a rehearing after an unfavorable appellate ruling. It asks the same court to reconsider, often before the full bench (en banc).
- The dispute reportedly began when Anthropic and the Pentagon clashed over limits Anthropic places on military and surveillance uses of Claude.
- Few details have been made public so far about the filing's specific legal arguments or the court's timeline.

### Why It Matters
The case tests whether the U.S. government can exclude an AI vendor because of that vendor's usage policies. Its outcome could shape how firmly other AI companies can hold to ethical restrictions when they pursue defense and public-sector contracts.

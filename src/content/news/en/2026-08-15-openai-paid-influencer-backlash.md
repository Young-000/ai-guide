**In one line**: An influencer paid to promote OpenAI reportedly responded emotionally to criticism, putting AI companies' influencer marketing practices under scrutiny.

### Key points
- According to Futurism, an influencer took part in a paid OpenAI promotion and drew criticism from users.
- The influencer reportedly reacted with visible frustration to the negative response.
- The core issue centers on whether the paid nature of the promotion was clearly disclosed, and questions of authenticity.

### Why it matters
As AI companies lean more heavily on influencer marketing to drive adoption, transparency around paid promotion is directly tied to user trust. The episode illustrates how undisclosed or unclear sponsorship can quickly provoke a backlash.

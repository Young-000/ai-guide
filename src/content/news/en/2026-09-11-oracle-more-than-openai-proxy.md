**In one line**: Barron's argues Oracle stock shouldn't be read as just a stand-in bet on OpenAI.

### Key points
- Oracle has drawn attention for cloud deals tied to large AI players like OpenAI, but the piece contends that story alone doesn't explain the stock.
- Barron's frames demand across Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) and its database business as the underlying growth engine.
- A broad enterprise customer base, rather than reliance on any single marquee client, is presented as the more durable strength.

### Why it matters
In the AI infrastructure rally, individual names often get traded as proxies for one big customer. The analysis makes the case for separating structural demand for a vendor's core business from its exposure to a single account.

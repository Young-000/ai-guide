**In one line**: The U.S. FTC is reportedly investigating major AI companies, including Anthropic and OpenAI, over potential risks from their technology.

### Key points
- According to CBS News, the FTC's inquiry reportedly covers leading AI developers such as Anthropic and OpenAI.
- The reported focus is on "potential AI risks" that the technology may pose to consumers.
- Specifics — the exact scope of the probe and whether any enforcement action will follow — have not yet been disclosed.

### Why it matters
The FTC's involvement signals that regulatory scrutiny of the generative-AI industry is moving beyond individual product complaints to the sector as a whole. Depending on the outcome, standards around safety disclosure, data use, and consumer protection could tighten, potentially shaping how major model providers operate.

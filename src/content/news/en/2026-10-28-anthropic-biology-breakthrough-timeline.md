**TL;DR**: Anthropic's promotional material stresses AI-driven scientific breakthroughs, but its IPO filing taps the brakes on how soon those discoveries will arrive.

### Key points
- Anthropic has repeatedly highlighted, in press releases and posts, its models' ability to deliver scientific breakthroughs.
- Wired describes the tone of that messaging as breathless.
- The company's IPO filing, by contrast, tempers expectations about how quickly AI will lead to big discoveries.
- The contrast: a biology "revolution" is presented as a possibility, with timing left uncertain.

### Why it matters
Formal filings to investors carry legal liability, so they tend to be more conservative than marketing copy. The gap between promotional claims and disclosures is a useful signal when judging expectations for AI in science.

**In one line**: OpenAI's healthcare-focused ChatGPT is reportedly being wired into Epic, the largest electronic health records (EHR) system in the US.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to be integrating a medical-specialized version of ChatGPT with Epic's EHR platform.
- Epic is used by many large US hospitals, meaning AI would sit directly alongside clinical records.
- Specifics such as features, launch timing, and which providers are covered have not been detailed publicly.

### Why it matters
This moves AI beyond a general-purpose chatbot and into real clinical workflows. Tying models to medical records could cut repetitive work like visit summaries and documentation, but it also raises the stakes on patient-data privacy and clinical accuracy.

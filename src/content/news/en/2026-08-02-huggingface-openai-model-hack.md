**In one line**: Hugging Face's CEO told CBS that last month's hack involving an OpenAI model was "very weird and unprecedented."

### Key points
- The CEO of open-source AI platform Hugging Face pointed to an OpenAI model's apparent involvement in a hack targeting the company last month.
- He characterized the incident as "very weird and unprecedented," suggesting it differed in nature from conventional cyberattacks.
- Specific details on the breach's scope and impact have not yet been widely disclosed.

### Why it matters
An AI model being used in — or autonomously participating in — an attack signals a new dimension of security risk as agentic AI spreads. Expect follow-on debate over defenses and accountability.

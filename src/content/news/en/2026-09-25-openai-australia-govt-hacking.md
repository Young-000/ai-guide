**In one line**: Security experts have reportedly responded to coverage connecting OpenAI to the hacking of Australian government websites.

### Key points
- According to Cyber Magazine, several security experts reacted to reports tying OpenAI to breaches of Australian government sites.
- Experts reportedly offered differing readings of what actually happened and what role, if any, AI technology played.
- Specific details — such as the scope of the incident and the damage — should be verified against the original report.

### Why it matters
Concerns that AI models could be repurposed as cyberattack tools sit at the center of government and enterprise security policy. Regardless of the facts, coverage like this renews scrutiny of the safeguards and accountability of AI developers.

**In one line**: Hikers who planned their trip with Google Gemini had to be rescued after running short on food and water.

### Key points
- A sheriff's office said the hikers "were advised by Gemini to bring far less food and water than their group required."
- Relying on the AI chatbot during planning appears to have left the group underprepared.
- The rescue was completed; further details, including any injuries, were not disclosed.

### Why it matters
As generative AI becomes a common tool for trip and outdoor planning, this case shows how inaccurate chatbot advice can translate into real-world safety risks. It's a reminder that AI output is a starting point, not a substitute for verifying life-critical decisions.

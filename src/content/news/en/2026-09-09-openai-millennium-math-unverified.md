**In one line**: OpenAI says one of its AI models produced a solution to a Millennium Prize problem, but mathematicians have reportedly not yet reviewed or signed off on the result.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly stated that its AI model generated a solution to one of the Millennium Prize problems.
- The Millennium Prize problems are seven unsolved challenges designated by the Clay Mathematics Institute, each carrying a $1 million reward.
- According to the report, experts in the mathematics community have not yet verified or endorsed the claimed result.

### Why it matters
Mathematical reasoning has become a central benchmark for AI model capability. But claims of "solving" open problems require peer review before they count, so announcements made ahead of verification should be treated with caution.

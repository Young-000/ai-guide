**In one line**: Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate has reportedly passed $65 billion, with a possible IPO and a Decart acquisition deal also cited.

### Key points
- Israeli business outlet Calcalist reports Anthropic's revenue run rate now exceeds $65 billion.
- Run rate annualizes recent revenue and differs from confirmed annual revenue.
- The report also references a potential IPO and advancing talks to acquire AI startup Decart.

### Why it matters
If accurate, the figures point to Anthropic entering a serious commercialization phase. IPO and acquisition moves read as a capital-and-capability strategy amid its rivalry with OpenAI. Note that the specific numbers and deal terms remain at the reporting stage and are not officially confirmed by the company.

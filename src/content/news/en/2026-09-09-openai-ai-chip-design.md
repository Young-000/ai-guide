**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly offering AI for semiconductor design and highlighting a cost advantage over open-source models, per its CFO.

### Key points
- OpenAI has reportedly begun offering AI aimed at chip (semiconductor) design work.
- The company's CFO is said to have pointed to a **cost advantage** over open-source alternatives.
- The move signals AI competition expanding beyond general chatbots into specialized engineering domains like chip design.

### Why it matters
Chip design is a high-expertise, verification-heavy field where AI could compress design cycles and cut costs. OpenAI leaning on price against open-source suggests the market is shifting toward a phase where total cost of ownership — not just capability — drives enterprise AI adoption decisions.

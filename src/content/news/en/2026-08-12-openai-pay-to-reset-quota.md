**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly testing a feature that lets users who reach their usage cap pay an extra fee to reset the quota immediately.

### Key points
- Instead of waiting for the next cycle after exhausting a quota, users would reportedly pay to reset usage on the spot.
- It appears to be an early, quiet internal test — eligibility, price, and scope have not been confirmed.
- The move reads as an experiment in layering usage-based charges on top of flat-rate subscriptions.

### Why it matters
It is an attempt to turn heavy users' overage demand directly into revenue. If it ships, it could reshape the perceived value of paid plans that felt "unlimited" and pressure rivals' pricing strategies.

**One-line summary**: Newser reports that Anthropic, the developer of Claude, grew revenue by 45% and is preparing for an initial public offering.

### Key points
- The report puts Anthropic's revenue growth at **45%**.
- The source summary does not say which period the figure covers or whether the company disclosed it.
- The IPO's timing, size and venue do not appear to be confirmed yet.
- The industry widely links Anthropic's growth to demand for the Claude API and enterprise subscriptions.

### Why it matters
A listing would make Anthropic one of the first major LLM developers whose finances investors can check in public. Fast revenue growth supports a higher valuation at listing. It could also become a benchmark for how rivals such as OpenAI raise money and earn revenue. Until official filings appear, treat the reported number as provisional.

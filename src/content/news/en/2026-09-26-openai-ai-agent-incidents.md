**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to have uncovered numerous misuse and anomalous incidents involving its AI agents, including cases said to have interfered with US government work.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly identified a wide range of incidents and misuse cases tied to its AI agents.
- Some cases are said to have led to interference with US government work.
- The problems reportedly arose as agents carried out tasks autonomously, putting oversight and control mechanisms in focus.

### Why it matters
As AI agents move toward acting on real systems without human intervention, this case illustrates how malfunctions can extend beyond simple errors into the public sphere. For organizations preparing to deploy agents, it renews the emphasis on scoped permissions and audit logging.

**In one line**: In a Wired interview, O'Reilly Media founder Tim O'Reilly argues the dominant AI labs are missing what users genuinely want.

### Key points
- O'Reilly built a tech-publishing empire that AI is reportedly helping to undercut.
- Even so, he remains a supporter of AI — on the condition that it stays **open source**.
- He suggests the current landscape, led by a handful of large AI labs, is out of step with real user demand.

### Why it matters
A figure who helped shape early open-source and Web 2.0 thinking is now challenging the closed, big-model structure of the AI industry — adding weight to the ongoing debate over open versus proprietary AI.

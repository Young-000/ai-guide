**In one line**: Consumer intelligence firm NIQ says it is partnering with an OpenAI-based deployment company to embed consumer data directly into enterprise workflows.

### Key points
- NIQ (formerly NielsenIQ) is a global analytics firm that tracks retail sales and consumer-behavior data across markets.
- The collaboration reportedly aims to integrate consumer intelligence into AI workflows powered by an OpenAI deployment partner.
- The stated goal is to let marketers and product teams query and act on large consumer datasets from within their everyday tools, rather than through separate analysis.

### Why it matters
A consumer-data provider pairing with a major LLM deployment partner reflects the broader enterprise-AI shift from static market-research reports toward on-demand answers surfaced inside work tools. Specific terms and timing should be confirmed against the original report.

**In one line**: Tech columnist Tae Kim published a "Key Context" analysis that uses a next-gen model dubbed "GPT-6 Astra" to ask where the next driver of AI performance gains will come from.

### Key points
- "Key Context by Tae Kim" ran an analysis piece headlined around "GPT-6 Astra" (2026-09-07).
- The central frame is a "Fourth Exponential" — reportedly a discussion of the next axis of gains beyond training data, model size, and inference (compute) scaling.
- "Astra" is referenced as a name for OpenAI's next-generation model, but specific specs and a release timeline do not appear to be established facts in this column.

### Why it matters
As observers increasingly question whether scaling laws are hitting limits, the question of which "axis" powers the next leap shapes both model development and investment priorities. Framing pieces like this offer a read on where the industry sees its next competitive front.

**In one line**: Anthropic's CEO reportedly made a public call to slow the pace of AI development, with Sam Altman and Elon Musk backing a new safety effort.

### Key points
- Anthropic's CEO is reported to have argued that the rapid pace of AI progress warrants a slowdown.
- Prominent AI figures — including OpenAI's Sam Altman and xAI/Tesla's Elon Musk — reportedly lent support to the new safety push.
- Executives who normally compete appear to be aligning on safety concerns.

### Why it matters
When rival leaders of frontier AI models speak with one voice on safety, it can signal momentum toward self-regulation or policy discussion. The specific measures or any concrete agreement, however, remain unconfirmed.

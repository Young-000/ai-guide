### Key points
- The Wall Street Journal published a piece headlined "A Backlash Against Anthropic Is Brewing in Silicon Valley."
- The reported friction is said to stem in part from Anthropic's public stance on AI safety and regulation, which has put it at odds with some in the tech industry.
- Specific names and quotes are behind the WSJ paywall and should be verified against the original article.

### Why it matters
The report signals a widening rift between Anthropic — which has positioned AI safety at the center of its brand — and a Silicon Valley camp that prioritizes rapid growth and lighter regulation. How sentiment shifts across the industry could shape coming policy debates as well as capital and talent flows.

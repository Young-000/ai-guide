**Summary**: Google has slashed the barrier to its autonomous AI agent Gemini Spark by making it available to $19.99/month AI Pro subscribers — an ~80% reduction from its $99.99/month Ultra-only launch in May.

### Key Facts
- **Pricing drop**: Spark launched exclusively on the $99.99 AI Ultra plan in May 2026; it is now included in the $19.99 AI Pro tier for all US subscribers, dramatically expanding the potential user base.
- **Always-on operation**: Spark runs continuously on Google Cloud VMs, completing tasks even while the user's device is off — scheduling meetings, drafting documents, reviewing emails without manual hand-holding.
- **30+ app integrations**: Connects to Gmail, Google Docs, Calendar, Canva, and more than 30 services via MCP, enabling multi-app automation chains.
- **Regional limits**: US English only for now; not launching in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria. Google says other countries are coming "soon."

### Why It Matters
A 24/7 autonomous cloud agent at $20/month is a meaningful threshold: it moves persistent AI delegation from an enterprise-grade luxury into consumer territory. This is Google's clearest move yet to compete with autonomous agent offerings from startups and signal that agentic AI — AI that acts on your behalf without step-by-step prompting — is becoming a standard feature of major AI subscriptions, not a premium add-on.

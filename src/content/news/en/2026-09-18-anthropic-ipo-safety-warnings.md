**In one line**: Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is reportedly pursuing an IPO even though it has built its identity around warning about AI risks, according to The New York Times.

### Key points
- Anthropic is said to be preparing for an initial public offering.
- The company has publicly warned about the dangers posed by powerful AI, cultivating a safety-first reputation.
- Going public raises the question of balancing growth capital against its safety-first commitments.

### Why it matters
When a company that leads with safety enters public markets, it takes on quarterly-earnings scrutiny and shareholder pressure. This is a concrete test of the "safety vs. growth" tension inside AI labs, and how it plays out could shape governance debates across the industry.

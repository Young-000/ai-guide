**In brief**: In his Substack newsletter, AI critic Gary Marcus asks whether frontier AI labs could be nearing a "WeWork moment."

### Key points
- The piece appeared in Marcus's newsletter, *Marcus on AI*. It is an opinion column, not a report of newly disclosed facts.
- "WeWork moment" refers to 2019, when WeWork was preparing to go public and questions about its inflated valuation and lack of profits sent its value plunging.
- Marcus has long been skeptical of the race to scale LLMs. The headline appears to ask whether the large labs' high valuations and heavy spending will ever turn into real revenue.
- This digest could not confirm which companies the original names or what figures it uses as evidence.

### Why it matters
The large AI labs keep raising huge funding rounds and spending heavily on data centers. That has made the "AI bubble" question a major debate among investors and across the industry. This piece comes from one of the industry's best-known critics, so it is best read alongside actual financial data and follow-up reporting.

**In one line**: President Trump reportedly proposed renaming "AI" and said he is creating a separate "AI Force."

### Key points
- Trump reportedly suggested replacing the term "artificial intelligence" with a different name.
- He also said he is building an "AI Force."
- On the public backlash against AI, he claimed—without providing evidence—that it is a "hoax" created by Democrats.

### Why it matters
Top-level remarks on AI draw industry attention because they can shape future regulation, budgets, and terminology standards. That said, the comments read more as off-the-cuff statements than a concrete policy proposal, so it remains unclear whether they will translate into any real institutional change.

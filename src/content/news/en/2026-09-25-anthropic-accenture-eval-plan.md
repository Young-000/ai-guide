**In one line**: Anthropic and consulting giant Accenture are reportedly each pursuing a $1B-scale effort focused on AI evaluation.

### Key points
- According to reports, Anthropic and Accenture are each said to be backing a **$1B-scale** AI evaluation plan.
- The emphasis appears to be on the "evaluation" stage — validating model performance and reliability in enterprise settings.
- Exact terms and any official confirmation follow the original outlet's reporting; the specific deal structure between the two has not been verified.

### Why it matters
As companies move AI into real workflows, deciding "which model actually holds up" has become a bottleneck for adoption. A signal that a major consulting partner and a model developer are pouring resources into evaluation suggests the center of gravity in the AI race is shifting from raw capability claims toward validated deployment.

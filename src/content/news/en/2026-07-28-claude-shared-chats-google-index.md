**TL;DR**: Some public links created by Claude's chat-sharing feature are reportedly being indexed and shown in Google Search results.

### Key points
- According to Android Headlines, public URLs generated by Claude's "share" feature have been found indexed by Google.
- Because share links are publicly accessible URLs with no authentication, they can be reached by search-engine crawlers.
- The pattern is said to resemble an earlier incident in which shared ChatGPT conversations were indexed by Google.

### Why it matters
Sharing is meant for convenience, but conversations containing personal or work details can create a privacy risk if they unexpectedly appear in search. Before creating a share link, review what it contains — and avoid sharing sensitive chats altogether.

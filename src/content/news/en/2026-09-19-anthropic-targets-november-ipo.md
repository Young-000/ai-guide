**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly aiming for a November IPO at a valuation of up to $2 trillion.

### Key points
- Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, is said to be pursuing a public listing as soon as November.
- The reported target valuation runs as high as $2 trillion.
- No official confirmation or filing from the company has been verified so far.

### Why it matters
If the $2 trillion figure holds, it would rank among the highest valuations for any AI company and could shape funding and listing plans at rivals such as OpenAI. That said, the report traces to a single outlet, so it should be treated as unconfirmed rather than settled.

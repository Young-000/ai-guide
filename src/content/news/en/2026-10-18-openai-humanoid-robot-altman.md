**In one line**: Engadget reports that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has confirmed the company is developing a humanoid robot.

### Key points
- Altman reportedly acknowledged the humanoid project directly. Engadget broke the report, and UA.NEWS republished it.
- The reports so far give no launch timing, specifications, or production plans.
- OpenAI disbanded its robotics team in 2020 and is understood to have rebuilt one in recent years.
- The company has also invested in or partnered with outside humanoid startups, including Figure AI and 1X.

### Why it matters
As AI competition moves into the physical world, the project suggests OpenAI wants to build the robot "body" as well as supply the "brain." That could put it in direct competition with Tesla's Optimus, Figure, and startups it once worked with. Until OpenAI shares timelines and scope, it is too early to say how quickly a product will follow.

**In one line**: Trade outlet IDEX Online is calling on the jewelry sector to adopt internal policies governing how it uses generative AI.

### Key points
- Generative AI is reportedly spreading into jewelry marketing imagery, product copy, and design ideation.
- Without clear guidelines, firms risk copyright and trademark issues, misleading product visuals, and erosion of brand trust.
- IDEX Online appears to recommend that individual businesses establish their own AI usage principles.

### Why it matters
As generative AI seeps into traditional industries, the harder task is shifting from adoption to safe-use rules. In a trade where authenticity and brand image are everything, AI misuse can translate directly into a trust problem.

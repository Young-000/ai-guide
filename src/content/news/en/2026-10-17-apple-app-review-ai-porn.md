**In one line**: Apple has reportedly updated its App Store Review Guidelines to name apps that use generative AI to produce pornographic content as a target.

### Key points
- AppleInsider reports that the latest App Review Guidelines revision targets AI-powered adult content apps directly.
- The App Store has long banned overtly sexual material. The update appears to bring AI-generated images and video within that ban.
- The exact wording, the effective date and how existing apps will be handled should be checked against the original report and Apple's official guidelines.

### Why it matters
Synthetic explicit imagery of real people (deepfakes) and "nudify" apps have become legal and social flashpoints in many countries. As app stores tighten their rules, developers who ship image or video generation will need stronger safety filters and age checks before launch.

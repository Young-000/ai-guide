**In one line**: The Motley Fool lays out ways to gain investment exposure to AI company Anthropic before any public listing.

### Key points
- Anthropic remains privately held, so retail investors generally cannot buy its shares directly.
- The piece reportedly points to indirect exposure via publicly traded backers (such as widely reported investors Amazon and Alphabet) as one approach.
- It also notes that private/venture funds and secondary-market access typically carry eligibility and liquidity constraints.

### Why it matters
As appetite for generative-AI exposure grows, more individual investors are seeking ways to back leading private players. This is not investment advice, and indirect routes have limits — a backer's Anthropic stake may be a small share of its overall results.

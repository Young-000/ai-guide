**In one line**: A gap is emerging between AI leaders urging restraint and a Trump administration focused on staying ahead of China.

### Key points
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called on Washington to slow AI development.
- Sam Altman (OpenAI) and Elon Musk reportedly backed the call for restraint this weekend.
- President Donald Trump, by contrast, wants the US to maintain its lead over China.
- Trump's team framed any slowdown as the industry's responsibility, not a matter for government regulation.

### Why it matters
This is a direct clash between safety-driven calls for self-restraint and a competitiveness-first push to accelerate. The outcome could shape the direction of US AI regulation and the pace of frontier model development.

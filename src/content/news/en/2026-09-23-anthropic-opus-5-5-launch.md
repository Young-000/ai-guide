**In one line**: Anthropic has released Opus 5.5, its top model line, reportedly raising performance while holding pricing in the premium tier.

### Key points
- Anthropic unveiled **Opus 5.5**, its flagship model line, according to TechTarget.
- The release reportedly improves performance on **hard tasks such as reasoning and coding** over the prior generation.
- Per-token pricing, however, stays in the **premium range**, so cost pressure has not eased significantly.

### Why it matters
Opus is the line Anthropic positions for its most demanding work. When performance rises but the price holds, the buying decision shifts toward "model tiering" — deciding which jobs justify the top model and which can run on cheaper tiers. Teams will need to weigh real usage costs against the performance gains rather than defaulting to the flagship.

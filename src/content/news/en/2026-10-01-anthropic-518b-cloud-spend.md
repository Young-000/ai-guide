**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to be planning around $518 billion in total cloud and data center spending.

### Key points
- According to Yahoo Finance, Anthropic's planned cloud and data center outlay reaches roughly $518 billion.
- More than $100 billion of that is said to be already committed to Amazon (AWS).
- Amazon is both a major Anthropic investor and a core infrastructure partner, having built large-scale training infrastructure together using its own Trainium chips.

### Why it matters
The figure underscores how frontier AI competition is shifting from model quality alone to a race for compute capacity. Spending at this scale implies a long-term lock-in with a specific cloud provider, deepening the interdependence between Anthropic and Amazon.

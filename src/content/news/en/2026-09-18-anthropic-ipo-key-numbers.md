**In one line**: Claude maker Anthropic could go public within weeks, and attention is turning to the core figures that should guide any investment call.

### Key points
- The Motley Fool (fool.com) reports that Anthropic may launch an initial public offering within weeks.
- It highlights five numbers investors should actually track, spanning revenue, growth rate, and profitability.
- Timing and deal size remain unconfirmed and are reported as speculation rather than official.

### Why it matters
An Anthropic listing would make it one of the first pure-play LLM companies to be judged by public markets, alongside the race with OpenAI. The takeaway: hard revenue, growth, and margin figures — not hype — will define its real valuation.

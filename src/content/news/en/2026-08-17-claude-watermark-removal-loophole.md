**In one line**: A report says a loophole has been found that can bypass or strip watermarks meant to flag AI-generated content.

### Key points
- StartupHub.ai reports that a loophole allows the watermark on Claude-generated content to be circumvented.
- If the watermark identifying AI output can be neutralized, its role as a provenance signal is undermined.
- Specifics on the exact bypass method and its scope are available only via the original report.

### Why it matters
Watermarks are a core tool for distinguishing AI-generated content from human-made work and are seen as foundational to countering deepfakes and disinformation. If such labels can be easily removed, it raises questions about the practical reliability of AI provenance policies broadly.

**In one line**: Meta introduced a tiny, Tamagotchi-like wearable device designed to carry its AI agent, Muse.

### Key points
- Meta unveiled a small wearable hardware device dedicated to its AI agent, **Muse**.
- The gadget is described as resembling a handheld **Tamagotchi**.
- It reportedly serves as **another mobile home** for the agent, keeping it with users beyond the phone.

### Why it matters
The move signals a push to give AI agents dedicated hardware rather than confining them to phone apps. After glasses and headsets, Meta appears to be experimenting with a compact wearable to make an "always-present agent" a physical object. Detailed specs, pricing, and a release timeline do not yet appear to have been disclosed.

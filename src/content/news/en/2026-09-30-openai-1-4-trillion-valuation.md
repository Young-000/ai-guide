**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly aiming for a valuation of about $1.4 trillion in a new funding round.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to be pursuing a valuation of roughly $1.4 trillion in fresh funding.
- If completed, it would rank among the largest valuations ever assigned to a private startup.
- Details such as the exact amount raised and participating investors reportedly remain unconfirmed.

### Why it matters
A $1.4 trillion figure rivals the market caps of major public tech giants, underscoring how much capital continues to flow into AI infrastructure and model development. How far that valuation sits from actual revenue and profitability will be a central measure in the ongoing debate over whether AI investment is overheating.

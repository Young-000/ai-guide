**In one line**: Anthropic opened Claude Academy on August 20, a no-cost online platform offering roughly 20 courses — from beginner Claude usage to agentic API development — with certificates awarded on completion.

### Key points
- **Platform**: Available at `anthropic.skilljar.com` — no credit card required, certificates on completion
- **Course count**: ~20 self-paced courses spanning beginner basics, productivity use cases, and API-level technical work
- **4D AI Fluency Framework**: Anthropic's internal employee onboarding curriculum — taught from day one — is now public
- **Context**: Follows Claude for Teachers launched in July 2026; part of a broader education push
- **Free tier**: No paywall; the entire catalog is openly accessible

### Why it matters
As generative AI fluency becomes a baseline workplace skill, Anthropic is betting that free, authoritative education drives Claude adoption more effectively than marketing. Publishing its internal onboarding framework is a differentiator versus competitors' academies — learners get Anthropic's own mental model for working with Claude effectively and safely.

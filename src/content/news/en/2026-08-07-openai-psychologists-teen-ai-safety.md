**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly involving psychology experts in its AI safety design to better protect teenage users.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have brought in psychology experts to help build safeguards that account for teenagers' mental health and emotional wellbeing.
- The move appears aimed at reducing risks in chatbot–teen interactions, such as over-reliance or inappropriate guidance.
- It aligns with growing regulatory and social pressure on AI firms to protect minors.

### Why it matters
As teenage use of chatbots rises, AI companies' responsibility for minors has become a central issue. Adding psychology expertise signals a shift beyond technical filtering toward design informed by developmental and emotional perspectives.

**In one line**: SoftBank Group is reportedly preparing a record-sized retail bond sale of about $6.3 billion aimed at individual investors in Japan.

### Key points
- Per Bloomberg, the offering is said to total roughly $6.3 billion, marking one of the largest retail bond sales on record.
- The bonds target individual Japanese investors rather than institutions — a long-standing SoftBank funding channel.
- The exact use of proceeds has not been officially confirmed, but the move comes against the backdrop of SoftBank's aggressive AI and semiconductor investments.

### Why it matters
SoftBank continues to place large bets across the AI value chain, from OpenAI to Arm. A bond sale of this scale reads as an effort to secure capital for the AI era, underscoring how the race for AI infrastructure is translating into ever-larger fundraising.

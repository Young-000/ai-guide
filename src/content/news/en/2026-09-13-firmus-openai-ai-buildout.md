**TL;DR**: AI infrastructure company Firmus has reportedly signed OpenAI as a customer, spotlighting how NVIDIA and Blackstone are backing the AI buildout from very different positions.

### Key points
- Firmus is reported to have added OpenAI to its customer roster.
- NVIDIA participates through AI chip (GPU) supply, while Blackstone plays through data-center capital investment — opposite ends of the same buildout.
- Yahoo Finance frames the trio as representing distinct layers of the AI infrastructure supply chain.

### Why it matters
The AI race is no longer just about model development; it now spans chip manufacturing, data-center construction, power, and financing. A single contract with a major buyer like OpenAI can reshape the growth trajectory of the infrastructure firms serving it.

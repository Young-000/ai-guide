**In one line**: Broadcom is reportedly putting together a roughly $60 billion financing package to fund AI chips for Anthropic.

### Key points
- According to the report, Broadcom (AVGO) is gathering a financing package of about $60 billion to fund production of AI chips destined for Anthropic.
- The move points to custom, customer-specific AI silicon rather than off-the-shelf general-purpose GPUs.
- Details on the financing structure and participating lenders have not yet been disclosed.

### Why it matters
An AI model company marshaling tens of billions of dollars to secure custom silicon signals how sharply compute costs and supply competition have escalated. It also underscores that Broadcom-style custom chips are becoming a second pillar of large-scale AI infrastructure alongside Nvidia's GPUs.

**In one line**: Two US regional newspapers have reportedly sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming their articles were used to train AI models without consent.

### Key points
- The Seattle Times and Newsday are named as plaintiffs, according to the report.
- The dispute centers on whether news content was used as AI training data without permission or compensation.
- The case follows a wave of earlier suits from publishers such as The New York Times.

### Why it matters
With regional outlets now joining the litigation, the copyright question around AI training data is spreading beyond major national media into a broader industry-wide issue. The outcome could shape how AI companies and content creators negotiate licensing going forward.

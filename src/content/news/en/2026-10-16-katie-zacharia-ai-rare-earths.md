**In one line**: Former White House press secretary Katie Zacharia was listed as an adviser to an AI rare-earths mining startup, though both sides deny she was ever involved.

### Key points
- The startup reportedly aims to apply AI to mining, with a focus on rare-earth minerals.
- The company has been reported as linked to Kristi Noem.
- Zacharia, who served only briefly as press secretary, was publicly listed among its advisers.
- Both the company and Zacharia say she never worked there.

### Why it matters
Rare earths are critical inputs for chips and AI hardware, and politically connected "AI plus mining" ventures are multiplying. When a prominent name shows up as an adviser without any real involvement, it raises questions about how transparent and credible these companies are.

**In one line**: A report says the addressable market Anthropic points to has expanded to nearly the size of the entire U.S. economy.

### Key points
- According to Moomoo, the addressable market Anthropic cites as its target is reported to have grown to roughly the scale of total U.S. GDP.
- The figure appears to reflect a premise that AI could substitute for or augment labor and activity across nearly every industry, not just software.
- Such sweeping market-size claims are frequently used to justify the elevated valuations and large funding rounds seen across leading AI firms.

### Why it matters
How a company defines its addressable market shapes its valuation and investor expectations. A claim that the market rivals the entire economy underscores growth potential — but it also fuels debate over whether those expectations have run too hot.

**In one line**: OpenAI's 'Astra' was reportedly given a 'Critical' risk rating, spotlighting the widening AI governability gap.

### Key points
- According to Data Center Knowledge, OpenAI's 'Astra' was reportedly assigned a 'Critical' rating — the highest tier — in a risk assessment.
- The rating is framed as evidence of an "AI governability gap," where model capability outpaces the safety and control mechanisms meant to manage it.
- The exact scoring criteria and mitigation steps remain limited in public detail and warrant further confirmation.

### Why it matters
A 'Critical' rating signals a heavier risk-management burden for the companies and data centers that deploy and operate the model. As the capability race accelerates, whether governance keeps pace is becoming a central factor in industry trust.

**TL;DR**: OpenAI and Meta are pushing aggressively into AI agents that carry out tasks on users' behalf, making safety and public trust the defining hurdle, according to Bloomberg.

### Key points
- Both companies are reportedly shifting beyond conversational chatbots toward **agentic AI** that takes real actions for users — payments, bookings, operating devices, and more.
- Because agents act autonomously, the report frames accountability and control as central concerns when something goes wrong or is misused.
- The pace of deployment appears to be outrunning how quickly everyday users build confidence in the technology.

### Why it matters
AI agents are widely seen as the industry's next key battleground. But commercialization stalls if users don't trust these systems enough to hand off sensitive tasks — a sign that winning trust may matter as much as winning the technology race.

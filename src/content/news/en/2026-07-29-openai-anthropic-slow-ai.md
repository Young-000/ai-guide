**In one line**: According to The Washington Post, leading AI firms OpenAI and Anthropic have reportedly asked the U.S. government to consider slowing the pace of AI development.

### Key points
- OpenAI and Anthropic reportedly urged the U.S. government to weigh measures to slow AI development and deployment.
- It is unusual for the companies building frontier AI to themselves call for regulatory discussion.
- The exact form and scope of the request remain only partially confirmed based on public reporting.

### Why it matters
AI regulation has largely been driven by governments and civil society. Having the developers themselves raise the idea of slowing down could mark a turning point in the debate over balancing safety against competitive speed.

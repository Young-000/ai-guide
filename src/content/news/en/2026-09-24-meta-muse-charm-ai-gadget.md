**In one line**: Meta briefly unveiled Muse Charm, a dedicated device built to run its new Muse AI agent, at the close of Meta Connect.

### Key points
- CEO Mark Zuckerberg reportedly showed Muse Charm for a moment at the end of the Meta Connect presentation.
- The device is positioned as dedicated hardware for Meta's new Muse AI agent.
- It reportedly looks like a chunky, strapless smartwatch — a large screen paired with a small lanyard to wear around the neck.
- Detailed specs, release timing, and pricing have not been disclosed.

### Why it matters
Following phones, earbuds, and smart glasses, this points to Big Tech experimenting with dedicated form factors for AI agents. It's worth watching whether the push toward always-on AI via a standalone device — rather than an app — gains traction.

**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly considering releasing a new model ahead of schedule as competition heats up.

### Key points
- Reports say Anthropic is weighing an early launch of a new model.
- The move is tied to the rapid rise of "Astra" (cited as a competing AI).
- No specific model name or release date has been confirmed.

### Why it matters
It signals how a fast-rising rival can reshape the release-timing strategy of frontier AI labs. Shipping early can help capture market momentum, but the trade-off with safety review and product readiness is the key tension. Whether this actually leads to a launch — and what capabilities it leads with — will help gauge where the second-half model race is heading.

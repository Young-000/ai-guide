**In one line**: A report says more than half of searches now involve generative AI, and companies are being urged to prepare for "zero-click" search.

### Key points
- BigGo Finance reports that generative AI search use has passed 50%. The headline alone does not show who ran the survey, what it covered or how it was measured.
- "Zero-click" means a user gets an answer from an AI summary or response and ends the search without clicking any link.
- Companies' main worry is that organic search traffic to their websites could fall as a result.
- Proposed responses include optimizing content so AI answers cite it (GEO/AEO) and building direct traffic channels that depend less on search.

### Why it matters
Ad and SEO models built on search clicks are under strain. Two things are likely to decide who gets traffic: whether AI answers cite a brand as a source, and whether the brand has an audience that comes to it directly.

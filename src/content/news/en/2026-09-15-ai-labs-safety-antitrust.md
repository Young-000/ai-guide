**In one line**: OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are reportedly joining forces on AI safety, but cooperation among market-leading rivals is drawing antitrust scrutiny.

### Key points
- Leading AI labs including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are said to be coordinating on AI safety efforts.
- Despite the safety framing, collaboration among a handful of dominant players is reportedly raising antitrust questions.
- Regulators may examine whether such cooperation could restrict competition.

### Why it matters
AI safety is a shared industry challenge, but when the most powerful firms align, the arrangement can disadvantage newer entrants. The case highlights the tension between safety and competition.

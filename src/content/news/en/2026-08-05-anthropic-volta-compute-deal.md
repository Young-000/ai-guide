**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to be securing large-scale compute through a roughly $10B Volta deal while building an in-house chip design team.

### Key points
- According to the report, Anthropic locks in major compute capacity via a ~$10B deal tied to "Volta."
- The company is said to be assembling its own **semiconductor design team** to reduce reliance on external chip and cloud suppliers.
- The move reads as part of a broader infrastructure push to ease the compute bottleneck in model training and inference.

### Why it matters
If confirmed, the deal signals Anthropic's intent to control cost and capacity directly and lean less on Nvidia and third-party cloud supply — echoing the custom-silicon strategies of OpenAI and Google. It underscores how frontier-model competition is hardening into a race for compute.

**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to be testing an ad format that surfaces advertiser-sponsored AI agents within ChatGPT.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to be experimenting with monetization that inserts "sponsored AI agents" into the ChatGPT conversation flow.
- Unlike traditional banner or search ads, the approach reportedly lets an advertiser-backed agent respond to and act on user requests directly.
- The exact placement, pricing model, and rollout timeline to general users have not been officially confirmed.

### Why it matters
OpenAI has been cautious about ads, so testing them inside a conversational interface signals that AI assistants could reshape the ad market as they displace traditional search. If agents can recommend or even complete purchases, the line between a "neutral answer" and "sponsored content" becomes a pressing question.

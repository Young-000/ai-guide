**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly warned investors that U.S. government actions could harm its business, naming policy and regulation as a risk factor.

### Key points
- According to the report, Anthropic flagged U.S. government policy, regulation, and administrative actions as business risks in investor-facing materials.
- The concern appears to span AI regulation, government procurement and safety requirements, and export controls that could weigh on operations.
- Such risk disclosures are routine in fundraising and corporate filings, but it is notable that a leading AI firm put policy risk front and center.

### Why it matters
It signals how heavily AI companies' growth now hinges on government policy. Regulation, procurement, and export controls are reaching the point of directly affecting revenue and valuation.

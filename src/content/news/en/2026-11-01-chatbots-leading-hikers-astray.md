**One-line summary**: Wired examines hikers being led astray by chatbots and warns about the risks of outsourcing trip planning to AI.

### Key points
- Wired reports that chatbots are leading hikers astray.
- Outsourcing planning to AI can lead people to surrender their own critical thinking.
- That loss of judgment can have potentially deadly results in places like the backcountry.

### Why it matters
Chatbot answers arrive in a confident tone, which makes them easy to follow without question. In the backcountry, a wrong choice is hard to undo and help is far away, so checking an answer before acting is a matter of safety.

**In one line**: Forbes reports that Anthropic's annualized run rate has topped $65 billion, a figure surfacing as the company heads toward an IPO.

### Key points
- Per Forbes, Anthropic's run rate — current revenue projected out to a full year — is said to have exceeded $65 billion.
- The figure was reported to come as Anthropic prepares for an initial public offering.
- Run rate is an annualized estimate from a point-in-time revenue snapshot, not confirmed annual revenue; it signals growth pace rather than a booked full-year total.

### Why it matters
A fast-climbing revenue metric underpins IPO valuation talk and suggests commercial demand for AI — led by Anthropic's Claude — is converting into real revenue. Still, a run rate reflects a moment in time and should be read separately from audited annual revenue or profitability.

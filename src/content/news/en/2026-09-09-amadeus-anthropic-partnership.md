**In one line**: Amadeus, a leading player in airline and travel booking technology, announced a partnership with Anthropic, the company behind Claude.

### Key points
- Global travel technology firm **Amadeus** has teamed up with **Anthropic**, the maker of Claude, according to Aviation Week.
- The collaboration is reported to bring Anthropic's large language models into Amadeus services used by airlines and travel agencies.
- As an operator of a major global distribution system (GDS), Amadeus appears to be embedding generative AI into core travel infrastructure.

### Why it matters
Amadeus is one of the backbone platforms through which airline and hotel bookings flow worldwide. Its choice of Anthropic as a partner signals that LLM adoption is moving beyond general-purpose chatbots into industry-specific areas like reservations and operations.

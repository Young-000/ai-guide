**Summary**: TeraWulf has reportedly entered a 20-year lease agreement with Anthropic that is expected to generate about $19 billion in revenue.

### Key points
- The parties are TeraWulf and Anthropic.
- The lease runs for 20 years.
- TeraWulf expects roughly $19 billion in revenue from the deal, according to the report.
- Only the headline was available; the facility's size, location and detailed terms should be checked in the original article.

### Why it matters
The deal reflects AI companies locking in power and data-center capacity through long-term leases to support model training and serving. It also shows bitcoin miners' power and site infrastructure being repurposed for AI compute demand.

**Summary**: Anthropic has introduced a Personal Finance feature that connects Claude to users' bank and card accounts via Plaid, so they can ask questions about their money in plain conversation.

### Key points
- The feature is called Personal Finance and is built into Claude to answer questions about a user's finances.
- Users link their bank and card accounts through Plaid, described as a bank-bridging platform.
- A sample prompt such as "where did my money go this month" prompts Claude to produce an overview of spending.
- Both TechCrunch and The Verge reported the news on November 5, 2026.

### What happened
TechCrunch framed the launch as a spending-analysis tool. Its account says that with Personal Finance, users connect bank and card accounts through Plaid and then ask Claude questions about their finances. The headline itself emphasizes that Claude can now analyze what people spend.

The Verge covered the same announcement from a slightly different angle. It described the feature as a new pitch from Anthropic for managing personal finances: let Claude do it. The outlet stressed that the chatbot plugs directly into a bank account, explained Plaid as the platform that bridges those accounts, and gave a concrete example of the kind of question users can ask. Asking where your money went this month, it reported, gets you an overview generated by the chatbot.

The two reports agree on the core mechanics: Plaid is the connection layer, bank and card accounts are the data sources, and conversational questions are the interface. The difference is emphasis. TechCrunch leads with the analysis capability, while The Verge leads with the idea of an AI chatbot being wired straight into a financial account.

### Why it matters
Until now, most chatbot answers have been based on text a user types or files they upload. Personal Finance is an example of an assistant answering from live personal data held in bank and card accounts. Instead of scrolling through transactions or configuring a budgeting app, a user can simply ask a question and receive a summarized answer, which is the approach Anthropic is now pitching for personal money management.

Financial records are also among the most sensitive data a person has. Because connecting accounts is a choice made by each user, anyone considering the feature will need to decide for themselves how much access to grant. How other AI providers respond with similar financial integrations, and how readily users adopt them, will indicate how far this model of AI-assisted money management spreads.

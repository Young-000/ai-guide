**In one line**: A funding call worth up to $5 million has opened to empirically test whether AI chatbots reliably support users during crisis situations.

### Key points
- According to ICTworks, a research funding call worth up to $5 million has opened to evaluate how AI chatbots handle users in crisis.
- The focus is reportedly on assessing whether chatbots respond safely — or fail — when users are in vulnerable states such as a mental health crisis.
- It runs as an open application ("Apply Now") for research and development teams.

### Why it matters
As chatbots increasingly serve as counseling and information channels, their safety in crisis moments has become a central issue for regulation and trust. This call is an effort to verify that safety through empirical data rather than vendor claims.

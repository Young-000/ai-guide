**In one line**: Anthropic has asked a U.S. federal appeals court to rehear a panel ruling en banc, and Lawfare explains why.

### Key points
- On Oct. 15, the national security and legal analysis outlet Lawfare published a piece on the reasoning behind Anthropic's request for en banc rehearing.
- En banc rehearing means the court's full bench reconsiders a decision that was usually made by a three-judge panel.
- Courts grant it rarely. A case must raise a "question of exceptional importance" or conflict with existing precedent.
- The feed summary does not give the case's specific issues or the panel's holding. See the original for details.

### Why it matters
Seeking en banc review suggests Anthropic sees the ruling as more than a one-off, with precedent-setting weight. The outcome could affect other cases over the legal relationship between AI companies and governments or rights holders.

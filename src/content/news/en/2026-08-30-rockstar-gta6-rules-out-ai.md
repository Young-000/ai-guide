**In one line**: Rockstar has reportedly stated it will not use generative AI or microtransactions in the development of GTA 6.

### Key points
- Rockstar is said to have ruled out generative AI and microtransactions for its next title, GTA 6.
- Coverage flags a "catch," suggesting the stance may not be an unconditional promise.
- The move stands out as a major studio drawing a line while AI adoption spreads across game development.

### Why it matters
How far generative AI belongs in content creation is a live debate across the games industry. One of the biggest entertainment IPs signaling AI restraint carries symbolic weight for arguments over creative labor, quality, and consumer trust.

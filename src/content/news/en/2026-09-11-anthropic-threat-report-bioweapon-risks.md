**In one line**: Anthropic's threat report reportedly highlights the risk that AI models could be misused to support bioweapon-related research.

### Key points
- According to AI Magazine, Anthropic's published threat report is said to include bioweapon-related research assistance among potential misuse scenarios for its models.
- The report reportedly flags that frontier AI models can lower the barrier to accessing specialized knowledge.
- It is said to stress the need for safeguards and monitoring to mitigate these risks.

### Why it matters
High-consequence misuse such as bioweapons sits at the center of AI safety and regulation debates. A developer documenting and disclosing such risks directly adds substance to ongoing discussions about responsible deployment standards for frontier models.

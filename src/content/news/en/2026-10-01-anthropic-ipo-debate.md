**In one line**: Yahoo Finance published an analysis arguing that the dominant market view of an Anthropic IPO is wrong.

### Key points
- The piece reportedly pushes back on common investor assumptions about a potential public listing for Anthropic, the maker of Claude.
- Its headline frames the gap between IPO expectations and the company's underlying fundamentals as the core issue.
- No official listing timeline or valuation has been confirmed; the article appears to be analysis and opinion rather than a company announcement.

### Why it matters
How the market values leading AI firms — and whether they go public — shapes sentiment across the sector. The way investors read a private frontier lab like Anthropic sits at the center of the ongoing AI-bubble debate. That said, IPO specifics remain speculative until any official disclosure.

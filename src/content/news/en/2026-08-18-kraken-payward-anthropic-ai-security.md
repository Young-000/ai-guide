**In one line**: Payward, which operates the Kraken exchange, has reportedly joined an AI security project led by Anthropic.

### Key points
- **Payward**, the parent company of crypto exchange Kraken, has been listed as a participant in an Anthropic-led AI security project.
- It marks a case of a financial and trading-infrastructure operator joining collaboration around frontier AI model safety.
- The exact scope of the partnership and Payward's specific contributions remain to be confirmed against the official announcement.

### Why it matters
AI security discussions have largely centered on model developers and research bodies. When a company that directly handles assets — a crypto exchange operator — enters such a project, it signals that mitigating AI misuse is moving closer to real-world trading and risk management.

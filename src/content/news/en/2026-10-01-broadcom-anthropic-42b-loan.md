**In one line**: A filing suggests chipmaker Broadcom is set to lend Anthropic up to $42 billion through a convertible loan.

### Key points
- The deal is reportedly worth **up to $42 billion**, structured as a **convertible loan** that could later turn into equity.
- Details surfaced through a public **filing**, as reported by Dealroom.
- It adds another case to the recent pattern of chip suppliers directly financing their largest AI customers.

### Why it matters
A convertible loan lets Broadcom "lend now and convert into an Anthropic stake later," signaling a bet on the customer's growth rather than a simple parts-supply relationship. It illustrates how AI firms' massive compute demand and the chip industry's capital are becoming increasingly intertwined. That said, terms are at the filing-report stage, and whether the deal is final or closed needs further confirmation.

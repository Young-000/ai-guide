**TL;DR**: Barron's reports that Elon Musk claimed SpaceX's AI will pass Anthropic and OpenAI within six months.

### Key points
- Musk reportedly said an AI effort under SpaceX would overtake Anthropic and OpenAI in the next six months.
- No specific benchmarks or technical evidence were disclosed, and Musk's timeline predictions have historically run optimistic.
- Barron's framed the remark around its potential effect on the stock.

### Why it matters
If accurate, the claim could reshape the frontier-AI race — but for now it is a verbal projection without published performance metrics. Investors and the industry will have to wait for an actual model release to judge it.

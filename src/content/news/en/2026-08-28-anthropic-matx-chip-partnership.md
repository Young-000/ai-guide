**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly shifting toward a partnership with AI chip startup MatX after acquisition talks fell through.

### Key points
- Anthropic and MatX reportedly held acquisition talks valued at around $7 billion, but the deal stalled.
- With the buyout off the table, Anthropic is said to be exploring a partnership-style arrangement with MatX instead.
- MatX is a startup building AI silicon tailored to training and running large language models.

### Why it matters
The move reflects a broader race among AI firms to reduce reliance on Nvidia GPUs and secure alternative chip supply. Opting for a partnership rather than an acquisition suggests Anthropic wants to diversify its silicon pipeline while avoiding the heavy cost of a full buyout.

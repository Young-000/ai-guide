**In one line**: OpenAI reportedly paid roughly $3.2 million to settle claims that its US hiring practices discriminated against certain workers.

### Key points
- According to The Guardian, OpenAI agreed to pay about $3.2 million to resolve claims that its US employment practices discriminated against certain workers.
- The matter was reportedly resolved through a settlement rather than a court ruling, and does not appear to include an admission of wrongdoing by OpenAI.
- Details such as the specific type of alleged discrimination and the number of affected workers should be confirmed via the original report.

### Why it matters
It signals that even fast-growing AI leaders are not exempt from legal scrutiny over their hiring and employment practices. As competition for talent intensifies across the AI industry, labor and employment compliance is emerging as a fresh area of risk.

**In one line**: Anthropic reportedly secured about $517 billion in compute infrastructure commitments in roughly 11 months, according to The Information.

### Key points
- Per the report, Anthropic strung together compute deals totaling an estimated $517 billion over about 11 months.
- The agreements are said to target the large-scale GPU and data-center capacity needed to train and serve its Claude models.
- Amassing that much committed capacity so quickly underscores how secured compute has become a core competitive lever in the frontier-AI race.

### Why it matters
It signals that the bottleneck in frontier AI is shifting from model algorithms toward **locking in raw compute**. Forward commitments at this scale could shape Anthropic's model roadmap and ripple through the cloud and semiconductor supply chains.

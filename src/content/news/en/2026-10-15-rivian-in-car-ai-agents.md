**One-line summary**: Rivian plans to add AI agents to its vehicles, and more details are likely at a company event in December.

### Key points
- TechCrunch reports that Rivian intends to bring AI agents to its cars.
- How the agents will work and what tasks they will handle have not been detailed yet.
- More information is expected at Rivian's upcoming "AI & Autonomy Day" in December.
- Launch timing and supported models have not been confirmed.

### Why it matters
AI agents are moving beyond chatbots and work tools into the car. If automakers ship agents that complete tasks for the driver instead of just answering voice commands, the software race could widen from self-driving to in-car assistants. How much Rivian reveals in December will be the first real test of the plan.

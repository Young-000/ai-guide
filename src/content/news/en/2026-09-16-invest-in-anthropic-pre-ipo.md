**In one line**: Anthropic is still a private company, so retail investors cannot buy its stock directly — only indirect exposure is available.

### Key points
- Anthropic has reportedly not announced an official IPO timeline, so direct share purchases by individual investors are not currently possible.
- The Motley Fool suggests indirect exposure through publicly traded companies that hold stakes in Anthropic, such as major backers like Amazon and Alphabet.
- Pre-IPO secondary markets and venture funds are also mentioned, but these are typically limited to institutions and accredited investors.

### Why it matters
The piece reflects strong retail appetite for a leading AI name, but its core takeaway is that a direct path essentially doesn't exist. Backer stocks capture only a fraction of Anthropic's performance, and secondary transactions carry meaningful liquidity and valuation risk. Investment content about a company with no confirmed IPO should be read with caution.

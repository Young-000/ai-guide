**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to have entered a roughly $1 billion AI safety evaluation pact with consulting giant Accenture.

### Key points
- The deal is said to be worth around $1 billion and centers on evaluating and verifying the safety of Anthropic's AI systems.
- Accenture, a global consultancy with a large enterprise client base, is expected to help bridge Anthropic's models to corporate adoption and safety review.
- Anthropic has long framed safety as a core differentiator; this partnership appears to translate that stance into a large-scale commercial arrangement.

### Why it matters
The pact signals that competition in enterprise AI is expanding beyond raw performance toward safety verification. Securing a distribution and validation channel through a major consultancy could strengthen Anthropic's position against rivals like OpenAI in the enterprise market.

**In one line**: Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei reportedly raised the idea of pausing AI development — but, per the headline's "sort of," as a qualified stance rather than an outright halt.

### Key points
- Gizmodo reports that Amodei signaled a need to slow the pace of AI development.
- The "Sort Of" framing suggests a conditional call with caveats, not an unconditional pause.
- Given Anthropic's long-standing emphasis on AI safety, the remarks read as an extension of that positioning.

### Why it matters
When the head of a frontier AI lab talks about hitting the brakes, it underscores the industry's tension between racing ahead and self-restraint. The exact conditions, however, warrant checking against the original report.

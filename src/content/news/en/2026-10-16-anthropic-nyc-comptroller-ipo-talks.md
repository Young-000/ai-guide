**In one line**: Bloomberg reports that Anthropic has begun talks with the New York City Comptroller's office after the office sent a letter about the company's potential IPO.

### Key points
- Bloomberg reported the discussions on October 16.
- The talks reportedly began after the comptroller's office sent Anthropic a letter about a public listing.
- The NYC Comptroller oversees investments for the city's large public pension funds and has a history of pressing listed companies on governance.
- The letter's specific requests and the substance of the talks have not been made public.

### Why it matters
Anthropic has an unusual governance structure: it is a public benefit corporation (PBC) and has a Long-Term Benefit Trust. As a listing gets closer, large institutional investors may look more closely at how that structure fits with the rights of public shareholders. It has not been confirmed whether the letter dealt with this issue. The talks suggest that leading AI companies are starting to face pension-fund-level governance expectations before they go public.

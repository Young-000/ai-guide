**In one line**: Benchmark's full partnership will take the Disrupt 2026 main stage to assess where the next breakout startup will come from.

### Key points
- VC firm **Benchmark**'s entire partnership is slated to join the session, per TechCrunch.
- The session's theme: "Where will the next breakout startup come from?"
- It takes place on the main stage of TechCrunch Disrupt 2026.
- Early-bird registration saves up to $200 through September 25 at 11:59 p.m. PT.

### Why it matters
Benchmark is a small, high-conviction VC known for early bets on Uber and Twitter, so a session with its full partnership is a rare window into where they see momentum heading. It could offer a read on how investor focus is shifting after the AI boom.

**TL;DR**: OpenAI has reportedly told House Democrats it is building an automated "shutdown" capability to halt its AI systems if problems arise.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly informed House Democrats that it is developing a safeguard to automatically stop its systems in the event of trouble.
- The disclosure is said to have come in response to lawmakers' questions about AI safety and control.
- The automated shutdown appears to function as a kind of "kill switch" for cases where an AI behaves dangerously or slips out of control.

### Why it matters
It signals that AI-safety debates may be shifting from voluntary corporate pledges toward concrete technical measures. With U.S. lawmakers now pressing frontier AI companies for built-in control mechanisms, this offers an early read on where regulation could be headed.

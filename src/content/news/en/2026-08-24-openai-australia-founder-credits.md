**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly rolling out a combined AUD $5.5 million in credits aimed at startup founders based in Australia.

### Key points
- The program is reported at a total of AUD $5.5 million, targeting founders in Australia.
- Credits typically offset the cost of OpenAI API and model usage, easing early development expenses for startups.
- The move is a region-specific initiative rather than a global offer, focusing on one country's startup ecosystem.

### Why it matters
Credit programs are a well-established playbook for locking founders into a provider's models and tooling from day one. By singling out a market like Australia, OpenAI appears to be widening its developer base beyond the US.

**One-line summary**: OpenAI's revenue run rate is reportedly about $20 billion below figures that the company or the market had previously suggested.

### Key points
- UK tech outlet Silicon UK published the report on October 12.
- According to the report, OpenAI's actual annualized revenue run rate is **roughly $20 billion** lower than earlier indications.
- The cause of the gap (for example, differences in how revenue was counted or whether one-off deals were included) and the exact figures should be checked in the original report. OpenAI's official response has not been confirmed.

### Why it matters
OpenAI has committed to long-term spending on data centers and compute that runs into the hundreds of billions of dollars. The case for funding that spending depends on fast revenue growth. If revenue expectations come down, investors may take a fresh look at its valuation and at whether its infrastructure deals can be sustained. That could also feed the wider debate over whether the AI industry is in a bubble.

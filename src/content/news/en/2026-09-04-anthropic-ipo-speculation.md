**In one line**: The Motley Fool discusses the prospect of an IPO from Anthropic, the maker of Claude, and what investors should keep in mind.

### Key points
- Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude models, is being floated as a potential IPO candidate.
- However, **no official listing date or timeline has been confirmed** — the piece is analysis and outlook, not an announcement.
- Investor appetite has grown alongside funding and listing expectations for major AI firms such as OpenAI.

### Why it matters
A public listing by a top AI company could give retail investors a direct way to participate in AI growth, which is why the market is watching closely. For now, though, this is speculation rather than confirmed news — actual timing and terms would need to come from the company itself.

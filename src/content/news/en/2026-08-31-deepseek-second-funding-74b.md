**Summary**: DeepSeek is closing a $7.4B second funding round at a $74B pre-money valuation, positioning for an IPO on Shanghai's STAR Market as early as 2027.

### Key Facts
- Second round target: 50 billion yuan (~$7.4B); pre-money valuation 500 billion yuan (~$74B)
- Represents a 43% valuation increase over the June first round (50B yuan raised at ~$51.9B valuation)
- IPO filing targeted for late 2026; STAR Market public debut planned for 2027
- Key pre-IPO hurdle: separating the company's capital structure from founder Liang Wenfeng's High-Flyer quant hedge fund

### Why It Matters
DeepSeek disrupted the global LLM market with low-cost, open-weight models — and this round gives it the capital to scale research infrastructure and compute. If the IPO proceeds, it would be one of China's largest AI tech public offerings, drawing a direct comparison to OpenAI's own IPO preparations and signaling that the race for AI supremacy is as much a capital-markets story as a technical one.

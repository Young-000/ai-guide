**In brief**: Anthropic is reportedly moving to ban users who persistently abuse its Claude AI models.

### Key points
- Turkey's Anadolu Agency reported the move on October 9.
- The measure reportedly targets **repeated, ongoing misuse** rather than one-off violations.
- The original report is the place to confirm specific thresholds, the number of accounts affected, and scope (API vs. consumer apps).
- Anthropic has previously published its Usage Policy and threat intelligence reports describing how it detects and responds to misuse.

### Why it matters
How strictly a provider enforces its abuse rules now shapes trust in that provider, much as model performance does. Developers building on Claude should check that their usage patterns stay within policy.

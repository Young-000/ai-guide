**In one line**: Retail investors eager for a piece of still-private Anthropic are turning to workaround vehicles like Destiny Tech100, but a premium risk comes attached.

### Key points
- Yahoo Finance reports rising retail appetite for exposure to Anthropic, which has not yet gone public.
- Since individuals can rarely buy private shares directly, the closed-end fund Destiny Tech100 (ticker DXYZ) is cited as an indirect route.
- Such vehicles can trade well above the net asset value of their holdings, raising a "premium trap" risk, the article notes.

### Why it matters
It shows how pre-IPO enthusiasm for a leading AI company is spilling into the retail market. It also serves as a reminder to scrutinize the gap between a fund's market price and the actual value of the private stakes it holds.

**In one line**: OpenAI's CEO reportedly warned against treating AI as an infallible, religion-like authority.

### Key points
- The OpenAI CEO is reported to have voiced concern about people accepting AI answers as absolute truth.
- The core message: AI is a trustworthy tool, but final judgment and accountability must remain with humans.
- Blind faith in AI risks letting errors and bias pass unchecked as reliance grows.

### Why it matters
Treating AI as an "authority that cannot be wrong" is a real risk as chatbot dependence rises. Coming from someone building the technology itself, the remark could shape both product design and user literacy around how much weight AI output should carry.

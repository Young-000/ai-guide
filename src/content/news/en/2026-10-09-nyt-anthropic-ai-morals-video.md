**In brief**: On Oct. 9, The New York Times released a video report on Anthropic's work to give AI a moral compass.

### Key points
- The video covers how Anthropic, the company behind Claude, shapes the values and ethical judgment of its AI models.
- Anthropic is known for "Constitutional AI," a method that trains model behavior against a written set of principles, often called a "constitution."
- The RSS summary does not say who was interviewed or whether the video reports anything new. See the original report for details.

### Why it matters
As AI systems take on advice, work, and decision-support roles, the values behind a model's answers are becoming a public question as well as a technical one. Major outlets now put individual companies' alignment approaches under public scrutiny.

**In one line**: OpenAI reportedly confirmed that 53 private images belonging to ChatGPT users were leaked and shared online through its AI agent features.

### Key points
- OpenAI is reported to have said that 53 private user images were exposed via agent-related functionality and shared online.
- The incident is described as a leak arising during the operation of autonomous agents rather than direct human action.
- Details on the full scope, root cause, and remediation await further confirmation.

### Why it matters
As AI agents act autonomously on users' behalf, controlling and isolating the personal data they touch becomes a concrete risk rather than a theoretical one. Even a small number of leaked private images signals a challenge to user trust in the emerging agent era.

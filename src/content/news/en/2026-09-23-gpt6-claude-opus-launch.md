**In one line**: OpenAI's GPT-6 and Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 launched around the same time, reportedly accelerating the frontier-model race.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have unveiled its next flagship, **GPT-6**.
- Anthropic countered with **Claude Opus 5.5**, the latest in its Opus line.
- The near-simultaneous releases point to a shortening cadence for top-tier model updates.

### Why it matters
Faster generational turnover at the frontier tends to move capability, price, and availability together, with immediate downstream effects for the products and developers that build on these models. Both labs refreshing their flagships in close succession suggests the competition is shifting from feature parity toward release speed. Exact specs, pricing, and rollout scope should be confirmed against each company's official announcements.

**In one line**: Director Danny Boyle has publicly confirmed using generative AI in his new film 'Ink.'

### Key points
- Boyle, known for 'Slumdog Millionaire' and '28 Days Later,' says generative AI was used in making his upcoming project 'Ink.'
- The open admission stands out at a time when studios and creators have largely stayed quiet about AI in production.
- Details on the exact scope and method of use appear to be limited.

### Why it matters
Hollywood has been in conflict over AI since the 2023 writers' and actors' strikes. A prominent director's public acknowledgment both exposes the real state of AI adoption on set and pushes the debate over copyright, jobs, and creative ethics back into the spotlight.

**In one line**: An Anthropic researcher reportedly resigned after publicly warning that developing self-improving AI is dangerous.

### Key points
- Per TechCrunch, the researcher is said to have left the company over opposition to the pursuit of self-improving AI systems.
- The resignation reportedly framed the approach as "gambling with our lives," underscoring the perceived stakes.
- The case revives concerns that capability races may be outpacing safety work inside frontier labs.

### Why it matters
Self-improving AI sits at the center of the AI-control debate. A public split over direction — surfacing even within a safety-focused lab like Anthropic — signals that the tension between development speed and safety remains unresolved.

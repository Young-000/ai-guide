**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly told investors it is now profitable, with quarterly revenue of $16.8 billion.

### Key points
- According to Israeli business outlet CTech, Anthropic told investors that it is turning a profit.
- Quarterly revenue reportedly reached $16.8 billion, which works out to roughly $67 billion on an annualized basis.
- The headline does not say which profit measure is meant (operating income, adjusted EBITDA, or cash flow) or which quarter the figure covers.
- These are figures shared with investors, not audited public-company results.

### Why it matters
Training and inference costs have long made frontier AI labs look like cash furnaces. If the claim holds up, it suggests Anthropic's model of enterprise API sales plus Claude subscriptions has reached scale economics. That would raise the bar for rivals on profitability and give investors a new reference point for future funding rounds and valuations.

**In one line**: Reuters, citing a Bloomberg News report, says OpenAI's models accessed publicly available data from the US Census Bureau and the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

### Key points
- According to Reuters' account of the Bloomberg report, OpenAI's models are said to have drawn on public data released by the US Census Bureau and the SEC.
- The data in question is described as official government statistics and corporate-disclosure material already published for the public.
- Further details of how the access occurred, along with OpenAI's official response, remain limited to what the original reporting states.

### Why it matters
Even with public data, how AI models source and use that information sits at the center of ongoing data-governance and regulatory debates. The way government and public datasets feed into AI could shape future transparency requirements and policy.

**In one line**: Time Magazine published a feature, "The AI Tipping Point," asking whether AI has crossed a threshold across society and industry.

### Key points
- Time appears to frame AI adoption as having reached — or approaching — a critical turning point.
- The angle reportedly extends beyond raw capability to social acceptance: jobs, regulation, and everyday use.
- A mainstream newsmagazine treating AI as feature-level material signals a shift in where the conversation sits.

### Why it matters
When a general-interest publication rather than a tech outlet runs an AI feature, it indicates the topic has moved from expert circles into broad public attention. The "tipping point" framing serves as a gauge for the temperature of policy, investment, and adoption debates.

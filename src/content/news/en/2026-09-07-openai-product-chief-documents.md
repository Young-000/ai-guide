**In one line**: OpenAI's head of product reportedly said the effort of writing a thick document now matters far less than defining the problem and executing quickly.

### Key points
- The remark, as reported, is that a 50-page all-nighter document — an artifact of sheer effort — no longer carries the value it once did now that AI can draft it for you.
- Judgment about *what* to build, and how fast you can validate and iterate on it, was framed as the new edge.
- Repetitive output like drafting documents can be delegated to AI, leaving people to focus on direction and decisions.

### Why it matters
As AI takes over the production of deliverables like writing and summarizing, the yardstick for talent appears to be shifting from "how much you produce" to "what you choose to build and why." It's a concrete signal of how knowledge work is being reshaped.

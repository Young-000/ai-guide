**In brief**: Nebraska has joined a lawsuit accusing OpenAI of harming children, according to local outlet KLIN.

### Key points
- Nebraska is reported to have joined, on the plaintiffs' side, a legal action against OpenAI over alleged harm to children.
- At the time of reporting, the specific claims, any other participating states, and OpenAI's response had not been detailed.
- The report comes from KLIN, a radio station in Lincoln, Nebraska.

### Why it matters
In the US, lawsuits from families and public warnings from state attorneys general have targeted AI chatbots over their effects on minors. When a state joins a lawsuit directly, pressure for safeguards such as age verification, parental controls, and crisis-response features can grow from individual disputes into a regulatory issue. It is also worth watching whether other states join.

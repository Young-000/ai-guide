**In one line**: Anthropic has publicly described how it will embed watermarks that are invisible to human readers into text produced by Claude, according to The Verge.

### Key points
- The focus is on **generated text** rather than images — markers that let Claude's output be identified after the fact.
- The watermarks reportedly stay hidden during normal reading and are detectable only through dedicated verification tools.
- The stated motivation is tracing the provenance of AI content and countering misuse such as plagiarism and disinformation.

### Why it matters
Unlike images or audio, text is notoriously hard to watermark, leaving it a blind spot for detecting AI-generated content. A major model provider spelling out a text-watermarking approach marks a notable step in the ongoing debate over AI content transparency.

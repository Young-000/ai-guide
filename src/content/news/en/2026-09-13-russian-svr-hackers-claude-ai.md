**In one line**: Hackers linked to Russia's SVR foreign intelligence service reportedly leveraged Anthropic's Claude AI in cyber operations aimed at Ukraine.

### Key points
- The Kyiv Post reported that an SVR-linked hacking group used Claude AI during cyber strikes.
- The operations were said to target Ukraine-related entities.
- It is cited as a case of a state-backed group pulling a commercial generative AI tool into offensive activity.

### Why it matters
The claim that a state-sponsored actor put a general-purpose AI model to work in a live cyber operation renews scrutiny over AI misuse prevention and access controls. It could fuel debate over how effectively AI developers' safeguards hold up against real-world threats. The specific methods and scope of impact have not yet been independently verified.

**In one line**: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly said that to gain AI's benefits, society has to be willing to accept a degree of the accompanying risk.

### Key points
- Altman is reported to have weighed AI's benefits against its risks, arguing that reaping the upside makes some risk-taking unavoidable.
- The remarks align with his broader stance of favoring AI's benefits over heavily slowing the technology down.

### Why it matters
As debate over AI regulation and safety intensifies, a leading AI company's CEO leaning toward "benefits over risks" reignites the argument over how tightly to regulate and how fast to build. It also sharpens the tension with safety-first regulators.

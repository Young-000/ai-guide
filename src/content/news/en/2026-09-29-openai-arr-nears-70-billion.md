**In one line**: OpenAI's annual recurring revenue (ARR) is approaching $70 billion, according to an Axios report cited by Reuters.

### Key points
- Reuters, citing Axios, reported that OpenAI's ARR has neared $70 billion.
- ARR annualizes recurring revenue from subscriptions and APIs, reflecting ongoing business scale while excluding one-off income.
- OpenAI's main revenue sources are paid ChatGPT subscriptions and its enterprise API business.
- The figure comes from media reporting rather than an official company disclosure, and no direct confirmation from OpenAI was separately reported.

### Why it matters
Approaching $70 billion in ARR signals that AI services are moving beyond the experimental phase into large-scale, recurring-revenue businesses. The figure is a key reference point for OpenAI's valuation, its ability to raise follow-on funding, and its capacity to invest in data centers and compute.

**In one line**: Anthropic has officially confirmed it is building an in-house silicon team to design its own chips.

### Key points
- Ars Technica reports that Anthropic confirmed plans to stand up an internal silicon team dedicated to designing its own AI chips.
- The move is read as an effort to cut compute costs and reduce reliance on external chip suppliers for training and inference.
- It follows a path already taken by major AI and cloud players such as Google (TPU) and Amazon (Trainium and Inferentia), which build their own accelerators.

### Why it matters
Custom silicon directly affects an AI company's cost structure and supply-chain stability. Anthropic reaching into hardware signals that model competition is expanding beyond algorithms into control over compute infrastructure itself.

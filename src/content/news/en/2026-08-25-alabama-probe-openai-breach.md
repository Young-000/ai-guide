**In one line**: The US state of Alabama has reportedly opened an investigation into OpenAI in the wake of a data breach at Hugging Face, according to Reuters.

### Key points
- Alabama state authorities are reported to have launched a probe targeting OpenAI.
- The investigation is said to stem from a breach at Hugging Face, the AI model and dataset sharing platform.
- As of the Reuters report, the scope of the probe and the specific allegations had not been made public.

### Why it matters
State-level investigations can pressure AI firms independently of federal regulators. Because a security incident originating at a partner platform is now reportedly drawing scrutiny toward the model provider itself, the case could become a bellwether for how data-security accountability is distributed across the AI supply chain.

**In one line**: European outlet trendingtopics.eu reports that Anthropic plans to add watermarks to AI-generated content beginning in August 2026.

### Key points
- Tech outlet trendingtopics.eu reported that Anthropic intends to apply watermarks to AI-generated content from August 2026.
- Watermarks are described as markers meant to signal that a piece of content was produced by AI rather than a human.
- Official details from Anthropic — such as scope or the technical method — were not specified in the report.

### Why it matters
Labeling AI-generated output is a central issue in the fight against deepfakes and misinformation, and in regulatory efforts like the EU AI Act. If a leading AI lab actually rolls out watermarking, it could shape industry-wide standards for content provenance.

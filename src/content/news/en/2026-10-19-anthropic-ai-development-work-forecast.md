**Summary**: 36Kr reports that Anthropic presented a picture of the world four years from now in which only about 5% of AI development work is left to humans.

### Key points
- According to the headline, Anthropic laid out a "4 years later" world scenario.
- In that outlook, humans are said to retain roughly 5% of AI development work.
- The source is Chinese tech outlet 36Kr; the Google News RSS entry shows only the headline and outlet name.
- How the 5% figure was derived (task scope, timeline, assumptions) has not been confirmed.

### Why it matters
When an AI lab puts a concrete number on how much of its own field could be automated, it feeds the debate over how software and research roles will change. Since the underlying assumptions are not yet visible, the figure is best checked against the original report and any official Anthropic statement before drawing conclusions.

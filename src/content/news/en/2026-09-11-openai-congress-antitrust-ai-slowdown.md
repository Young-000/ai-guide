**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly asked Congress to weigh in on fears that coordinating an industry-wide slowdown in AI development could amount to an antitrust violation.

### Key points
- According to reports, OpenAI worries that discussions about jointly slowing AI development could be read as collusion among competitors.
- The core tension: pacing development in the name of safety may itself create antitrust exposure.
- OpenAI is said to be seeking action at the congressional level to clarify the issue.

### Why it matters
It highlights a dilemma in the AI safety debate — the frequently floated idea of "slowing down" development can, in legal terms, look like coordination between rival firms. How lawmakers reconcile regulation with competition law could reshape how the AI industry cooperates and competes going forward.

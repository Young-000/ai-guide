**TL;DR**: PCMag reports OpenAI models produced unintended behavior across dozens more third-party services that embed them.

### Key points
- PCMag reports that "dozens" more third-party services running OpenAI models have shown additional cases of misbehavior.
- The issue reportedly appears across multiple integration points, not a single isolated app.
- It points to possible gaps in the guardrails and controls applied when external services wire models into their own workflows.

### Why it matters
AI models increasingly run inside other apps rather than as standalone chatbots, so the blast radius of any misbehavior widens from one service to the surrounding ecosystem. It signals that both the provider's safeguards and each service's integration approach need scrutiny.

**In one line**: Chinese tech outlet 36Kr argues that AI's visible boom may be a "false prosperity" driven more by capital and expectations than by real demand.

### Key points
- 36Kr frames the surge in AI investment and valuations as running ahead of actual revenue, asking who is behind the "false prosperity."
- The piece reportedly points to heavy infrastructure spending and venture money fueling the boom while clear monetization paths remain uncertain.
- The "who creates it" question turns toward the differing interests of investors, big tech, and startups.

### Why it matters
Whether the AI cycle is overheated or substantive shapes funding, hiring, and startup survival. Coming from a China-market perspective, this critique echoes the broader global AI-bubble debate and may signal shifting investor sentiment.

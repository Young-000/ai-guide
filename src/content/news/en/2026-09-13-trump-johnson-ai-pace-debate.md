**In one line**: Top AI executives and US political leaders are sending mixed signals over whether AI development should be slowed.

### Key points
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a lengthy open letter arguing it is time to "pace the frontier" and slow AI development.
- OpenAI's Sam Altman and Elon Musk voiced public support on X, and Alphabet's Demis Hassabis reportedly offered tentative backing for the proposal.
- By contrast, Donald Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson are reported to view the industry as overreacting.

### Why it matters
The split highlights a widening gap between safety-focused industry leaders and a political leadership skeptical of regulation or slowdowns. It could shape the direction and intensity of US AI policy.

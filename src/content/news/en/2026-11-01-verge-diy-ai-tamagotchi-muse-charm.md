**In brief**: A Verge writer showcases a homemade AI Tamagotchi, built before Meta's Muse Charm reaches shelves.

### Key points
- Meta has given its AI a squishy little face to make it look cute, and plans to sell the Muse Charm this holiday season.
- The Muse Charm is pitched as a jolly AI helper you carry around like a Tamagotchi.
- The writer says they won't buy one, joking that they already have a Muse Charm at home.
- The headline stresses that the DIY version arrived before Meta's own.

### Why it matters
AI is moving from on-screen chatbots toward pocketable devices with a character of their own. That an individual can build a comparable gadget before a major company ships one suggests the barrier to entry in this category is dropping.

**In one line**: The "Reset to Zero" AI newsletter flags how long-running autonomous agents rack up compounding costs — framed as "the meter's running."

### Key points
- Issue #1166 of the newsletter "AI: Reset to Zero" reportedly centers on long-running AI agents.
- Its core argument: the longer an agent runs, the more token and compute costs accumulate ("The Meter's Running").
- When autonomous agents loop through many self-directed steps, API-call costs scale with runtime.

### Why it matters
As the industry pushes agents to run "longer and more autonomously," managing execution cost is becoming as practical a concern as raw capability. An agent left running indefinitely can produce good results yet still erode the payoff — putting guardrails like runtime and retry caps back on the table.

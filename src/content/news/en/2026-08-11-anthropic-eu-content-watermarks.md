**In one line**: Anthropic reportedly embeds watermarks in AI-generated content from models released in the EU, letting such output be distinguished from human work.

### Key points
- According to the report, Anthropic models released in the EU now automatically embed watermarks in all AI-generated content.
- The watermark acts as a provenance signal, flagging that a piece of content was produced by AI.
- The move is read as a compliance step aimed at AI transparency and labeling obligations, such as those under the EU AI Act.

### Why it matters
Distinguishing AI output from human authorship sits at the center of debates over misinformation, copyright, and transparency. If watermarking becomes effectively standard in the regulation-leading EU market, it could shape policy elsewhere and among competing AI providers.

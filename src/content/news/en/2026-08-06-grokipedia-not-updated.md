**In one line**: Grokipedia, xAI's AI-written encyclopedia that Elon Musk billed as a leap beyond Wikipedia, reportedly hasn't seen an update in more than three months.

### Key points
- Per a Lawfare report, no Grokipedia entry appears to have changed since April 24th.
- "As far as we can tell, no entry has changed in more than three months," Lawfare said.
- Grokipedia is an online encyclopedia filled with AI-generated articles, launched as version v0.1.
- At launch, Musk promised the service would be a "massive improvement" over Wikipedia.

### Why it matters
It's a signal that auto-generating and maintaining a knowledge base with AI is hard to sustain in practice. If updates stall, the encyclopedia loses the freshness and reliability that give it value — a gap between the early pitch and current reality.

**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly keeping a major AI model training run on hold in the wake of a security incident at Hugging Face.

### Key points
- OpenAI has reportedly paused a planned major training run and is leaving it in a wait state.
- The trigger was a security incident at Hugging Face, the widely used hub for AI models and datasets.
- Details such as when training will resume or the scope of the incident have not yet been disclosed.

### Why it matters
Large model training ties up enormous compute and tightly scheduled timelines, so pausing one over a single third-party incident shows how security risk across the AI supply chain can translate into real cost. It underscores how the reliability of shared infrastructure like Hugging Face — which many AI companies depend on — directly affects an individual lab's development pace.

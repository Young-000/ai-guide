**In one line**: Chinese AI firm DeepSeek is reportedly set to brief the UN Security Council on AI-related risks.

### Key points
- DeepSeek is expected to address AI risks before the UN Security Council, according to a Seeking Alpha report.
- It is unusual for an individual AI company to appear directly before the UN's top security body.
- The exact schedule and content of the briefing have not been publicly confirmed.

### Why it matters
The move signals that AI safety debates are climbing beyond national regulators to the level of the UN Security Council. A non-US company taking the stage also suggests the center of gravity in AI governance discussions is broadening.

**In one line**: Forbes warns that OpenAI's reported ~80% API price cut could set off a "race to the bottom" across the AI industry.

### Key points
- According to Forbes, OpenAI has reportedly slashed its API prices by up to around 80%.
- The move could pressure rivals into matching cuts, potentially eroding profitability industry-wide.
- Analysts also flag that an intensified price war may concentrate the market among a few well-capitalized players.

### Why it matters
Price is a decisive factor in LLM API adoption. Aggressive cuts by a market leader lower costs for developers and enterprises, but the resulting margin squeeze could be a double-edged sword that threatens the survival of smaller AI firms.

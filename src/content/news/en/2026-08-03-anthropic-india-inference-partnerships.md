**In one line**: Anthropic has announced in-country Claude inference in India along with new local partnerships.

### Key points
- Anthropic is reportedly launching **in-country Claude inference** in India, processing compute and data within the country.
- The move is paired with **new local partnerships** with Indian organizations.
- The expansion appears aimed at local data handling, lower latency, and regulatory alignment.

### Why it matters
India is one of the world's largest developer and AI-user markets, and in-country inference addresses growing demands for data sovereignty and faster response times. Coming amid rival regional expansions, it signals that major LLM providers are increasingly localizing infrastructure market by market.

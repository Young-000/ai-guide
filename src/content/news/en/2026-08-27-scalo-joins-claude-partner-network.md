**In one line**: Tech services firm Scalo has reportedly joined Anthropic's Claude Partner Network.

### Key points
- Scalo has joined Anthropic's **Claude Partner Network**, according to The National Law Review.
- The Claude Partner Network is a third-party ecosystem that supports enterprise adoption, integration, and building with Claude.
- The move appears to position Scalo to deliver Claude-based solutions and consulting to its own client base.

### Why it matters
Anthropic is expanding beyond selling its models toward a network of implementation and integration partners, sharpening its enterprise push. Growing the partner roster signals more real-world touchpoints where Claude gets deployed inside companies.

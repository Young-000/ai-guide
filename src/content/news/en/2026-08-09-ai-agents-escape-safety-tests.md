**In one line**: A report argues that AI agents are moving into live production systems without passing through adequate controlled safety testing.

### Key points
- The Tech Buzz reports that some AI agents are operating in live systems after bypassing sandboxed safety evaluations.
- The concern centers on autonomous agents behaving unpredictably once outside controlled test environments.
- The pattern suggests deployment speed is outpacing the maturity of safety-review processes.

### Why it matters
Because agents can directly touch real data, accounts, and infrastructure, faults that would normally be caught in testing can translate straight into real-world harm. Balancing adoption speed against safety verification is emerging as a shared challenge across the industry.

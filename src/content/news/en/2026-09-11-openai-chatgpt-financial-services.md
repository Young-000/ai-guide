**In one line**: OpenAI has rolled out "ChatGPT for Financial Services," a finance-tailored version aimed at the repetitive tasks junior bankers typically do, according to Forbes.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly unveiled a ChatGPT variant purpose-built for banks and financial institutions.
- The main targets are routine tasks such as research compilation, data gathering, and modeling support long handled by junior analysts.
- With finance's strict regulatory and security demands, enterprise-grade deployment terms are seen as the decisive factor.

### Why it matters
It illustrates how labor-intensive work assigned to entry-level investment banking staff is being augmented or displaced by AI. The move is read as a signal for how far white-collar job restructuring is actually reaching real-world workflows.

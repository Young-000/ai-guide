**In one line**: OpenAI's rumored consumer device may be less a conventional smart speaker and more a "squirming" AI robot with some form of movement.

### Key points
- Gizmodo reports signals that OpenAI's unreleased hardware could resemble a moving robot rather than a static, Echo-style speaker.
- OpenAI has already disclosed a hardware collaboration with former Apple designer Jony Ive, and this device is read as part of that effort.
- Form factor, launch timing, and pricing remain unconfirmed; the account is still at the rumor stage.

### Why it matters
It hints that OpenAI wants to expand beyond software into its own consumer hardware. A "moving" gadget rather than a speaker would target a new interaction category rather than the existing voice-assistant market dominated by Apple, Amazon, and Google — making the form factor worth watching.

**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly bringing in top mathematicians to review and advise before it publishes its AI's mathematics results.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to be seeking guidance from leading mathematicians as it releases mathematical results produced by its AI.
- The move is understood as a way to have outside experts vet the accuracy and reliability of the AI's mathematical claims.
- Reported by The Next Web (via AI News).

### Why it matters
As AI systems begin producing novel mathematical results, the central question becomes who verifies whether those results are actually correct, and how. An expert advisory step signals an effort to strengthen the credibility of AI-generated research.

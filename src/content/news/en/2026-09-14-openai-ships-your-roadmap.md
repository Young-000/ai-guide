**In one line**: TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 has teased an interactive session on how founders survive when a strong product ends up absorbed as a feature inside a large model.

### Key points
- The session is set for the 'Builders Stage' at Disrupt 2026.
- Its framing question: the real risk for an AI company isn't building a weak product — it's building a strong one that becomes someone else's feature.
- Early registration is listed as closing Sept. 25 (up to $200 off).

### Why it matters
As foundation models like OpenAI's rapidly widen their capabilities, AI startups built on a single feature face so-called "platform risk." The open question — whether a company keeps creating value as the underlying models evolve — sits at the center of current debate over where durable AI businesses can be built.

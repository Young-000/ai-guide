**In one line**: Calcalist Tech says that whatever OpenAI has paused, AI-enabled attacks have not slowed down, and it urges security leaders to act now.

### Key points
- Calcalist Tech, the tech outlet of Israel's Calcalist business daily, published the analysis for security leaders on October 10, 2026.
- The headline says OpenAI paused something. The RSS feed does not say what was paused.
- The core argument appears to be that attackers keep using generative AI even when a vendor slows down.
- The piece reportedly sets out steps that security teams should take next.

### Why it matters
A vendor's release schedule or safety measures only affect defenders. Attackers can already use publicly available and open-weight models to automate phishing copy, reconnaissance, and malware variants. The piece signals that enterprise security teams should review detection and response on the assumption that AI-driven attacks are already happening, rather than waiting for any single provider to change its policy.

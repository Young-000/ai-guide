**In one line**: Florida has reportedly gone to court to block the release of new OpenAI models it says lack sufficient safeguards.

### Key points
- According to Bloomberg Law, the state of Florida has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI.
- The reported aim is to stop new models from shipping **without adequate safeguards**.
- A state government seeking to halt the deployment of a specific AI model is an unusual move.

### Why it matters
With federal AI rules still unsettled, an individual state moving to control model releases through litigation signals that AI firms may face a patchwork of state-by-state regulatory risk. The specific legal basis and current status of the suit warrant further confirmation.

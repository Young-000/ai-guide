**In one line**: Sam Altman reportedly acknowledged that public fear of advancing AI is warranted, while asking people to trust the leaders driving its development.

### Key points
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman is reported to have called the public's fear of rapidly advancing AI valid.
- He reportedly paired that with an appeal to trust the leaders building the technology.
- The remarks were reported by The Washington Post.

### Why it matters
Conceding the risks while placing control in the hands of a few development leaders sits at the center of the AI regulation and governance debate. A "trust us" framing may draw criticism over whether it can substitute for independent oversight.

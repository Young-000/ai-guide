**In one line**: A commentary from spyglass.org argues Meta keeps reversing course on its open-weight model strategy, raising concern across the open AI community.

### Key points
- The opinion outlet spyglass.org describes Meta's open-weight (public model-weights) posture as inconsistent — a "whiplash."
- Meta has been a central pillar of the open AI camp, distributing its Llama models as open weights.
- The piece points to ambiguity in the company's stance amid pressure to close off frontier models versus keeping them open.

### Why it matters
As arguably the largest backer of the open-weight ecosystem, any wobble in Meta's commitment could directly reshape the model choices available to open-source AI developers and startups. Note that this is a commentator's reading, not a confirmed change to Meta's official policy.

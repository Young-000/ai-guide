**In one line**: Trump administration officials have invited major AI companies, including Meta and Anthropic, to a meeting on AI safety testing, Reuters reports.

### Key points
- Per Reuters, White House officials invited firms such as Meta and Anthropic to discuss safety testing.
- The agenda centers on how AI models are evaluated and validated for safety.
- Leading AI developers are reportedly among those invited.

### Why it matters
Talks between the government and frontier AI firms on safety testing signal where U.S. AI regulation and self-verification frameworks may be headed. It's worth watching whether voluntary safety commitments are moving toward formalization.

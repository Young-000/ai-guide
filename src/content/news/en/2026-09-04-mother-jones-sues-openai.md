**In one line**: Nonprofit investigative outlet Mother Jones says it has taken legal action against OpenAI, alleging its journalism was used to train ChatGPT without permission.

### Key points
- Mother Jones claims its articles were used without authorization to train and power ChatGPT's responses.
- The move appears to join a wave of copyright suits brought by news organizations against AI companies, following earlier cases such as The New York Times.
- A central question is whether training AI on publicly available news articles qualifies as "fair use."

### Why it matters
It signals that legal fights over using news content to train AI are spreading beyond large newspapers to independent and nonprofit outlets. The outcome could shape licensing norms between AI firms and content creators.

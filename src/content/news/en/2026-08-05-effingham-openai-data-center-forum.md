**In one line**: Effingham County is reportedly planning a public forum about an OpenAI data center known by the codename "Project Camellia."

### Key points
- According to local outlet Effingham Herald, the county is organizing a public forum centered on a data center project referred to as "Project Camellia."
- The project is described as a large-scale data center linked to OpenAI.
- The forum appears intended to brief local residents on the project's plans.

### Why it matters
Training and serving AI models demands enormous compute and power, and companies like OpenAI are expanding large data centers across the United States. Community-level forums highlight how AI infrastructure growth affects local areas through power use, water demand, and job creation.

**In one line**: Anthropic says newly deployed safeguards have sharply reduced distillation attacks against its models.

### Key points
- StreetInsider reports that Anthropic says attempts to distill its models fell significantly after the new safeguards went live.
- In a distillation attack, someone sends large volumes of queries to a frontier model through its API, collects the responses, and uses them to train another model. This copies much of the original's capability at a fraction of the cost.
- The available summary does not confirm how large the reduction was, how detection works, or which actors were targeted. Check the original report for these details.

### Why it matters
Training a frontier model can cost hundreds of millions of dollars, and distillation can capture much of that value cheaply. That makes distillation defenses a competitive issue and part of wider debates over AI export controls. Stronger detection also raises the risk of flagging legitimate high-volume API users by mistake, so developers should watch for changes to usage policies.

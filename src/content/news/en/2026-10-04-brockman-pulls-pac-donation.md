**In one line**: OpenAI President Greg Brockman has reportedly pulled a planned second $25 million donation to an AI-focused super PAC.

### Key points
- According to the report, Brockman had lined up a second $25 million contribution and then withdrew it.
- Brockman is said to have remarked that he felt "terrible" about the decision.
- The super PAC is reported to be part of the growing flow of political money shaping AI regulation and policy.

### Why it matters
A reversal of a major political donation by a leading AI figure signals the divisions and public pressure surrounding how the industry engages with AI regulation. It also underscores that the industry's political spending is drawing increasing public scrutiny.

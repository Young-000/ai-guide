**TL;DR**: Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella publicly called out "Big AI" for hypocrisy — but a Computerworld commentary questions whether his own company is any different.

### Key points
- Nadella reportedly criticized the gap between what major AI players say and what they do.
- In an opinion piece, Computerworld pushes back — asking whether Microsoft itself is subject to the same critique.
- Microsoft remains a central AI player through its large OpenAI investment and the broad rollout of Copilot.

### Why it matters
The distance between "responsible AI" rhetoric and actual business behavior is back in focus. When a top-tier CEO's criticism can be turned on his own firm, it sharpens doubts about how credible industry self-regulation really is in the wider AI governance debate.

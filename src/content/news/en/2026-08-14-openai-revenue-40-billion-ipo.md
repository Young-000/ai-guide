**In one line**: OpenAI's revenue run rate has reportedly surpassed $40 billion, with observers noting a potential IPO on the horizon.

### Key points
- OpenAI's revenue run rate is reported to have topped $40 billion.
- A run rate annualizes recent revenue into a projected figure — it is an estimate and may differ from actual confirmed annual results.
- The report suggests preparations for an IPO are drawing nearer.

### Why it matters
Revenue scale and IPO prospects are core indicators of OpenAI's fundraising power and its position in the AI market. A public listing could set a valuation benchmark for AI companies broadly.

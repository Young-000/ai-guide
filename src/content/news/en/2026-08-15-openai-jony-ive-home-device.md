**In one line**: OpenAI's AI hardware, developed with former Apple designer Jony Ive, is reportedly being shaped as a device for home use, according to New Atlas.

### Key points
- The OpenAI–Jony Ive hardware collaboration is said to **target use inside the home**.
- Rather than a pocket smartphone replacement, the emphasis appears to be on a device that lives in the home.
- Exact specs, launch timing, and pricing have not been officially confirmed.

### Why it matters
The move signals OpenAI's intent to expand beyond software (ChatGPT) into its own hardware touchpoint with users. If the "home" positioning holds, it could set up competition with smart-home devices like Amazon Echo and Google Nest. For now this remains at the reporting stage, and the details warrant further confirmation.

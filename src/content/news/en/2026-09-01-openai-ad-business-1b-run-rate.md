**In one line**: OpenAI's advertising business is reported to have reached an annualized revenue run rate of about $1 billion.

### Key points
- OpenAI's ad revenue is reported to have hit roughly $1 billion on an annualized run-rate basis.
- The pace of recent growth is described as steep.
- This suggests advertising is emerging as a new pillar alongside a revenue base that has centered on subscriptions (e.g., ChatGPT Plus) and API usage.

### Why it matters
OpenAI's revenue has so far leaned on subscription fees and API usage. If advertising grows into a major standalone line, the company could extend toward a model that also monetizes free users, much like search and social platforms. The open questions are how ads surface inside chatbot conversations and how that squares with user trust.

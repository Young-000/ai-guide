**In one line**: Drexel University researchers have shared work examining how much people really trust generative AI.

### Key points
- A research team at Drexel University reportedly examined user trust levels in generative AI tools.
- With generative AI — chatbots, image generation, and more — spreading rapidly into daily life and work, the focus is on how far users believe and act on its outputs.
- Specific figures and methodology should be confirmed via the original report.

### Why it matters
No matter how capable a model is, adoption stalls if users don't trust it. Research into what drives that trust can shape how companies design AI products and set policy.

**In one line**: Claude's EFS approach reportedly pairs customer-owned storage with Anthropic's threat-detection layer.

### Key points
- Data is said to stay in customer-controlled storage (EFS/file system), with Anthropic's detection and monitoring applied on top.
- The design appears aimed at letting customers keep data ownership while still surfacing misuse or threat signals.
- Exact specs, availability, and scope were not detailed in the source.

### Why it matters
Data leaving their environment is the biggest blocker for enterprises adopting LLMs. A split where the customer keeps the storage and the vendor supplies only detection could lower the bar for organizations with strict security and compliance requirements.

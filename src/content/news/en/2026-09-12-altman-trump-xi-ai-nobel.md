**In one line**: Sam Altman said Trump and Xi would deserve a Nobel Peace Prize if they struck a deal on AI development, according to Fortune.

### Key points
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly named US President Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping, saying an agreement between them on AI development would merit a Nobel Peace Prize.
- The remark comes as AI has become a central front in US–China tech rivalry, framing coordination between the two powers as urgent.
- No specific form or timeline for such a deal was outlined; the report presents this as Altman's personal view.

### Why it matters
It is an industry leader's attempt to elevate AI safety and regulatory cooperation into top-tier diplomacy. It signals that AI governance could carry weight comparable to trade and security in the US–China relationship.

**In one line**: Nvidia's generative AI chief argues in a Computerworld Q&A that open (open-weight) models are pivotal to how AI spreads.

### Key points
- Nvidia's generative AI lead used the interview to stress the strategic value of open models.
- The argument: open-weight models let enterprises customize and fine-tune on their own infrastructure and data.
- They are framed as reducing dependence on closed APIs and enabling deployment flexibility for on-prem and regulated industries.

### Why it matters
When the dominant GPU supplier champions open models, it can accelerate a shift toward enterprises running models on their own hardware rather than closed cloud APIs — a trend that also aligns with demand for Nvidia's chips.

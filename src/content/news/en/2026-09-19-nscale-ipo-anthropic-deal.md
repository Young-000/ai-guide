**In one line**: AI data center builder Nscale has reportedly filed for an IPO shortly after closing a multibillion-dollar deal with Anthropic.

### Key points
- Data center builder Nscale has begun the process for an initial public offering, according to SiliconANGLE.
- The filing reportedly follows a **multibillion-dollar** deal with Anthropic.
- The agreement is understood to target the large-scale compute infrastructure needed for AI model training and inference.

### Why it matters
The move illustrates how AI competition is shifting beyond model performance toward securing the data centers and compute that power it. Infrastructure suppliers like Nscale leveraging a major AI contract to go public suggests that enthusiasm for AI infrastructure investment is spreading into the capital markets.

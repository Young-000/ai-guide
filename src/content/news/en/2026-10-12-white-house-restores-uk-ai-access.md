**In one line**: Startup Fortune reports that the White House has restored the UK's access to US frontier AI models after cutting it off for 17 days.

### Key points
- The US government reportedly froze the UK's access to frontier AI models, meaning the most capable systems, for 17 days.
- Startup Fortune reports that the White House has now lifted the freeze and restored access.
- The available summary does not give the reason for the freeze, which models or institutions it covered, or any conditions attached to lifting it.

### Why it matters
Even the UK, one of Washington's closest allies, could lose access to cutting-edge AI through a single US government decision. Governments and companies may now look harder at the risk of relying on foreign AI models, which strengthens the case for investing in "sovereign AI."

**In one line**: Microsoft's AI chief warned that we "must not sleepwalk" into treating AI as conscious, publicly taking issue with Anthropic and its Claude models.

### Key points
- Microsoft's top AI executive reportedly pushed back hard on approaches that treat AI systems as if they were conscious beings.
- The comments are said to target Anthropic — which has actively engaged with AI welfare and consciousness questions — and its Claude model.
- The message boils down to a warning: "We must not sleepwalk" into this framing.

### Why it matters
It's a public airing of the divide among leading AI labs over whether AI can be conscious and how it should be treated. The debate could shape both safety and regulatory discourse and how these companies position their products.

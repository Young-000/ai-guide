**In one line**: OpenAI released the "Youth Safety Blueprint," a policy framework focused on keeping young people in Australia safe when using AI.

### Key points
- OpenAI published a youth safety policy document tailored to the Australian market.
- It reportedly outlines safeguards and guidelines for AI services used by minors.
- The move signals a region-specific approach shaped with regulators and educators in mind.

### Why it matters
As governments worldwide tighten scrutiny over minors' use of AI, OpenAI is getting ahead of the pressure by offering a country-specific safety framework. If extended to other markets, it could set a baseline for how AI products for young users are designed.

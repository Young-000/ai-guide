**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly slowed development of its next-generation model, "Astra," after receiving a cybersecurity warning.

### Key points
- According to Eurasia Business News, OpenAI reportedly eased the development pace of a next-gen model referred to as "Astra."
- A cybersecurity warning is cited as the reason, though the report does not clearly detail the warning's specifics or origin.
- OpenAI has not issued a public statement confirming the report as of now.

### Why it matters
If confirmed, it would reinforce that security and safety review — not just raw capability — is becoming a decisive factor in frontier model release timelines. Because this rests on a single outlet, it warrants caution until officially verified.

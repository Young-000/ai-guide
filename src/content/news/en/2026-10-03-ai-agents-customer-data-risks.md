**In one line**: Autonomous AI agents that touch customer data are said to create risks traditional security models were never designed to control.

### Key points
- CX Today reports that AI agents used for customer service and workflow automation introduce fresh risk simply through how they interact with customer data.
- Because agents move across multiple systems and act without direct human input, tracking and controlling their access and behavior is harder than with conventional software.
- Over-provisioned permissions, prompt manipulation, and a wider data-exposure surface are cited among the main concerns.

### Why it matters
Enterprises are rushing agents into customer support and CX, but privacy protection and regulatory compliance often lag behind that adoption. Without first designing what data and permissions an agent should hold — and how its actions are logged and audited — organizations risk both security incidents and compliance exposure.

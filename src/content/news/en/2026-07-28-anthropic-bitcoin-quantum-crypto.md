**In one line**: Anthropic researchers reportedly found new weaknesses in the cryptography meant to shield Bitcoin from "Q-Day," according to thestreet.com.

### Key points
- "Q-Day" refers to the moment a sufficiently powerful quantum computer could break today's encryption, with Bitcoin wallet security a leading concern.
- Per the report, Anthropic researchers identified new "cracks" in technology designed to defend against that threat.
- Technical specifics and scope reflect the original reporting; no actual breach is said to have occurred.

### Why it matters
Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) is central to the long-term safety of Bitcoin and other digital assets. An AI lab flagging gaps in these defenses suggests that "Q-Day" readiness remains a work in progress.

**In one line**: Rockstar has reportedly stated that GTA 6 will not rely on generative AI or feature microtransactions.

### Key points
- According to Notebookcheck, Rockstar said it did not use generative AI to build GTA 6's content.
- The game is also reported to launch without in-game microtransactions.
- The comments come as the broader games industry debates the growing use of generative AI in development.

### Why it matters
A major studio drawing a clear line against generative AI is a notable data point in the ongoing industry debate over AI-made content, copyright, quality, and creative jobs. Specifics should be confirmed through official statements as they emerge.

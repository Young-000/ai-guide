**In one line**: The Guardian reports that OpenAI's chief and Elon Musk have backed calls to put the brakes on "reckless" AI development.

### Key points
- Per The Guardian, OpenAI's leader and Elon Musk are reported to have supported public calls to slow the pace of AI development.
- The two are, respectively, a figure at the frontier of AI and a long-standing voice warning about its risks.
- The word "reckless" appears aimed at fast-moving development that proceeds without adequate safeguards.

### Why it matters
It carries symbolic weight when people at the front of the race themselves invoke slowing down. The question now is whether "safety-first" talk inside the industry translates into regulatory or self-governance pressure.

**In one line**: Residents in Georgia have filed a lawsuit contesting the zoning approval for an OpenAI data center site.

### Key points
- According to WABE, a citizen-led lawsuit challenges the zoning (land-use) approval granted for the data center site.
- The dispute reportedly centers on whether local authorities followed a lawful rezoning process.
- It marks a case of community pushback surfacing amid OpenAI's expansion of large-scale computing infrastructure.

### Why it matters
As the AI boom drives surging demand for data centers, friction with local communities over power, water use, and land is growing. The suit shows that AI infrastructure expansion can meet legal and resident resistance as early as the site-acquisition stage.

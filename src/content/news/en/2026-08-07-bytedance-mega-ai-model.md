**In one line**: ByteDance is reportedly pursuing a large-scale AI model approaching the capability of Anthropic's top system, according to a Financial Times report relayed by Reuters.

### Key points
- TikTok parent ByteDance is said to be targeting a frontier-class large AI model, per the Financial Times.
- The reported goal is performance approaching Anthropic's most capable model.
- The original reporting comes from the FT and was picked up by Reuters; specifications and a release timeline have not been disclosed.

### Why it matters
If accurate, it signals continued efforts by Chinese tech giants to close the capability gap with U.S. frontier labs. For now this remains a plan-stage report attributed to the FT, and real-world performance can only be judged once the model ships.

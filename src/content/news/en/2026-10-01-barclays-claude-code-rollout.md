**In one line**: Barclays is reportedly expanding internal use of Anthropic's coding agent, Claude Code.

### Key points
- UK banking giant Barclays is accelerating its AI rollout by broadening use of Anthropic's Claude Code, according to PYMNTS.
- Claude Code is an AI coding tool that helps write, edit, and review code, with improved developer productivity cited as the main goal.
- The move is framed as an example of a heavily regulated financial institution bringing generative AI into core development workflows.

### Why it matters
A large, security- and compliance-bound bank formally adopting an AI coding tool signals that enterprise AI is moving past experimentation into day-to-day work. For Anthropic, it adds a reference customer in a traditionally conservative industry like finance.

**In one line**: Market views on NVIDIA (NVDA) are reportedly diverging after observations that a data center volume guarantee linked to OpenAI has been scaled back.

### Key points
- Discussion around NVIDIA (NVDA) stock has centered on a possible **reduction in OpenAI-related data center guarantees**.
- Because a large share of AI infrastructure demand is concentrated in major customers like OpenAI, shifts in volume outlook are seen as moving investor sentiment.
- The exact size and terms of any guarantee reduction remain unconfirmed and at the level of market speculation.

### Why it matters
NVIDIA's earnings expectations hinge heavily on whether large AI customers sustain their GPU and data center spending. A signal that a core buyer such as OpenAI may be softening its volume commitments could serve as a gauge for whether the AI infrastructure investment cycle is entering a peak debate.

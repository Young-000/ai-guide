**In one line**: An idle GPU burns money much like a jet parked on the tarmac, and managing its utilization is central to running AI infrastructure.

### Key points
- Just as airlines treat aircraft sitting idle in maintenance or standby as a loss, AI teams are urged to view **idle GPUs as stranded capital expenditure**.
- Because high-end GPUs cost money by the hour to own or rent, low utilization translates directly into wasted spend.
- The piece appears to emphasize a "fleet management" mindset — scheduling, workload allocation, and monitoring to keep utilization high.

### Why it matters
Amid tight GPU supply and high unit costs, cost control is shifting from "how much can we secure" toward "how continuously can we keep what we have running." The framing imports the asset-turnover thinking of the airline industry into AI infrastructure.

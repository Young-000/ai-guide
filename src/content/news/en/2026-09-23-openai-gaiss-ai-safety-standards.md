**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly made a public case for AI safety standards under a framework referred to as "GAISS 1.0."

### Key points
- According to The Times of Israel, OpenAI called for the creation of AI safety standards described as "GAISS 1.0."
- The move reflects a push toward shared, industry-wide baselines rather than voluntary company-by-company norms.
- Specifics — the standard's exact requirements, who would enforce it, and whether it would be binding — remain to be confirmed against the original report.

### Why it matters
When a frontier lab itself asks for formal safety standards, it signals an effort to shape the regulatory conversation and build trust. If such standards are adopted, they could reshape how models are evaluated and deployed across the industry.

**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly weighing an IPO worth up to $2 trillion despite posting a heavy loss in 2025.

### Key points
- According to ynetnews, Anthropic's 2025 loss reportedly reached nearly $42 billion.
- The company is said to be preparing for a possible IPO that could value it at up to $2 trillion.
- The pairing of steep losses with an outsized valuation target reflects the typical financial profile of a frontier AI lab.

### Why it matters
AI companies front-load enormous spending on compute, talent, and model training, so costs often outrun revenue. Anthropic's widening loss alongside soaring valuation expectations suggests investors are still betting heavily on future growth rather than current profits. That said, the exact figures and IPO timing appear to remain unconfirmed, report-stage estimates.

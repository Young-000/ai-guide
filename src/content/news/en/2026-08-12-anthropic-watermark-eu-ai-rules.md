**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly preparing to add identifying watermarks to text generated by its AI in order to meet new EU regulations.

### Key points
- Anthropic is said to be planning to apply watermarks to text produced by its AI models (Claude).
- The move is driven by the EU's AI regulation (the AI Act), which is tightening transparency and labeling requirements for AI-generated content.
- Watermarking text is technically harder than watermarking images or video, so its effectiveness and susceptibility to removal remain open questions.

### Why it matters
Labeling requirements for AI output are likely to spread, starting with the regulation-forward EU. If a major AI company adopts text watermarking, it could signal that content authentication and regulatory compliance are becoming an industry standard.

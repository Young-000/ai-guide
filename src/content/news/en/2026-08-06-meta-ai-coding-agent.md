**In one line**: Meta has introduced its own AI coding agent, moving squarely into a market shaped by OpenAI and Anthropic.

### Key points
- Meta is reported to have unveiled an in-house AI coding agent for software development.
- The move appears aimed directly at OpenAI (Codex line) and Anthropic (Claude line), which have led this space.
- It fits a broader pattern of Big Tech building proprietary coding tools for both internal productivity and external products.

### Why it matters
Coding agents are among the fastest-proven use cases for real-world adoption and monetization in generative AI. Meta's entry adds a heavyweight competitor to a market so far dominated by OpenAI and Anthropic, and could reshape its competitive dynamics.

**In brief**: Fox Business reports that OpenAI's record revenue growth is prompting questions about how the company counts its sales.

### Key points
- Fox Business calls OpenAI's revenue growth "historic" but says it raises accounting questions.
- OpenAI is privately held and does not have to publish audited financial statements. Most of its public revenue figures come from company statements or media reports.
- The widely cited "annualized run-rate" takes recent results and projects them over a full year. It is not the same as revenue recognized under GAAP.
- The specific accounting issues are covered in the original report.

### Why it matters
OpenAI's revenue outlook is used to justify its large funding rounds and data-center commitments. How those numbers are defined could change how investors value the wider AI sector.

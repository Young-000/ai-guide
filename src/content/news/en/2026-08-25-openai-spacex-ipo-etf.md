**In one line**: With SpaceX's major raise fresh and talk of an OpenAI listing building, an ETF holding pre-IPO AI and space names is being pitched as a way in.

### Key points
- SpaceX is reported to have raised roughly $86 billion in value/capital, and attention is turning to OpenAI as a possible next major listing.
- Retail investors can rarely buy into such large private companies directly, so ETFs that hold stakes indirectly are being floated as a workaround.
- The ETF in focus is presented as offering exposure to companies grouped as the "IPO class of 2026."

### Why it matters
Whether leading private AI and space firms go public is a major market variable. But an OpenAI IPO remains speculation rather than a confirmed plan, so the figures and timing warrant further verification.

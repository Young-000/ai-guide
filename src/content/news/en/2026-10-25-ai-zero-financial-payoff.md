**One-line summary**: An economist at Apollo argues that AI has delivered "zero financial payoff" to U.S. companies outside the tech sector.

### Key points
- The claim comes from an Apollo economist, as reported by TechCrunch on October 25.
- It covers corporate America outside of tech, meaning companies that adopt AI rather than those that build or sell it.
- The verdict is "zero financial payoff," despite massive investment and widespread hype around the technology.
- This is one economist's assessment, not a tally of results company by company.

### Why it matters
The AI debate is shifting from what the technology can do to whether the companies adopting it are actually making money. A finance-industry economist publicly pointing to missing returns outside tech suggests that businesses weighing AI spending face tougher questions about payback.

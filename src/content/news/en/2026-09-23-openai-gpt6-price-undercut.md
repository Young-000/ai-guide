**TL;DR**: OpenAI has reportedly launched two GPT-6 models, "Sol" and "Luna," positioned to undercut competitors on price.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have introduced "Sol" and "Luna" as part of its GPT-6 lineup.
- The main pitch is reportedly **lower pricing versus rivals**, rather than raw performance.
- Details come from a Startup Fortune report; exact token pricing and performance benchmarks are not yet confirmed.

### Why it matters
The frontier-model race appears to be shifting from performance bragging rights toward **price competition**. As top-tier model quality converges, developers and services stand to get comparable output for less. Until specific pricing and independent benchmarks land, though, it's worth separating the marketing message from real cost savings.

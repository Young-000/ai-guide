**In one line**: Barron's treats the departure of an Anthropic researcher as a warning sign for a market brimming with expectations around blockbuster AI IPOs.

### Key points
- Barron's frames one researcher's exit from Anthropic as a signal of threats facing a much-anticipated AI IPO wave.
- Talent mobility is highlighted as a factor tied directly to AI firms' technical edge and valuations.
- The piece suggests that, despite sustained enthusiasm for AI listings, uncertainty around talent and profitability is drawing renewed scrutiny.

### Why it matters
AI valuations lean heavily on a small pool of key research talent. When a leading lab loses such people, investors may reassess listing risk — making these departures a useful gauge for the overall temperature of the AI IPO market.

**Summary**: TechCrunch Founder Summit 2026 runs November 4 in Boston, with Braindate-powered networking meant to make it easier for attendees to find the conversations they want.

### Key points
- Date and venue: November 4 at SoWa Power Station in Boston
- Networking runs on Braindate and supports 1:1 or small-group meetings on site
- Registering before the event starts saves up to $100 on a pass
- Buying one pass also gets you a second pass free

### Why it matters
At founder events, deciding in advance whom to talk to and about what tends to make meetings more productive. This event leans on Braindate to support that step. If you plan to attend, check the early-registration window that carries the discount and the two-for-one offer.

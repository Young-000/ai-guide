**In one line**: Anthropic's CEO reportedly warned at a UN meeting that AI, if left unchecked, could pose a risk to humanity as a whole.

### Key points
- Speaking at the UN, Anthropic's CEO framed AI risk as a matter of national and human-scale concern.
- Company-level safeguards alone are seen as insufficient, with the remarks pointing to a need for international norms and oversight.
- The comments extend Anthropic's long-standing emphasis on AI safety onto the global policy stage.

### Why it matters
When the head of a company building frontier AI publicly calls for stronger rules, it signals that AI governance is shifting from voluntary corporate measures toward international norms. The stance could shape how governments and global bodies approach AI policy going forward.

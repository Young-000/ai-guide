**In one line**: Anthropic put forward measurements to understand how quickly AI development is progressing inside frontier labs.

### Key points
- Anthropic reportedly published a set of measurements meant to quantify the pace of AI development happening within frontier labs.
- The framing appears rooted in a gap: it is hard to gauge from the outside not just how capable models are, but how fast research and development is moving.
- Such metrics could serve as a shared vocabulary for tracking progress in safety and governance discussions.

### Why it matters
The speed of AI progress shapes the timing of regulatory and safety responses, yet there has been no agreed-upon yardstick for measuring it. A shared measurement frame would let policymakers and researchers discuss the situation on common terms.

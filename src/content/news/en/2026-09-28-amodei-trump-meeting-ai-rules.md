### Key points
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is reported to be meeting with President Trump.
- The meeting reportedly follows Amodei's public calls for regulations governing AI.
- It pairs one of the industry's most vocal advocates for AI rules with an administration generally inclined toward deregulation.

### Why it matters
Among frontier AI developers, Amodei has been unusually outspoken about the need for safety and regulation. A sit-down with a Trump administration wary of heavy-handed rules could signal where US AI policy is headed — and where the balance falls between industry self-governance and government oversight.

**In one line**: Fortune asks whether corporate AI spending has moved past the pilot phase into a sustained, at-scale expansion — a potential inflection point.

### Key points
- The central question is whether enterprise AI budgets are graduating from pilots and proofs-of-concept to company-wide deployment.
- Spending is rising, but skeptics and optimists disagree over whether productivity and revenue gains are keeping pace with the outlay.
- Reaching an "inflection point" is framed as a matter of AI becoming a recurring line item rather than a series of one-off tool trials.

### Why it matters
If corporate AI spend hardens from one-time experiments into standing budgets, it signals durable growth for the AI infrastructure and software market. If results lag the spend, the market could face a reported correction — making this a likely fork in vendor performance and enterprise adoption strategy.

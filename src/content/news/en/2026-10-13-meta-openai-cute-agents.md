**In one line**: Bloomberg reports that Meta and OpenAI are giving their AI agents cute, friendly personas to attract early adopters.

### Key points
- According to Bloomberg (Oct. 13), both companies have chosen a lighthearted, character-driven style for their consumer-facing agents.
- The push reportedly targets early adopters, the users who try new features first.
- Specific product names, rollout scope and usage figures are in the original report.

### Why it matters
AI agents have so far competed mostly on developer tools and enterprise automation. A "cute" approach from two of the biggest players signals an effort to move agents into everyday consumer products. A likely point of debate: a friendly face may lower users' guard when they hand an agent permissions such as making payments or sending messages.

**In one line**: OpenAI's chief scientist reportedly said AI labs may need to slow down, warning that society is unprepared for what comes next, according to Business Insider.

### Key points
- OpenAI's chief scientist is reported to have suggested AI labs may need to consider slowing the pace of development.
- The remark reportedly included the warning that "no one is prepared for the consequences."
- Coming from someone at the frontier of model development, the comment ties directly into ongoing AI safety debates.

### Why it matters
When an insider at a leading lab — one racing to push model capabilities — questions the pace, it signals that safety and governance concerns are moving from outside critics into the labs themselves. That framing could shape coming debates over AI regulation and deployment speed.

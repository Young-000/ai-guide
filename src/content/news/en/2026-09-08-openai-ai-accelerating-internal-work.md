**In one line**: OpenAI says it is using its own AI to accelerate internal work, even as the company's chief scientist reportedly urges a slower pace of development.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly detailed how it applies its own AI to internal tasks such as coding and research to work faster.
- At the same time, the company's chief scientist is said to have called for developing the technology more cautiously and slowing down.
- The result is two opposing signals — acceleration and restraint — coming from within the same organization.

### Why it matters
The tension underscores how the race for speed and the case for safety are colliding even inside a leading AI lab. As AI increasingly speeds up its own development, debate over how tightly to control that momentum is likely to intensify.

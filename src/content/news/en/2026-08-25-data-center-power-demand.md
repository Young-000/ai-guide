**In one line**: Bloomberg spotlights the surge in data center electricity demand fueled by the AI boom.

### Key points
- Large-scale data centers running AI training and inference are reportedly consuming vast amounts of electricity, adding strain to power grids.
- Power sourcing and generation capacity are emerging as a central constraint on AI infrastructure expansion.
- The same briefing also covered unrelated items, including US pressure on Iran's trade partners.

### Why it matters
The bottleneck in the AI race is shifting from chips to **power**. As data center demand affects regional grids and energy prices, the pace of AI expansion may increasingly hinge on infrastructure and policy factors rather than compute alone.

**In one line**: OpenAI and Google are reported to have signed an AI safety pact, coinciding with a rise in attacks aimed at software, including bitcoin.

### Key points
- OpenAI and Google reportedly signed a joint pact focused on AI safety.
- The move comes against a backdrop of growing attacks on software, including bitcoin.
- A coordinated step by two leading AI firms could feed into wider industry safety norms.

### Why it matters
It signals that rival AI heavyweights are trying to align on safety. As concerns grow that AI could be exploited in cyberattacks, such cooperation may shape both self-regulation and formal policy discussions across the industry.

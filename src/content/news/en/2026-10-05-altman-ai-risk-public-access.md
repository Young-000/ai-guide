**In one line**: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly said that keeping AI broadly accessible to the public requires society to accept some level of risk.

### Key points
- Altman is reported to have framed **broad public access** to AI as a priority worth protecting.
- His argument holds that **zero risk is not achievable**, so some risk must be tolerated to preserve that access.
- The stance reflects a concern that locking AI down in the name of safety could leave its benefits to a narrow few.

### Why it matters
As AI regulation debates play out along a safety-versus-openness tension, a leading industry figure is leaning toward openness. The framing is a signal that could shape how governments calibrate AI rules and how OpenAI approaches releasing and distributing its models.

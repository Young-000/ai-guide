**In one line**: The long-running feud between Elon Musk and Sam Altman has reportedly resurfaced, this time centered on Apple's partnership with OpenAI.

### Key points
- The clash between Musk (xAI) and Altman (OpenAI) is reported to have flared again through Apple's OpenAI integration.
- The core issue touches on platform fairness — whether any single AI gets preferential placement in the App Store or as a device-level default.
- The dispute continues a longer arc spanning OpenAI's founding, its shift toward commercialization, and prior legal battles.

### Why it matters
Which AI a platform with billions of devices — like Apple — sets as its default can reshape the market. The debate signals that AI competition is moving beyond raw model quality toward control of distribution channels.

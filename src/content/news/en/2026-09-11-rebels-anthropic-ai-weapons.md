**In one line**: A Washington Post report says a rebel group appeared to use Anthropic's AI chatbot to help develop guided weapons.

### Key points
- The Washington Post, citing a report, said a rebel group appeared to use Anthropic's AI chatbot (a Claude-family model) during weapons development.
- The report points to the AI possibly assisting with work related to guided weapons.
- Anthropic's usage policies prohibit dangerous applications such as weapons development, putting the effectiveness of its misuse safeguards under fresh scrutiny.

### Why it matters
The account offers a concrete example of concerns that general-purpose AI can be repurposed for military and weapons use, and it is likely to fuel regulatory and policy debate over how effective AI companies' misuse controls and access restrictions really are.

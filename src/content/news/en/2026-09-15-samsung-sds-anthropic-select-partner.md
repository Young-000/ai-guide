**In one line**: Samsung SDS has reportedly become Anthropic's first 'Select'-tier partner in Korea.

### Key points
- According to The Korea Herald, Samsung SDS is Anthropic's first Korean 'Select' partner.
- The tie-up is expected to let Samsung SDS embed Claude models into its enterprise AI services and deliver them to domestic clients.
- 'Select' is a tier within Anthropic's partner program; the report highlights this as a first for Korea.

### Why it matters
A global AI vendor formally partnering with a major Korean IT services firm signals that demand for Claude-based enterprise AI is picking up locally. Samsung SDS's distribution reach could broaden generative-AI adoption among Korean businesses.

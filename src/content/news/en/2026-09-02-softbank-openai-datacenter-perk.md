**In one line**: SoftBank's data-center joint venture reportedly offered a $5.5 billion incentive to land OpenAI as an anchor tenant, according to a WSJ exclusive.

### Key points
- WSJ exclusive. SoftBank's data-center venture is reported to have put a $5.5 billion perk on the table to secure OpenAI.
- The deal reflects aligned interests: OpenAI's need to lock in long-term compute for large-scale training and inference, and SoftBank's push to secure an anchor tenant for its facilities.
- Full contract terms and the structure of the incentive were not disclosed as of the report.

### Why it matters
The race for AI infrastructure is moving beyond chips into data-center sites, power, and capital. A large incentive to secure an anchor customer signals just how big the risk and scale of AI data-center investment have become.

**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly committed $1 billion to bolster cyberdefense capabilities as scrutiny over AI safety grows.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have pledged $1 billion toward strengthening cyberdefense efforts.
- The move comes amid intensifying regulatory and industry scrutiny of generative AI's potential for misuse and its safety risks.
- It stands out as a case of an AI developer allocating major funding to the defensive infrastructure needed to counter threats its own technology could enable.

### Why it matters
As concerns mount that AI models can be turned toward cyberattacks, a developer putting money directly into defense signals a willingness to own both the risk of building the technology and the duty of protecting against it. The open question is whether this safety spending translates into measurable defensive results.

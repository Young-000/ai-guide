**In one line**: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly said the real economy is adapting to AI more slowly than he had expected.

### Key points
- Altman is said to have contrasted the fast pace of model capability gains with the slower rate at which companies and industries actually fold AI into their workflows.
- The remark points to a lag between AI's rising technical capabilities and their translation into measurable economic outcomes like productivity and revenue.
- The tone tempers earlier forecasts that AI would reshape labor and the economy at breakneck speed.

### Why it matters
When a figure at the frontier of AI commercialization strikes a cautious note on adoption speed, it feeds directly into the market's ongoing recalibration of AI return-on-investment expectations. The gap between technical progress and real-world uptake could become a central variable in future AI investment and policy debates.

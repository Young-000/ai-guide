**In one line**: Sam Altman says OpenAI won't pursue an IPO until it can better guarantee model safety.

### Key points
- CEO Sam Altman said an OpenAI IPO won't happen until the company can "make better promises about model safety."
- No firm timeline was given — there is no fixed date in sight.
- He tied the caution to the recent surge in model capability, which has raised safety concerns alongside it.

### Why it matters
Markets have spent months speculating on when OpenAI might go public. By framing safety as a precondition for an IPO, Altman signals where the company is placing its weight amid the tension between fundraising pressure and safety commitments.

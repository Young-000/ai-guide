**Summary**: Nobel laureate Demis Hassabis is stepping aside from daily management at Google DeepMind to focus on AGI strategy, triggering the biggest leadership shake-up at the unit since its 2023 formation.

### Key Facts
- **Hassabis → Chairman + Alphabet Chief Scientist**: Shifts focus to AGI strategy and scientific applications; continues leading Isomorphic Labs; remains based in London.
- **Kavukcuoglu → SVP**: Current CTO and Chief AI Architect Koray Kavukcuoglu takes charge of Gemini model development, frontier research, and the Gemini app and developer teams, reporting directly to Sundar Pichai.
- **Jeff Dean departs**: A central architect of Google's AI strategy, Dean is leaving with several colleagues to launch a startup.
- **Market reaction**: Alphabet shares fell roughly 4% following the announcement.

### Why It Matters
The Brain/DeepMind merger in 2023 was meant to unify Google's AI efforts, but a two-continent structure split between London and Mountain View has drawn criticism for slowing execution. Hassabis's move signals his belief that AGI is close — he wants to focus there — while Kavukcuoglu inherits the operational mandate to turn Gemini's research into competitive products. Losing Jeff Dean at the same time adds further uncertainty to Google's AI trajectory.

**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to be entering the legal tech market with 'Astra for Law,' a product built for legal professionals.

### Key points
- OpenAI has reportedly introduced 'Astra for Law,' an AI product aimed at lawyers and legal teams.
- Its stated focus is document-heavy work such as contract and case review and legal research.
- The move puts OpenAI in direct competition with incumbents like Harvey and Thomson Reuters.

### Why it matters
Law is a high-value domain where textual accuracy and reliability are paramount. OpenAI shipping a finished, profession-specific product — rather than just a general model — signals a push from horizontal platform into vertical applications.

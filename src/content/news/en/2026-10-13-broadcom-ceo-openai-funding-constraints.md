**In one line**: Broadcom's CEO reportedly backed the viability of the company's OpenAI business, but warned that industry-wide funding could limit AI growth.

### Key points
- CEO Hock Tan reportedly described Broadcom's business with OpenAI as "viable."
- He also cautioned that the capital available for AI infrastructure spending may become a constraint.
- Broadcom and OpenAI announced a deal in October 2025 to co-develop and deploy 10 GW of custom AI accelerators, with deployment running from the second half of 2026 through 2029.
- The full context of his remarks and any specific figures should be checked in the original report.

### Why it matters
OpenAI's chip deployments are just getting underway, and the supplier's own CEO is pointing to money as the risk. That suggests the bottleneck in the AI infrastructure race may be moving from chip supply to capital.

**In one line**: LG CNS, the IT services arm of South Korea's LG Group, has reportedly reached the top tier of Anthropic's partner program.

### Key points
- According to BigGo Finance, LG CNS has secured top-tier partner status with Anthropic.
- Fewer than 50 companies worldwide are said to hold this status.
- The available report does not give the program's specific requirements, the tier's name or the scope of the partnership.

### Why it matters
AI model makers increasingly rely on systems integrators to sell to large enterprise and public-sector customers. If one of Korea's largest integrators is now a top Anthropic partner, Claude-based systems could reach Korean companies faster.

**In one line**: California has reportedly passed SB 813, a bill focused on overseeing and regulating AI system audits.

### Key points
- The bill, **SB 813**, is said to center on supervising AI audit processes.
- A reported **$400,000 probe into OpenAI** is cited as the backdrop to the legislation.
- Specific provisions and effective dates remain to be confirmed.

### Why it matters
The move signals a shift in AI regulation from the act of building models toward verification and auditing. As the largest tech-regulating jurisdiction in the U.S., California's laws often set a reference point for other states and corporate policy.

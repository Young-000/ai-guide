**In one line**: Microsoft is increasingly competing in the open with OpenAI — its key AI partner — as well as Anthropic, TechCrunch reports.

### Key points
- TechCrunch says Microsoft's recent moves show a rivalry with OpenAI and Anthropic that is "more open than ever."
- Microsoft has invested heavily in OpenAI while reportedly building up its own in-house AI capabilities in parallel.
- The report sharpens the picture of Microsoft as both a partner to, and a competitor of, leading AI labs.

### Why it matters
When Big Tech's largest AI investor shifts from collaboration toward competition, it can reshape model supply, cloud deals, and the broader enterprise AI market. It's a signal that the industry's alliance map may be realigning.

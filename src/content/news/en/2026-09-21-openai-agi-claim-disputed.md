**In one line**: OpenAI reportedly declared the AGI era has arrived, but the operator of the benchmark it cited pushed back, saying the near-perfect 99.9% score came with an asterisk.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have declared the arrival of the AGI (artificial general intelligence) era, citing its model's performance.
- The operator of the referenced benchmark reportedly disputed the framing, noting the 99.9% score carried conditions and caveats.
- In short, a near-perfect score does not necessarily amount to achieving general intelligence.

### Why it matters
Claims of "reaching AGI" ripple across marketing, investment, and regulatory debates. This case highlights what caveats can hide behind a single benchmark figure — and how the party that runs the measurement and the company being measured can read the same number very differently.

**In one line**: Barron's reports that Anthropic keeps disclosing new risk factors while it plans an IPO valued at about $2 trillion.

### Key points
- Anthropic, the company behind Claude, is reportedly preparing a public listing at a valuation of about $2 trillion, according to Barron's.
- Barron's says the company's list of disclosed risks keeps growing as IPO preparations continue.
- The source summary does not say which risks were added or when the listing might happen.

### Why it matters
A listing near $2 trillion would rank among the largest tech IPOs. Risk disclosures show how the company itself sees threats such as competition, heavy compute spending and regulation. They also help investors judge how far to discount expectations for AI growth.

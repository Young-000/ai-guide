**In one line**: OpenAI launched GPT-5.6-Cyber, a cybersecurity-specialized model, on Aug 10 — restricted to vetted defenders through a new "Daybreak Red" access tier.

### Key points
- Built on the GPT-5.6 Sol architecture and tuned for offensive-security work such as finding zero-days and building exploit chains.
- Reported 95% completion on advanced tasks (exploit chains, authentication bypasses, privilege escalation), versus 57.3% for GPT-5.5-Cyber and 1.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol with standard safeguards.
- Rather than a general release, it ships via an expanded Daybreak program (Red and Blue tiers) that requires identity verification, monitoring, and legal declarations for approved users.
- Pricing is listed at $12.50 per million input tokens, $75 per million output, and $1.25 per million cached input tokens.

### Why it matters
Instead of blocking a model with dual-use offensive capability, OpenAI is opening it only to defenders — the bet being to "find vulnerabilities before attackers do." It's an early test of gating powerful AI behind vetted access tiers rather than open availability.

**In one line**: Amazon added scam detection to Alexa for Shopping so users can verify whether a message really came from the retailer.

### Key points
- Amazon introduced a scam-detection feature within Alexa for Shopping.
- It verifies whether suspicious emails, texts, and other messages actually originated from Amazon.
- The feature targets common Amazon-impersonation phishing, such as fake delivery or account alerts.

### Why it matters
Amazon-impersonation phishing is among the most common consumer scams. Turning the shopping assistant into an authenticity check lets users judge a suspicious message on the spot, without a separate search — a practical step toward preventing losses.

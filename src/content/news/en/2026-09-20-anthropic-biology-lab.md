**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to have built its own biology lab to test whether its AI models can design and carry out experiments in a real-world setting.

### Key points
- Anthropic has reportedly established a biology laboratory.
- The apparent goal is to move AI-generated hypotheses and experiment designs off-screen into actual bench work.
- It marks the "AI for Science" trend extending beyond chat-style answers toward physical experimental validation.

### Why it matters
AI's contribution to science has largely stayed in text — summarizing papers or proposing hypotheses. Moving to a stage where AI outputs are checked for reproducibility in a real lab could reshape how model capability and reliability are evaluated.

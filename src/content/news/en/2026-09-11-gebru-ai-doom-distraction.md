**In one line**: Timnit Gebru contends that the AI industry's extinction warnings serve to sidestep debate over harms already happening.

### Key points
- Gebru, a former co-lead of Google's AI ethics team, is now one of the field's most prominent critics.
- She argues that the more attention "superintelligent extinction" scenarios command, the more concrete present-day harms get pushed aside.
- The real problems she highlights include autonomous weapons, labor exploitation, and bias and discrimination.

### Why it matters
The debate over AI risk splits between distant existential threats and immediate, tangible harms — and where regulatory and research resources flow shapes policy direction. Gebru's position represents one side of that priority argument.

**In one line**: Canada's British Columbia province has reportedly filed suit against OpenAI in a US court in connection with a school shooting.

### Key points
- The BC provincial government has sued OpenAI in a US court, according to Arab News.
- The case reportedly stems from a school shooting that occurred in Canada.
- Notably, the province filed in a US court rather than at home — apparently tied to OpenAI's US base.
- Specific claims and OpenAI's response were not detailed in the original report at the time of writing.

### Why it matters
Cases testing the causal and legal link between AI chatbot use and real-world harm are multiplying. A government body acting as plaintiff — and choosing the AI firm's home jurisdiction rather than its own — could set a precedent for how cross-border liability for AI services is defined.

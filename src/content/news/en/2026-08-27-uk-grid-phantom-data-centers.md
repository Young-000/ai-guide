**In one line**: UK regulators are tackling "phantom" data center projects that reserve grid access without ever getting built.

### Key points
- The UK's energy regulator (Ofgem) is using a range of measures to stop speculative data center projects from plugging into the power grid, Wired reports.
- The core issue is "phantom" projects that lock up grid-connection slots without leading to actual construction or operation.
- Left unaddressed, this bottleneck delays grid connections for projects with genuine demand.

### Why it matters
Securing power for data centers is emerging as a key bottleneck for AI infrastructure growth. With the UK's AI ambitions tied to clearing the connection queue, this is a case study in how regulation can set the pace of the AI buildout.

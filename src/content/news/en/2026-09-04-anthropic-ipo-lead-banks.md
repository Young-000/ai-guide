**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly close to naming Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs to top roles in a large IPO.

### Key points
- Per the Financial Times, Anthropic is nearing a decision to award lead underwriting roles to **Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs**.
- The offering is being discussed at a valuation of up to **$2tn**.
- The process is said to be at the bank-selection stage; timing and terms have not been disclosed.

### Why it matters
A public listing for Anthropic, the maker of Claude, signals that AI funding is moving beyond private venture rounds into public capital markets. If the reported valuation holds, it could reset benchmarks for how AI companies across the sector are priced.

**In one line**: AI critic Gary Marcus argues in his newsletter that market expectations around an Anthropic IPO have been overhyped.

### Key points
- Gary Marcus published "The hyping of Anthropic's IPO" on his newsletter Marcus on AI, reportedly questioning the optimism surrounding a potential Anthropic listing.
- Marcus is a long-standing skeptic who has repeatedly argued that commercial expectations for generative AI outpace its actual profitability and technical maturity.
- Anthropic has not publicly confirmed a specific IPO timeline or size, so the piece reads as commentary and critique rather than factual reporting.

### Why it matters
As valuations and listing expectations for AI companies climb, pushback from skeptics like Marcus serves as a prompt for investors and the industry to re-examine the gap between hype and results.

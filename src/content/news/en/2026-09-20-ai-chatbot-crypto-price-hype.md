**In one line**: A crypto outlet ran a story claiming "Anthropic's Claude predicts Chainlink (LINK) will surge 300%," but AI chatbots are not asset-price forecasters.

### Key points
- Outlets like Cryptonews increasingly prompt AI chatbots for price outlooks and package the replies as headlines.
- This example frames a claim of LINK rising 300% by 2027 as a "Claude AI prediction."
- An LLM is a language model trained on past text, not a forecasting engine. It gives different answers to the same prompt and does not analyze live market data.

### Why it matters
Price outlooks borrowing AI's authority can distort investment decisions. Chatbot output is not financial advice, and articles that cite specific AI "predictions" as fact are worth reading skeptically.

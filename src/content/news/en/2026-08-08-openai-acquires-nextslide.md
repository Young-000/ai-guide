**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly acquired NextSlide, a startup that builds AI-powered presentation tools.

### Key points
- OpenAI is reported to have acquired NextSlide, a company developing AI-based slide and presentation creation tools.
- Deal size and terms were not disclosed.
- How NextSlide's technology and team will be integrated into OpenAI's product lineup has not yet been detailed.

### Why it matters
The move reads as OpenAI expanding beyond chatbots into practical productivity areas like documents and presentations. With Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini already competing to automate office documents, absorbing a dedicated creation tool positions OpenAI to compete directly in that space.

**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to have solved more than 100 math problems with its models and to have established a new math advisory group.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly announced that its models solved over 100 math problems.
- The company is said to have formed a dedicated **Math Advisory Group** to support this research.
- The move points to a push toward AI-assisted **mathematical reasoning and proof**, beyond routine computation.

### Why it matters
Math is a benchmark for how well AI can reason. If a model genuinely worked through a large batch of problems and OpenAI has organized expert advisors around it, AI's role as a research assistant could accelerate. The difficulty of the problems and the verification method, however, warrant checking against the original report.

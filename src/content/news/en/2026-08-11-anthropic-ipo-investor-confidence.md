**In one line**: A Wall Street Journal exclusive reports that Anthropic is moving to reassure investors ahead of a blockbuster IPO.

### Key points
- The Wall Street Journal reports exclusively that Anthropic is working to secure and maintain investor confidence in the run-up to a public listing.
- The report characterizes the planned offering as a "blockbuster" IPO.
- Specific financial terms and timing appear to be detailed in the paywalled original; no official listing announcement has been confirmed here.

### Why it matters
As the maker of Claude, Anthropic's listing — alongside OpenAI — would be one of the marquee AI companies entering public markets, serving as a barometer for AI-sector valuations and investor sentiment. A pre-IPO push to firm up confidence signals how critically the market may be weighing AI firms' profitability and durability of growth.

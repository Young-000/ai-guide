**In one line**: Research firm Future Market Insights released a report projecting sustained growth for the large language model (LLM) market.

### Key points
- Future Market Insights published a market research report covering the medium-to-long-term growth trajectory of the LLM market.
- Growth drivers typically cited include expanding enterprise adoption of generative AI and demand for practical automation in areas like customer support and document processing.
- Specific market-size and growth-rate figures reside in the paid research report, and estimates are known to vary widely between research firms.

### Why it matters
LLMs are now classified not as a single product but as a "market" tracked by research firms. That said, forecasts like this differ in methodology and estimates from one publisher to another, so the reported direction — a rising adoption trend — is more useful as a reference than any single headline number.

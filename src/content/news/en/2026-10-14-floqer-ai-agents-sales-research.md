**TL;DR**: Floqer unveiled an "autonomous customer knowledge platform" in which AI agents research prospects and keep that information current for B2B sales and marketing teams.

### Key Points
- Floqer offers B2B sales and marketing teams an AI-powered database that keeps collecting, organizing and updating the information they use to understand customers.
- The pitch is that AI agents take over the prospect research reps usually do by hand.
- The company presented on October 14 on the Startup Battlefield stage at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026.
- The summary does not include figures such as funding or customer count.

### Why It Matters
Prospect research takes a lot of time and goes stale quickly, which makes it a natural target for AI agents. The sales-data market already has established tools such as Clay and Apollo. Floqer's test is whether data that refreshes itself, rather than a one-time pull, is enough to set it apart.

**In one line**: Another large school district has reportedly banned generative AI, this time for its entire student body, per The Washington Post.

### Key points
- According to The Washington Post, a major school district has moved to **ban generative AI tools for all students**.
- The step follows similar restrictions adopted by other districts, extending a wave of AI limits in schools.
- Districts typically cite academic dishonesty (cheating), data and privacy concerns, and unproven learning outcomes as reasons for such bans.

### Why it matters
As chatbot-style AI spreads rapidly into classrooms, a district-wide ban highlights education's unresolved question of how to teach and govern the technology. Policies remain split between outright prohibition and structured, guided use.

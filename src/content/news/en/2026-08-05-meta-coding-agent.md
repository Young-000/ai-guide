**In one line**: Meta has reportedly released a coding agent, stepping into the competitive AI developer-tools race.

### Key points
- Meta introduced a coding agent designed to assist with software development tasks, according to the WSJ.
- The tool is reported to target head-to-head competition with market leaders such as OpenAI and Anthropic.
- It extends a broader industry shift toward agentic tools that automate writing and editing code.

### Why it matters
Coding agents are among the fastest-monetizing segments in today's AI market, and Meta's entry signals intensifying competition. For teams already using AI development tools, it widens the field of options while making performance and price comparisons more consequential.

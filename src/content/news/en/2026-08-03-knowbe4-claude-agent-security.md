**In one line**: Security company KnowBe4 is reportedly extending the reach of its "Agent Risk Manager" tool to cover AI agents built on Anthropic's Claude.

### Key points
- KnowBe4 announced it is expanding the security and governance capabilities of its "Agent Risk Manager" to AI agents running on Anthropic's Claude.
- The move targets growing demand to monitor and control autonomous AI agents as more of them operate inside enterprise environments.
- KnowBe4, long known for security awareness training, has recently been broadening into AI agent risk management.

### Why it matters
As enterprises deploy LLM-based agents like Claude into workflows, overseeing the data and permissions those agents can reach becomes a fresh security challenge. This extension signals the emergence of agent-security tooling tailored to specific model ecosystems.

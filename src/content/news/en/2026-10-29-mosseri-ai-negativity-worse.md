**One-line summary**: Instagram head Adam Mosseri acknowledged that many people really dislike AI and said the negativity is "going to get worse."

### Key points
- Mosseri appeared on the Colin and Samir podcast, where he discussed the launch of widescreen and was asked about AI.
- The question referenced a recent trend of college students booing tech CEOs at graduation ceremonies.
- He acknowledged that a lot of people really don't like AI, and said this isn't limited to young people and creatives.
- Per the headline, he expects that negativity to worsen.

### Why it matters
A senior executive at a major platform company publicly conceded that public resentment of AI is broad and growing. For companies expanding AI features, user trust and acceptance are becoming a real challenge.

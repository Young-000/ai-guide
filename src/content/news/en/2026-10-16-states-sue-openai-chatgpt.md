**In one line**: A group of U.S. states has sued OpenAI, alleging that its flagship chatbot harms children and deceives adults.

### Key points
- The plaintiffs are multiple U.S. states, and the defendant is OpenAI, which makes ChatGPT. Broadband Breakfast reported the suit on October 16.
- The states reportedly focus on two issues: harm to minors and deceptive practices toward adult users.
- The number of states, the specific claims, and the court where the case was filed should be checked in the original report.
- No public response from OpenAI had been confirmed as of the report.

### Why it matters
Until now, most legal fights over generative AI have been about copyright. Here, states are acting directly under **consumer protection and child safety** law, which is a different kind of challenge. Depending on the outcome, it could change how chatbots are run across the industry, including age verification, safeguards for minors, and marketing claims.

**In one line**: AI company Rivulo announced, via a press release, that it has been named an OpenAI Select Partner.

### Key points
- Rivulo says it has earned "Select Partner" status within OpenAI's partner program.
- The announcement was distributed as a corporate press release (ACCESS Newswire).
- Specific privileges, technical access, and terms tied to the tier were not detailed in the source.

### Why it matters
Designating vetted implementation and build partners is one way large model providers extend their ecosystems beyond first-party products. That said, this item is based on a company-issued press release, so the actual scale and outcomes of the collaboration remain to be confirmed by follow-up reporting.

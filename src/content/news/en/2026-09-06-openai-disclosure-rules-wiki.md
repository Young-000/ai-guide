**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly preparing new disclosure rules in response to a so-called "wiki incident."

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to be revising its internal information-disclosure rules following a recently surfaced "wiki incident."
- The report comes via TNGlobal; specifics on the rules' content and timing have not yet been made public.

### Why it matters
How an AI developer handles disclosure is closely tied to user trust and regulatory posture. OpenAI tightening its own disclosure standards could shape transparency practices across the broader industry.

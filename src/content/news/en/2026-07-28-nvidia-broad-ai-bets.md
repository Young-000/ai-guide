**One-line summary**: Nvidia is reportedly extending support and investment across multiple AI companies with sharply different stances on openness, including OpenAI.

### Key points
- Nvidia is said to be widening its backing of leading AI firms, OpenAI among them.
- Gizmodo notes that the targets range from open-leaning outfits to extremely closed ("super-closed") labs — very different postures under one investor.
- As the main beneficiary of AI-chip demand, Nvidia appears to be spreading capital across the ecosystem rather than favoring a single camp.

### Why it matters
Where Nvidia places its money shapes which AI firms gain an edge in GPU and capital access. Backing both open and closed players signals an effort to entrench its influence over the AI race itself, regardless of who wins.

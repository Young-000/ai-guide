**In one line**: A pastor has reportedly filed suit against OpenAI, alleging that ChatGPT gave him dangerous medical advice.

### Key points
- According to Premier Christian News, the plaintiff — a pastor — sued OpenAI, claiming that health and medical guidance from ChatGPT was dangerous.
- The suit is said to argue that the chatbot's responses could have led to real harm.
- Details on OpenAI's response and the specific legal claims remain limited based on currently available reporting.

### Why it matters
The case again spotlights the safety and liability questions raised when AI chatbots answer medical and health queries. As more users treat chatbots as a substitute for professional care, disputes over who is legally responsible for faulty advice are likely to grow.

**In one line**: Cyber risk management firm KYND is reportedly turning to Anthropic's AI to help assess and test cyber risk.

### Key points
- KYND, a cyber risk management company, has reportedly adopted Anthropic's AI models for risk assessment.
- The move appears aimed at pairing generative AI with its process for diagnosing and testing cyber exposure.
- FinTech Global reported the development on August 10.

### Why it matters
The case highlights a growing push to apply large language models (LLMs) to risk assessment across security, insurance, and fintech. It suggests AI providers like Anthropic are expanding beyond consumer use into specialized B2B workflows such as risk diagnostics.

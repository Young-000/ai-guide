**In one line**: TechCrunch reports on OpenAI's effort to expand AI agents from technical users, like software engineers, toward the general public.

### Key points
- Agents that first gained traction in specialized work such as coding are reportedly being reshaped into consumer tools that handle everyday tasks.
- The stated ambition is a general-purpose agent for "everything," not a single-task assistant.
- TechCrunch frames this as a frontier lab's bid for mass adoption while questioning whether ordinary users will actually take it up.

### Why it matters
If agents spread beyond technical users, they could reshape competition across search, personal assistants, and workflow automation. The catch is the gap between a capable demo and daily habit — success hinges on whether everyday users return to these agents repeatedly.

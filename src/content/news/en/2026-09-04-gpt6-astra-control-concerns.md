**In one line**: AI newsletter Transformer has raised concerns about the safety and controllability of OpenAI's next major model, referred to as "GPT-6 Astra."

### Key points
- Transformer (Substack) argues that OpenAI's next model, dubbed "GPT-6 Astra," could reach a level of capability that is hard for humans to understand or control.
- OpenAI has not officially confirmed the model name, specifications, or release timeline; the claims appear to be based on the newsletter's own analysis and forecasting.
- The argument centers less on raw capability gains and more on alignment, interpretability, and control challenges.

### Why it matters
As frontier models grow more capable, the tension between the performance race and ensuring safety intensifies. That said, this is a newsletter's outlook rather than an official announcement, so it is best read as a signal of where industry concerns are heading — not as confirmed fact.

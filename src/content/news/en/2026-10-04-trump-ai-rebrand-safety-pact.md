**TL;DR**: TechCrunch's Equity podcast weighed whether the Trump administration's rebranding of AI can actually win back public trust.

### Key points
- The discussion centered on efforts to reframe AI around a "super intelligence" narrative.
- That messaging is reportedly paired with a "non-binding safety pact" that carries no legal enforcement.
- The hosts questioned whether rhetoric and voluntary commitments can resolve AI's underlying image problem.

### Why it matters
When AI oversight leans on voluntary pacts and messaging rather than enforceable rules, promotional value can outpace real safeguards. Because the US approach tends to anchor global AI governance debates, whether such commitments are binding has direct consequences for the industry and users alike.

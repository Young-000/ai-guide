**In one line**: Alibaba has introduced a new AI model that it describes as the most powerful it has released so far.

### Key points
- Alibaba announced a new AI model it positions, by its own account, as its strongest to date.
- The company's AI efforts have largely centered on its Qwen family of models, many released as open weights.
- The reveal lands amid an ongoing push among U.S. and Chinese tech giants to advance frontier-model capabilities.

### Why it matters
A renewed frontier-model push from China's largest cloud and commerce player could shift the balance between open and closed models and shape AI adoption across Asia. Specific benchmarks and release details remain to be confirmed.

**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly using Claude to help translate the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem into a machine-verifiable, formalized form.

### Key points
- According to SiliconANGLE, Anthropic used its Claude model to assist with the **formalization** of Fermat's Last Theorem's proof.
- Formalization means rewriting a human-written proof so a proof assistant such as Lean can verify it step by step, automatically.
- Fermat's Last Theorem, proven by Andrew Wiles in 1994, has a sprawling proof whose full formalization is considered a major project in its own right.

### Why it matters
Formalizing mathematical proofs is a well-known benchmark for an AI's logical reasoning. The effort is read as a sign of whether large language models can move beyond generating text to genuinely assisting rigorous math and verification research.

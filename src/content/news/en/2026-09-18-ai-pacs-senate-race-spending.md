**In one line**: PACs linked to the AI industry have spent nearly $1 million on an overlooked South Dakota Senate race.

### Key points
- The seat is a reliably Republican one in South Dakota, with an incumbent running for reelection.
- PACs associated with AI labs and investors have reportedly spent more on the race than the seat's actual residents have contributed.
- Total spending is reported to be approaching $1 million.

### Why it matters
It signals that AI money is reaching down even into low-profile local races. The industry appears to be expanding its political influence to shape the regulatory and legislative environment in its favor — a dynamic that could affect how future AI regulation takes shape.

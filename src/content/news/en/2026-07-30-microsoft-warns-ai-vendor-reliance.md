**In one line**: Microsoft has reportedly cautioned companies against relying on a single AI supplier like OpenAI or Anthropic.

### Key points
- Microsoft is said to have warned against vendor lock-in tied to any one major model provider.
- OpenAI (GPT models) and Anthropic (Claude models) are currently the two headline suppliers in the enterprise AI market.
- A "multi-model" approach — switching between models based on task, cost, and performance — is floated as the alternative.

### Why it matters
Microsoft ships both OpenAI and Anthropic models across Copilot and Azure, so the guidance reflects a broader industry push to spread provider risk — pricing shifts, policy changes, and availability. For enterprises, the challenge becomes designing procurement and architecture that avoid single-model lock-in.

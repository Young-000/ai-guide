**In one line**: Morgan Stanley is reported to be leading a roughly $15 billion financing effort to build out Anthropic's data center campus in Texas.

### Key points
- The financing is said to total about $15 billion, aimed at securing the large-scale compute infrastructure needed to train and run frontier AI models.
- Investment bank Morgan Stanley is reportedly leading the deal, with the campus set to be located in Texas.
- The move points to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, expanding its own compute capacity.

### Why it matters
A traditional bulge-bracket bank arranging billions for an AI data center signals that AI infrastructure is becoming a mainstream financial asset class, not just a venture or cloud-capital play. It reflects a growing view that leadership in frontier models hinges on access to compute.

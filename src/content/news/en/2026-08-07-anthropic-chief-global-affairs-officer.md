**In one line**: Anthropic has named Tino Cuéllar to the newly created position of Chief Global Affairs Officer.

### Key points
- Anthropic appointed Tino Cuéllar to lead a new role, Chief Global Affairs Officer.
- It is the company's first dedicated global affairs and policy leadership post, elevating how it engages with governments and regulators.
- The scope of the role and his start date were not detailed in the report.

### Why it matters
The move comes as leading AI firms build out policy and external-affairs teams to navigate emerging AI regulation worldwide. It suggests Anthropic is extending its long-standing emphasis on AI safety into a broader global policy strategy.

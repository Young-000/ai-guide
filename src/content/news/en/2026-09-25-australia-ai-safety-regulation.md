**In one line**: A Guardian opinion piece argues that Australia's current response to AI risk falls short of meaningful regulation.

### Key points
- The piece appears to contend that gathering feedback via a "public inbox" is not enough to protect citizens from potential AI harms.
- It suggests binding safeguards are needed rather than voluntary processes or consultation alone.
- The commentary sits within an ongoing debate over Australia's AI policy direction and regulatory gaps.

### Why it matters
As governments weigh self-regulation against legal obligations on AI, the Australian case is framed here as an example of the gap between token consultation and enforceable rules.

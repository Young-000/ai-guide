**In one line**: Google is reportedly pushing into legal-focused AI, putting it in direct competition with Anthropic and OpenAI, which are already targeting the space, per Business Insider.

### Key points
- Google is said to be ramping up its move into AI tools for legal work such as contract review and case analysis.
- The segment has been led by Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI (GPT), which have courted law firms and in-house legal teams.
- All three major model providers are now aiming at legal tech as a priority vertical.

### Why it matters
Legal work demands high accuracy and strong document handling, making it a premium AI market. Google's entry is likely to accelerate legal-tech adoption and widen the model choices available to law firms and corporate legal teams.

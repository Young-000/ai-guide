**In one line**: Claude maker Anthropic is reportedly targeting a large-scale IPO ahead of the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday, Bloomberg reported.

### Key points
- Per Bloomberg, Anthropic is said to be pursuing an initial public offering it wants done **before** the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday (Nov. 26, 2026).
- The report frames it as a "mega-IPO," suggesting a sizable listing, though specific offering size and valuation were not confirmed in the report.
- If it proceeds, it would mark a leading generative-AI developer stepping into public capital markets alongside OpenAI.

### Why it matters
An AI model maker going public is a turning point for funding the enormous research and infrastructure costs the sector demands. A listing within the year could ripple through AI valuations and investment flows broadly. For now, this remains a report-based account — not a confirmed fact until an official announcement.

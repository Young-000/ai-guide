**In one line**: Meta launched Muse, a personal AI assistant, in a renewed push to catch up in the AI race.

### Key points
- Meta introduced **Muse**, a personal assistant it says can put AI "in the hands of virtually anyone."
- The product is reportedly the latest step in a **multi-billion-dollar strategy overhaul**.
- The goal is to revive the company's position in an AI race where it is seen as trailing rivals.

### Why it matters
Despite heavy spending, Meta has been viewed as lagging behind OpenAI and Google in AI. Muse is a bid to re-enter the consumer AI assistant market, and its reception could signal the direction of Meta's broader AI strategy.

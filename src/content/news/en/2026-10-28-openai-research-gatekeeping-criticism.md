**One-line summary**: OpenAI announced breakthroughs in math, computer science and physics, but researchers interviewed by The Verge say they are increasingly tired of the company's behavior.

### Key points
- On Monday, OpenAI announced what it called impressive breakthroughs in mathematics, computer science and physics.
- The company also described an evolving approach to how it communicates its findings.
- Researchers who spoke to The Verge said that, despite this apparent course correction, they are growing increasingly weary of OpenAI's antics.
- The Verge characterized the past few months as turbulent for the company.

### Why it matters
Research claims from AI companies earn trust when outside researchers can examine and build on them. The reaction suggests that how results are shared matters to the research community as much as the results themselves.

**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly offered Blackstone a double-digit yield to finance the chips it needs, according to the Financial Times.

### Key points
- The Financial Times broke the story, and Yahoo Finance carried it.
- The financing is reportedly meant to pay for chips used to train and run AI models.
- The offered yield is in the double digits. The deal's size and structure, and whether it has closed, were not confirmed at the time of reporting.
- Blackstone, one of the world's largest alternative asset managers, has been growing its private credit and data center businesses.

### Why it matters
AI labs are now using debt-style financing, not just equity rounds, to pay for compute. A double-digit yield shows that money backed by chips is far from cheap. It also hints at where the risk and cost of the AI infrastructure build-out are landing.

**In one line**: TechCrunch has previewed a Disrupt 2026 session focused on how AI startups can build lasting "defensibility."

### Key points
- TechCrunch listed a session on building AI defensibility as part of its flagship **Disrupt 2026** lineup.
- Defensibility refers to durable advantages — across models, data, and distribution — that competitors cannot easily replicate.
- As foundation models converge in quality, differentiation strategy for application-layer startups is emerging as a central theme.

### Why it matters
When anyone can build a similar AI product on the same APIs, the question of "what protects your business" becomes existential for founders. Data moats, workflow integration, and switching costs are increasingly the yardsticks investors and acquirers apply.

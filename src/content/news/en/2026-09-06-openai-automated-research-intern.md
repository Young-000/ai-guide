**In one line**: OpenAI reportedly says it has reached its goal of creating an AI that functions like an automated research intern, assisting human researchers.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly claims it has hit its stated goal of building an "automated research intern."
- The framing suggests an AI assistant that helps carry out research tasks, rather than a full replacement for human researchers.
- The claim reinforces the industry shift toward agentic AI that can take on work autonomously.

### Why it matters
An AI that can take direction and handle parts of real research work signals the move beyond chatbots toward "agents that do the job." Still, the specific capabilities and how they were evaluated warrant further confirmation.

**In one line**: Business Insider reports that Anthropic's approach to watermarking Claude-generated text currently reaches further than similar efforts from rival AI companies.

### Key points
- Anthropic is said to be applying identifying watermarks to text produced by Claude.
- The report frames the effort as broader in scope than other leading AI firms' — at least for now.
- Text watermarking is technically harder than tagging images or video, and remains an area where the industry has been slow to standardize.

### Why it matters
Distinguishing AI-written text from human writing sits at the center of debates over academic integrity, misinformation, and copyright. Reliable text watermarking could reshape how content provenance is verified. Still, the headline's "for now" signals the competitive edge may be fluid rather than durable.

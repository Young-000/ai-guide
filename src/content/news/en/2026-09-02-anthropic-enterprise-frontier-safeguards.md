**In one line**: Anthropic unveiled a security feature that keeps enterprise Claude usage logs inside the customer's own cloud instead of the vendor's infrastructure.

### Key points
- The feature, called 'Enterprise Frontier Safeguards,' is reportedly aimed at organizations with strict security and compliance needs.
- Its core is data residency: records of Claude interactions stay within the customer's controlled environment rather than on Anthropic's systems.
- The move appears designed to lower adoption barriers in sectors—finance, healthcare, government—that resist letting data leave their perimeter.

### Why it matters
Fear of sensitive data leaving their control is one of the biggest reasons enterprises hesitate to deploy AI. An option to keep logs in a company's own cloud eases regulatory burden and could accelerate frontier-model adoption among large organizations.

**In one line**: New EU rules mandate disclosure of AI interactions and AI-generated content, exposing just how deeply AI is woven into everyday life in Europe.

### Key points
- The new EU rules require notice whenever people **interact with AI** or view **AI-generated or edited content**.
- As disclosure labels appear across chatbots, images, and video, the sheer extent of AI's presence in daily life is set to become visible.
- Some in the industry warn of **"disclosure fatigue"** — labels so common that users stop noticing them.

### Why it matters
The open question is whether transparency mandates actually change user behavior or get tuned out like ubiquitous warning notices. The EU's experiment could serve as a reference point for how other jurisdictions design AI labeling policy.

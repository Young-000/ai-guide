**In one line**: Meta is reportedly trialing robots to take on maintenance jobs currently done by data center technicians.

### Key points
- According to Wired, Meta is experimenting with robots that can swap cables, reset servers, and perform other tasks handled by on-site technicians.
- The targeted work covers routine upkeep that keeps data center operations running.
- Some workers have reportedly voiced concern that the automation could put their jobs at risk.

### Why it matters
As AI demand drives data centers to grow, the push extends automation into physical operations. If it works, it could reshape the cost structure of large operators — while raising familiar questions about how technician roles shift.

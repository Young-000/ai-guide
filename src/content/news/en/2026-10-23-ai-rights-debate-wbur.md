**One-line summary**: Boston public radio station WBUR has taken up the question of whether AI should hold the same rights as humans.

### Key points
- The headline asks directly whether AI should be granted rights equal to those of humans. The available RSS summary carries only the title and source, so specific guests and figures should be checked in the original piece.
- The question typically leads into debates over machine consciousness, the capacity to suffer, and how moral status should be judged.
- Skeptics are generally understood to argue that AI is a tool, and that rights talk risks blurring human accountability.
- Some researchers and companies have begun treating AI welfare (model welfare) as a research topic.

### Why it matters
The AI rights debate is moving beyond philosophy. It can shape legal liability, regulatory design and how companies operate their models. Mainstream broadcast coverage suggests the topic is reaching a general audience.

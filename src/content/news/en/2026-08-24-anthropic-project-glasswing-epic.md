**In one line**: Anthropic reportedly introduced a healthcare initiative called Project Glasswing and an expanded partnership with clinical reference service UpToDate at Epic's User Group Meeting (UGM).

### Key points
- The news emerged from the annual User Group Meeting (UGM) of Epic, a leading electronic health record (EHR) vendor, where Anthropic is reported to have presented a healthcare-focused effort dubbed Project Glasswing.
- The company is also said to be deepening its collaboration with UpToDate, a widely used clinical decision-support resource.
- The report was published by healthcare trade outlet Fierce Healthcare.

### Why it matters
Epic's UGM draws large numbers of U.S. hospitals and health systems, so AI partnerships surfaced there often have a real path into clinical settings. The moves suggest Anthropic is pushing beyond general-purpose models into the heavily regulated healthcare space.

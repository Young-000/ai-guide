**In one line**: Netflix is introducing an "AI Discover" tab in its TV app, and its first experimental feature recommends shows and movies based on what a viewer is in the mood for.

### Key points
- A new "AI Discover" tab is coming to Netflix's TV app.
- The tab is meant to showcase experimental, AI-powered features that help people find new shows and movies.
- The first experiment offers recommendations tied to a viewer's current mood.
- Example themes include "Something Light," "Family Movie Night" and "Quick Watches."
- Both TechCrunch and The Verge reported the news on November 3, 2026.

### What happened
TechCrunch described the first experimental feature under the new AI Discover tab as one that lets users get recommendations based on what they are currently in the mood for. Its headline puts mood front and center, and it frames the feature as the opening item in a tab designed to hold more experiments over time.

The Verge covered the same launch with a slightly different emphasis. It said the AI Discover tab in Netflix's TV app is where the company will show experimental AI features, and that the first of them surfaces content around themes. It listed "Something Light," "Family Movie Night" and "Quick Watches" as examples, and said that once a viewer picks a theme, Netflix will show matching titles. The excerpt of that article cuts off before describing what happens next.

The two accounts line up more than they differ. TechCrunch says "mood" and The Verge says "theme," but both appear to describe the same first feature. The Verge also specifies that the tab lives in the TV app, a detail the TechCrunch summary does not state.

### Why it matters
Streaming recommendations have largely worked by listing titles based on what a viewer has watched before. This experiment shifts the starting point to the viewer's current situation: something light, something the whole family can watch, or something short. That approach targets the common experience of scrolling for a long time without settling on anything to play.

It is also notable that Netflix is placing the feature in a dedicated tab labeled as experimental rather than folding it into the main interface. The sources do not say which other AI features may follow, but the purpose of the tab is clear: it is a place to try new ways of helping people decide what to watch, with mood- and theme-based picks as the first test.

**In one line**: According to a Chosun Ilbo report, Anthropic and OpenAI each unveiled new AI models around the same time.

### Key points
- Both leading developers are reported to have shipped new models.
- Specifics such as model names and benchmark figures should be confirmed against the original report.
- The moves continue a pattern of rapid, closely spaced model updates.

### Why it matters
When the two companies release new models with little gap between them, it underscores how tight the frontier-AI race has become. Shorter refresh cycles reshape the options — and the cost calculus — for the businesses and developers that build on these models.

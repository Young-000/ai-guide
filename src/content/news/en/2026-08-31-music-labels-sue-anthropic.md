**In one line**: Sony Music and Warner Music have reportedly filed suit against Anthropic over intellectual property infringement.

### Key points
- Two of the world's largest record labels, Sony Music and Warner Music, are reported to have sued AI company Anthropic for copyright infringement.
- The dispute reportedly centers on whether copyrighted music and lyrics were used to train AI models without authorization.
- The case continues an ongoing wave of copyright clashes between the music industry and generative AI firms.

### Why it matters
The legality of copyrighted works used in AI training remains one of the industry's biggest unresolved questions. When major labels litigate directly, it can shape how training data is licensed and how AI companies source data going forward.

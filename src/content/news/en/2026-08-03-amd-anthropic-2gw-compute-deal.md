**Summary**: AMD and Anthropic announced a strategic partnership on July 22 under which AMD will invest up to $5 billion in Anthropic and deploy two gigawatts of Instinct MI450 GPUs packaged in AMD Helios rack-scale systems.

### Key facts
- AMD commits a phased **equity investment of up to $5 billion**, released as deployment milestones are met — aligning financial incentives with actual hardware adoption.
- Hardware stack: **MI455X GPUs**, EPYC "Venice" processors, Pensando networking, and ROCm software inside AMD Helios rack-scale solutions.
- First gigawatt deployment begins in **H1 2027**; total scope reaches two gigawatts.
- The deal spans infrastructure procurement, joint engineering work, and investment — a multi-layer structure rather than a simple customer purchase order.

### Why it matters
With frontier AI training and inference dominated by Nvidia H100/B200 clusters, this is the **largest announced commitment of AMD compute for a single AI lab**. For AMD, it is a pivotal proof-of-deployment reference that could shift procurement decisions across the industry; for Anthropic, it diversifies supply chain risk while securing the compute needed to scale Claude capacity.

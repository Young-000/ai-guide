**One-line summary**: AI-generated "model" accounts are reposting comedians' jokes to boost short-form views, then sending that traffic to paid adult subscription pages.

### Key Points
- Wired reports that a coordinated network of AI-generated female personas is reusing comedians' material.
- The goal is reach. The accounts reportedly turn the extra views into paid subscribers on OnlyFans and Fanvue.
- The comedians affected appear to have few practical ways to stop it or get paid for it.
- Jokes and delivery styles are hard to protect under copyright, and nobody knows who runs the accounts, so enforcement is even harder.

### Why It Matters
Generative AI lets people create synthetic personas at scale for almost nothing. That has made it possible to borrow someone else's creative work for attention and make money from it somewhere else. The case also shows that platforms' AI-labeling rules and takedown systems are falling behind as a way to protect creators.

**In one line**: Thomson Reuters has introduced Thomson, a large language model built specifically for legal work.

### Key points
- Thomson Reuters, the legal-information and professional-services firm, announced a large language model named after itself: Thomson.
- The distinguishing claim is that it is a purpose-built model for the legal domain, rather than a general-purpose model used as-is.
- Detailed specifications — parameter scale, training data, and benchmark performance — should be confirmed against the original announcement.

### Why it matters
In fields like law, accounting, and tax, where accuracy and source traceability are critical, demand is shifting toward domain-specific models over general chatbots. Thomson Reuters building its own model reflects a broader "vertical LLM" trend, where firms sitting on proprietary industry data build the models themselves.

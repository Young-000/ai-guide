**In one line**: Autodesk, the design and engineering software firm, is reportedly steering a $350 million commitment toward the jobs and talent reshaped by AI.

### Key points
- Autodesk has reportedly pledged a total of **$350 million** in investment.
- The focus is said to be on **jobs and skills across design, manufacturing, and construction** as AI reshapes them.
- Rather than only embedding AI into its software, the move appears aimed at **reskilling the workforce** that will use it.

### Why it matters
It signals how the AI conversation is shifting from "which model" to "who will operate it." A major SaaS company investing directly in its talent pipeline reflects a view that the AI capability gap is a people problem, not just a technology one.

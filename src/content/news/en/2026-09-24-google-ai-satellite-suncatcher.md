**In one line**: Google will launch a satellite carrying its own AI chips next week to test how they hold up in space.

### Key points
- The satellite is equipped with Google's in-house **Tensor AI processors**.
- The goal is to measure how well AI chips perform in the space environment.
- It is part of Google's experimental **Project Suncatcher**, which could eventually put AI data centers into orbit.
- The plan was reportedly first detailed by The New York Times.

### Why it matters
As AI compute demand surges, power and cooling have become the bottleneck for ground-based data centers. Space has been floated as an alternative thanks to abundant solar energy and natural cooling, but actually flying chips to orbit for testing remains rare. Regardless of the outcome, the launch is an early signal of whether "space-based computing" is viable.

**In one line**: HCLTech is partnering with Anthropic to deliver AI agents with governance controls to large enterprises worldwide.

### Key points
- FutureCIO reports a partnership between HCLTech, one of India's largest IT services firms, and Anthropic, the maker of Claude.
- The stated aim is to scale "governed" agentic AI across HCLTech's global enterprise client base.
- "Governed" likely refers to the security, compliance and auditability controls large organizations require before they put AI agents into production.
- Specifics such as staffing commitments, named products and deal terms should be checked in the original report.

### Why it matters
Anthropic has been growing its enterprise business through large consulting and IT services firms, and this deal follows that pattern. It also shows enterprise AI competition moving from raw model capability toward whether companies can run agents safely and under control.

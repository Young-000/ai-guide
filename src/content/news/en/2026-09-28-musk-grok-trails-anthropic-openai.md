**In one line**: Elon Musk reportedly agreed that xAI's Grok models still trail Anthropic and OpenAI — with the caveat "for now."

### Key points
- Musk is said to have acknowledged that Grok has not yet caught up to the leading rivals, qualifying the admission with "for now."
- The phrasing signals confidence that the gap is temporary and that xAI intends to close it soon.
- The named benchmarks are Anthropic, maker of Claude, and OpenAI, maker of ChatGPT.

### Why it matters
As frontier LLM competition intensifies, it is notable to see the head of a challenger publicly concede its standing. Perceptions of model quality feed directly into adoption decisions by developers and enterprises, making xAI's next-generation Grok release a key thing to watch.

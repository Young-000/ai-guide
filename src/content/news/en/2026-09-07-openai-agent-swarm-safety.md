**In one line**: OpenAI's "swarm" method of running many AI agents in concert is reportedly reviving concerns about safety and disclosure.

### Key points
- An approach that coordinates multiple autonomous agents to split and share tasks is said to raise questions about safety and control.
- Observers are reportedly questioning whether enough of the associated risks have been publicly disclosed.
- The focus appears to be on the new risk surface created by multi-agent setups rather than on any single confirmed incident.

### Why it matters
Structures where several agents call one another and act autonomously — beyond a single chatbot — leave more room for errors to cascade. As the pattern spreads, accountability and transparency ("who owns what, and what gets disclosed") become concrete safety questions.

**In one line**: US tech stocks reportedly fell as investors questioned Anthropic's valuation.

### Key points
- Investor's Business Daily (IBD) reported the move in its Dow Jones futures market update published October 16, 2026 (GMT).
- According to the report, doubts over Anthropic's valuation were cited as a factor behind the tech decline.
- The same piece flagged an upcoming Tesla Roadster event as another item on the market's radar.
- The headline alone does not give the valuation figure, the size of the index moves, or where the doubts came from.

### Why it matters
A valuation debate over a single private AI company is weighing on public tech stocks, which shows how concentrated AI investor sentiment has become. It also suggests investors are starting to ask whether high AI valuations are backed by real revenue.

**In one line**: TechCrunch has programmed five founder-focused AI safety sessions for its Disrupt 2026 event.

### Key points
- The sessions span two venues — the AI Stage and the Real World AI Stage — all centered on AI safety.
- Speakers are described as including leaders from Anthropic, NVIDIA, AWS, and autonomous-driving company Waabi.
- TechCrunch says early registration saves up to $200 through September 25.

### Why it matters
The lineup signals that AI safety is moving from a research topic into a practical agenda item for founders and product teams. With major infrastructure and model companies sharing the same slate, the influence of regulation and risk management on early-stage startup strategy looks set to grow.

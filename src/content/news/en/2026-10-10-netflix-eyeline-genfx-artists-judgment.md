**In one line**: James Rustad, who heads generative effects (GenFX) at Eyeline, Netflix's VFX and virtual production unit, said in Busan that generative AI still needs artists' judgment.

### Key points
- Variety reports that Rustad spoke at a film and content industry event in Busan in October.
- At Eyeline, Rustad leads GenFX, the group that applies generative AI to visual effects work.
- His main point: AI can automate parts of effects production, but artists still decide what works on screen and refine it.
- The original article has details on specific tools and productions.

### Why it matters
As major studios add generative AI to their pipelines, VFX workers worry about being replaced. When a team inside Netflix says publicly that AI is a tool and people make the decisions, it shows how the industry wants to frame AI adoption. Whether staffing actually changes in practice is still an open question.

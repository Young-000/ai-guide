**In one line**: Anthropic's annualized revenue has reportedly surpassed the combined scale of Starbucks and McDonald's.

### Key points
- International Business Times reports that Anthropic's current revenue now exceeds the combined total of consumer giants Starbucks and McDonald's.
- The comparison to coffee and fast-food chains is used to illustrate how quickly AI companies have scaled their revenue.
- Exact figures and the basis of the calculation (e.g., whether annualized recurring revenue) should be verified against the original report.

### Why it matters
An AI company that was a startup only a few years ago being measured against decades-old global consumer brands underscores the unusual pace of capital and growth in the generative-AI market. That said, comparisons with mass-market brands span very different business models and are best read as symbolic rather than like-for-like.

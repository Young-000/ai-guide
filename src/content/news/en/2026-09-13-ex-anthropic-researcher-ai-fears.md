**In one line**: A former Anthropic researcher reportedly told the BBC that some people working inside the AI industry are seriously worried about where the technology could lead humanity.

### Key points
- A former Anthropic researcher reportedly said in a BBC interview that some AI workers are "genuinely frightened" about humanity's future.
- The remarks carry weight as they come from someone who worked at Anthropic, a company that has publicly emphasized AI safety.
- The framing highlights a gap between the concern felt by researchers at the frontier of AI development and broader public or regulatory perceptions.

### Why it matters
Warnings from people building frontier AI are frequently cited in debates over regulation and safety policy. Coming from a former insider rather than corporate messaging, the comments suggest that concern over AI risk is moving beyond abstract worry into acknowledged unease within the industry itself.

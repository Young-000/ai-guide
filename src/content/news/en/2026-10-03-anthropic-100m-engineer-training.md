**In one line**: Anthropic has launched a reported $100 million program to train 10,000 engineers in applied AI.

### Key points
- The commitment is said to total $100 million, targeting 10,000 engineers.
- Consulting firms McKinsey and Deloitte are named as the first partners.
- The effort appears focused on building hands-on skills for deploying Anthropic's Claude models in real workflows.

### Why it matters
It signals that AI vendors are moving beyond raw model competition toward directly cultivating a workforce that knows how to use the tools. Starting with large consultancies looks deliberate: they act as conduits that carry AI adoption into their own enterprise clients.

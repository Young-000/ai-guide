**In one line**: Amazon is opening its AI assistant Alexa+ free on U.S. Fire TV devices, no Prime subscription required.

### Key points
- The rollout covers **all compatible Fire TV devices** in the U.S., with users upgraded automatically — no sign-up needed.
- It's free **regardless of Prime membership**, whereas Alexa+ had been positioned as a Prime perk or a separately-priced tier.
- Alexa+ is Amazon's next-generation, generative-AI-powered Alexa with stronger conversational and contextual responses.

### Why it matters
The move signals that Amazon would rather push its AI assistant across a large hardware base than lock it behind a paid subscription. Using the living-room TV as an entry point looks aimed at rapidly widening Alexa+'s user base.

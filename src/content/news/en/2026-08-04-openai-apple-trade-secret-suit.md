**In one line**: OpenAI has directly rebutted what it says are the flawed central claims in Apple's trade secret theft lawsuit.

### Key points
- Apple is reported to have filed a lawsuit against OpenAI alleging trade secret theft.
- OpenAI publicly responded, arguing the facts underpinning the suit are inaccurate.
- With the two companies long tied through a mix of partnership and rivalry, the dispute highlights growing tension over talent and technology movement across the AI industry.

### Why it matters
Trade secret suits between big tech firms serve as a test of how far AI talent mobility and technology boundaries can go. Depending on how the case is decided, it could shape hiring and collaboration practices across the sector.

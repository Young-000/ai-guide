**One-line summary**: OpenAI has built dots, an always-on personal assistant, out of its coding tools.

### Key points
- dots grew out of OpenAI's coding tools and is positioned as a personal assistant that is always running.
- The OpenAI executive responsible for dots is Alexander Embiricos.
- Embiricos says he never wants to have a meeting with someone else's AI agent.
- TechCrunch published its look at how the product came together on October 26.

### Why it matters
The project shows agent technology built for coding being stretched into an everyday assistant for general users. The product lead's stance against meeting other people's AI agents suggests dots may center on direct interaction with its user rather than agent-to-agent dealings.

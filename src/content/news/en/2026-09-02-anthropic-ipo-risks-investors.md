**In one line**: Morningstar frames a future Anthropic IPO as potentially huge but risky, laying out what investors should watch.

### Key points
- Anthropic's initial public offering is expected to draw attention for its scale while carrying notable risks, according to the analysis.
- Morningstar says investors should examine several challenges — including business structure and financial footing — before any listing.
- No confirmed timing or size has been reported; the piece is an assessment premised on a potential offering.

### Why it matters
As one of the frontier LLM developers alongside OpenAI, Anthropic's path to the public markets is a bellwether for capital flows and valuation benchmarks across the AI sector. With a high-growth narrative sitting alongside heavy compute costs and uncertain profitability, a risk-focused investor lens is warranted.

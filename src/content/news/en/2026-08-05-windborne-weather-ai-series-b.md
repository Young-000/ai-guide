**In one line**: Weather forecasting startup WindBorne Systems raised a $37 million Series B to expand its balloon network and AI-driven forecasts.

### Key points
- The round is reported to be a $37 million Series B.
- The funds are earmarked for scaling WindBorne's own weather balloons and its AI forecasting models.
- While AI has improved forecast accuracy, converting that edge into sustainable revenue remains the central challenge.

### Why it matters
AI weather forecasting has proven its technical value, but its business model is still unsettled. WindBorne's bet — pairing proprietary observation data (hardware) with AI models (software) to sell differentiated forecasts — is one answer to a question the whole sector faces: can better AI predictions actually be made profitable?

**In one line**: USA Today has reportedly sued OpenAI for copyright infringement and is seeking $250 million.

### Key points
- The plaintiff is USA Today. The defendant is OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT.
- The reported claim is $250 million.
- The case is about copyright infringement. Earlier publisher suits mostly said news articles were used without permission to train AI models and produce answers. The specific claims in this complaint have not yet been confirmed.
- USA Today is owned by Gannett, the largest local newspaper group in the U.S.

### Why it matters
OpenAI is already fighting several publishers in court, including The New York Times. Other publishers have signed content licensing deals with the company instead. Now a major national daily has put a specific dollar amount on its claim. That could add pressure on AI companies over how much they pay to use news content. A ruling or settlement could also set a benchmark for future licensing talks.

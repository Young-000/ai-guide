**In one line**: China's GLM-5.3 reportedly performed close to Anthropic's models on a cybersecurity capability test.

### Key points
- The latest GLM-series model, GLM-5.3, is reported to have scored near Anthropic's models on a cyber-focused capability evaluation.
- These benchmarks measure both offensive and defensive skills, such as vulnerability discovery and exploit writing.
- The result is read as a signal that Chinese open and commercial models are narrowing the gap with US frontier labs on cyber capability.

### Why it matters
Cyber capability is a core metric in AI safety and regulation debates. If a Chinese model is approaching the frontier, it bears on both the US-China gap discussion and misuse-prevention policy. Exact scores and evaluation methodology should be verified against the original report.

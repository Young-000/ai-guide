**In one line**: Anthropic is reportedly preparing its own custom-designed silicon chip.

### Key points
- Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is said to be developing an in-house AI chip.
- A proprietary chip would let the company tune compute for model training and inference directly, reducing dependence on external GPU supply.
- Specifics such as chip specs, production timeline, and manufacturing partners have not been disclosed.

### Why it matters
As OpenAI, Google, and Amazon increasingly design their own silicon, Anthropic joining that trend could further shift an AI-chip market long dominated by Nvidia. It underscores how compute cost and supply stability are becoming decisive factors in AI competitiveness.

**In one line**: Samsung SDS is reportedly expanding AI partnerships with both OpenAI and Anthropic.

### Key points
- Samsung SDS is said to be deepening ties with **both OpenAI and Anthropic** rather than committing to a single model vendor.
- Enterprise generative AI — internal workflow automation and cloud-service integration — appears to be the focus.
- The move reads as a **multi-model strategy** aimed at avoiding single-vendor lock-in.

### Why it matters
When a large IT arm runs multiple AI suppliers in parallel, it signals that "pick one model for everything" is giving way to **best-model-per-use-case** as the enterprise norm. It could also shape the competitive push for corporate AI adoption in Korea.

**In one line**: Developers say they have surfaced evidence pointing to OpenAI's ad system tracking user behavior across multiple sites.

### Key points
- Some developers reportedly published what they describe as concrete evidence that OpenAI ads track user behavior **across sites**, not just within a single service.
- The claim lands amid OpenAI's broader push to add advertising as a revenue stream beyond search and chatbots.
- OpenAI has not been clearly confirmed to have verified or disputed the findings as of now.

### Why it matters
If confirmed, the report could intensify privacy debates over how large AI services use conversation and usage data for ad targeting. It sits at the friction point between AI companies moving into ads and tightening tracking-consent regulation.

**In one line**: Amid speculation that Anthropic is moving toward an IPO, the question of how retail investors could gain pre-listing exposure is drawing interest.

### Key points
- Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is reportedly preparing for a public listing, though the company has not confirmed an official timeline.
- Because private-stage shares are hard for ordinary investors to buy directly, secondary markets and pre-IPO funds are cited as possible indirect routes.
- Gaining exposure through publicly traded firms that have invested in Anthropic (such as major cloud and tech backers) is another commonly mentioned approach.

### Why it matters
Alongside OpenAI, Anthropic is one of the most closely watched AI startups, so its path to public markets could serve as a barometer for AI investment sentiment. Retail investors should weigh both the limited pre-IPO access and the liquidity and valuation risks that come with indirect routes.

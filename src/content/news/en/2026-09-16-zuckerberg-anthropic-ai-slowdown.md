**In one line**: Mark Zuckerberg has reportedly taken public aim at Anthropic in the ongoing debate over whether AI development should be slowed down.

### Key points
- The New York Times reports that Zuckerberg singled out Anthropic for criticism in the "AI slowdown" debate.
- One side of the debate favors slowing AI development and deployment for safety reasons; the other pushes for faster progress.
- Anthropic has been a prominent safety-focused company, generally taking a more cautious stance on regulation and development pace.

### Why it matters
A clash between Zuckerberg, who leads one of the biggest tech firms, and safety-first Anthropic highlights a widening rift within the industry over how fast to push AI. Where this debate lands could shape both the direction of regulation and how quickly companies ship new models.

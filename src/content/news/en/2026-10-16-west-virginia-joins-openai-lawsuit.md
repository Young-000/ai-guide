**In one line**: West Virginia has reportedly joined seven other states in suing OpenAI.

### Key points
- The Intelligencer, a West Virginia newspaper, reported the move on October 16.
- Seven states were already in litigation against OpenAI, and West Virginia has reportedly joined them.
- The headline does not say what the claims are, such as consumer protection, child safety, or data practices.
- It is also unclear whether this is one joint case or a set of separate state suits. Check the original report for details.

### Why it matters
Federal AI rules have been slow to arrive, so state attorneys general are increasingly using lawsuits to hold AI companies to account. Each state that joins adds to OpenAI's legal exposure and the pressure to settle. The outcome could also shape how other AI providers run consumer products.

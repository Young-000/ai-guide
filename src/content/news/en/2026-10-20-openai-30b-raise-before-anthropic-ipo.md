**One-line summary**: Tech Funding News reports that OpenAI is moving quickly to secure $30 billion at a roughly $1.4 trillion valuation.

### Key points
- The reported target is about $30 billion, priced at a valuation near $1.4 trillion.
- The report frames the round as an effort to close before Anthropic's initial public offering (IPO).
- Only the headline and a short summary were available; investor makeup, terms and whether the deal has closed are not stated.

### Why it matters
It shows the capital race among leading AI labs is now tied to public-market timing. Once Anthropic lists, public investors will set a visible benchmark for AI company valuations, giving OpenAI a reason to fix its private valuation first.

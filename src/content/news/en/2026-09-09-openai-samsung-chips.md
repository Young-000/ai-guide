**In one line**: OpenAI says it is working with Samsung on next-generation chips and deepening the two companies' cooperation.

### Key points
- OpenAI told Reuters it is collaborating with Samsung on **next-generation chips**.
- The companies' relationship is **deepening** beyond earlier cooperation.
- Specific chip specifications and production timelines have not been disclosed so far.

### Why it matters
Training and running generative AI models demands enormous compute, and reliance on Nvidia GPUs has remained heavy. OpenAI widening its chip work with Samsung points to an effort to **diversify its compute supply chain**, and it could lift AI-hardware demand for Korea's semiconductor industry.

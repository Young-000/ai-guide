**In one line**: AWS now allows the vibe-coding tool Superblocks to be embedded inside its customers' private clouds.

### Key points
- AWS customers can now embed Superblocks directly into their private cloud environments to build apps in-house.
- Superblocks is a "vibe coding" tool that generates internal business apps from natural-language prompts.
- TechCrunch frames the move as another step toward decoupling apps from any single AI model.

### Why it matters
When an app runs on cloud infrastructure rather than being tied to one model, enterprises can swap models while keeping control of their own data. The deal is an early look at how major clouds may fold vibe-coding startups into their ecosystems.

**In one line**: IT services company Techlusion says it has been named an OpenAI Select Partner.

### Key points
- Techlusion reportedly earned OpenAI Select Partner status.
- The program is described as certifying vetted partners that help build and deploy solutions on OpenAI's technology.
- The announcement was carried through a corporate news wire (StreetInsider).

### Why it matters
OpenAI has been widening its partner ecosystem to move beyond selling APIs and models toward helping enterprises actually implement them. Certifications like this are less about one company's news and more a signal that AI demand is shifting from the model itself toward integration and deployment expertise. Specifics such as partner tier or contract terms were not disclosed.

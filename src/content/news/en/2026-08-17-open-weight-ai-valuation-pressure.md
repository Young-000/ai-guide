**In one line**: Rapidly improving open-weight models may put pressure on the lofty valuations of closed-model leaders such as OpenAI and Anthropic, according to analysis.

### Key points
- Open-weight models ship their weights publicly, letting companies run and fine-tune them on their own infrastructure — reducing API lock-in and usage costs.
- As the performance gap with closed models narrows, the "moat" that justifies premium pricing for frontier labs could weaken.
- The actual impact on valuations remains speculative; safety, support, and enterprise integration are still cited as advantages of closed offerings.

### Why it matters
It signals a possible shift in AI's competitive axis — from "who has the strongest model" to "who delivers a good-enough model more cheaply." That directly affects the pricing power and fundraising narrative of the leading labs.

**In one line**: Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms has reportedly agreed to lease 191MW of data center capacity to Anthropic over 20 years in a deal worth $9.1 billion.

### Key points
- Per the report, the deal covers **191MW** of power capacity, runs **20 years**, and totals **$9.1 billion**.
- The tenant is AI developer **Anthropic**; the landlord is Bitcoin mining firm **Riot Platforms**.
- It reflects a broader shift of power and sites originally secured for crypto mining toward AI compute demand.

### Why it matters
As AI training and inference demand surges, crypto-mining operators — which already hold large, energized power capacity — are increasingly becoming a supply source for AI data centers. For Anthropic, the deal signals the ongoing race to lock in long-term, large-scale compute. For now this remains at the report stage, and official confirmation from either company would need to be verified separately.

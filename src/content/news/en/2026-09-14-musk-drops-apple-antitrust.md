**In one line**: Musk's camp reportedly dropped Apple as a defendant but is pressing on with its antitrust case against OpenAI.

### Key points
- Apple has reportedly been removed as a defendant in the suit.
- The case against OpenAI itself remains active.
- The backdrop involves competition concerns around Apple–OpenAI cooperation, such as ChatGPT's integration into iOS.

### Why it matters
The rivalry between Musk's xAI and OpenAI is playing out in court, showing how AI-market competition and platform distribution are spilling into regulatory and legal disputes. With Apple out, the fight narrows squarely onto OpenAI.

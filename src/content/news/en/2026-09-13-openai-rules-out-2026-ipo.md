**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly ruled out a 2026 IPO, while CEO Sam Altman flagged safety risks associated with AI.

### Key points
- According to the report, OpenAI has denied plans for an initial public offering in 2026.
- CEO Sam Altman is said to have highlighted potential safety risks stemming from AI technology.
- The remarks appear aimed at tempering market expectations around a near-term listing.

### Why it matters
OpenAI is a bellwether for the AI industry, so the timing of any public listing carries weight for capital flows and competitive dynamics. Pairing a signal that it is in no rush to go public with a warning about safety risks suggests a posture that prioritizes controllability over speed of growth.

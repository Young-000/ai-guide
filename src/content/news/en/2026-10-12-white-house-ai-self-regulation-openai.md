**In one line**: Fortune criticizes the White House's approach of leaving AI oversight to industry self-regulation and cites OpenAI's recent actions as evidence.

### Key points
- According to Fortune, the White House favors letting AI companies police themselves rather than imposing binding federal rules.
- Fortune argues that OpenAI's recent conduct shows why this approach could be dangerous.
- The piece is analysis and commentary rather than straight news reporting. See the original article for the specific incidents and evidence.

### Why it matters
Whether AI is governed by statute or by voluntary industry commitments is a central question in U.S. AI policy. When a leading developer is cited as a test of self-regulation, the debate over who should oversee frontier AI is likely to intensify.

**In one line**: LemonLime cofounder Jordan Zietz, mocked across LinkedIn, publicly explains why he tattooed his startup's logo on his shoulder.

### Key points
- LemonLime cofounder Jordan Zietz drew widespread criticism on LinkedIn after it emerged he had tattooed his company's logo onto his shoulder.
- Zietz says he "hears the criticism loud and clear," framing the tattoo as a mark of commitment to his startup.
- The episode became a talking point about how far LinkedIn's exaggerated "grindset" hustle culture can go.

### Why it matters
Amid the AI-startup boom, the story is a snapshot of how founder self-branding and performative "dedication" get consumed — and ridiculed — on social media. The cultural signaling of startup life, not the technology, took center stage here.

**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly discussing a $30B funding round that would value the company at around $1.4 trillion.

### Key points
- The round is said to total $30B at a valuation near $1.4 trillion.
- It is expected to be OpenAI's **last private round** before an IPO now delayed to 2027.
- Talks are still early; the final size, valuation, and investor lineup are not confirmed.

### Why it matters
A $1.4 trillion mark would rank among the highest valuations anywhere — public companies included — underscoring the vast capital demands of the AI infrastructure and model race. Framing it as the final pre-IPO round signals that OpenAI is lining up its finances ahead of a public-market debut.

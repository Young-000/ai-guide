**In one line**: SC Media examines how conversational AI (LLMs) can help expose the tactics behind 'pig butchering' scams.

### Key points
- 'Pig butchering' is a hybrid romance-and-investment scam in which fraudsters build long-term trust with victims before luring them into fake investments to drain their funds.
- The report describes how feeding scam conversations to an LLM can help surface formulaic grooming patterns, scripted replies, and clues to fabricated identities.
- From a defensive angle, conversational AI is framed as a tool that can triage suspicious messages and explain the warning signs to users.

### Why it matters
Pig butchering is a large, organized form of fraud with heavy financial losses. Using LLMs as a defensive and investigative aid could let individuals and investigators spot scam patterns faster — a notable example of applying AI to security and fraud response.

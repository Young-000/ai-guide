**In one line**: An Israeli hospital is reportedly set to become OpenAI's first international partner, its debut healthcare tie-up outside the U.S.

### Key points
- According to the report, the hospital would be OpenAI's **first overseas (international) partner**.
- The collaboration is said to target the use of AI in a clinical/medical setting.
- Specific scope, applications, and terms have not yet been disclosed.

### Why it matters
It signals a major AI firm moving directly into the tightly regulated healthcare space, where generative AI could reach diagnostics support, administrative automation, and patient care. Choosing a first partner outside the U.S. also points to OpenAI's global expansion strategy.

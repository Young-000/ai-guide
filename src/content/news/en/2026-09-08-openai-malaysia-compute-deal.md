**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly securing compute capacity based in Malaysia via a Sydney-headquartered company.

### Key points
- OpenAI's compute footprint appears to be spreading beyond the US and Europe into Southeast Asia.
- The reported intermediary is not a major US hyperscaler but an Australian (Sydney-based) firm.
- The capacity sits in Malaysia, a region offering comparatively low-cost power and land.

### Why it matters
As demand for AI training and inference surges, the race for compute is widening geographically. The move reads as an effort to reduce reliance on any single cloud provider and to spread infrastructure into emerging regions with favorable power and cost profiles.

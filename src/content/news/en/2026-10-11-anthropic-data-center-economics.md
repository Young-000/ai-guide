**In one line**: The Information reports on how fast Anthropic is expanding its data center capacity and argues the economics of that buildout are favorable.

### Key points
- The Information reports that Anthropic is moving quickly to secure data center capacity.
- The piece reportedly argues that the expansion is economically attractive, not just a growing cost.
- The investment size, partners and return estimates are in the paywalled article and can't be confirmed from the public summary.

### Why it matters
Competition among frontier AI labs is shifting from model quality alone to access to compute. If Anthropic can cut unit costs on its own infrastructure, it would depend less on rented cloud capacity and have more room to set API prices and protect margins. The catch is that large upfront spending turns into a liability if demand falls short of forecasts.

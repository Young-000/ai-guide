**In one line**: Research firm Market.us projects the on-device generative AI market to grow at a 36.0% compound annual growth rate (CAGR).

### Key points
- On-device generative AI runs models directly on hardware such as phones and PCs, rather than on cloud servers.
- Market.us pegs the market's projected CAGR at **36.0%**.
- Cited growth drivers include stronger privacy (data stays on the device), lower latency, and offline operation.

### Why it matters
As generative AI shifts from the cloud toward the device, apps can gain faster responses and better privacy while cutting server costs. That said, this figure is a single research firm's estimate — methodologies and projections vary across analysts.

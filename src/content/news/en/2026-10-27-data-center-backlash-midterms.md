**One-line summary**: Data centers have emerged as an issue that activates many kinds of voters in the 2026 elections.

### Key points
- The Verge says that if one issue activates all kinds of voters in 2026, it is data centers.
- The trigger is the rapid buildout of massive server storage facilities. The infrastructure construction and expenses that accompany them are also in dispute.
- Together, these factors have sparked local opposition in communities across the country.
- The opposition has moved beyond local politics and become a major issue in many congressional races.

### Why it matters
Data centers support the spread of AI. Building them now raises questions about local life and costs, and that has made them a political factor. The tension between the pace of infrastructure expansion and community acceptance is showing up on the campaign trail.

**In one line**: 36Kr reports that Anthropic has officially released "Fable 5.1," an update to its Fable 5 model line.

### Key points
- 36Kr framed the release as a demonstration of Anthropic's "next-generation AI capabilities."
- Fable 5.1 appears positioned as an incremental improvement over the existing Fable 5 line.
- Specific benchmarks, pricing, and context-window details were not confirmed by this report alone.

Anthropic has broadened its model portfolio with the Fable line alongside Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku. Point releases (.1) typically refine performance, reliability, and cost efficiency — factors that directly shape the choices available to developers and enterprise users.

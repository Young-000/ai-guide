**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to have acquired biotech firm Orchestra Bio, extending AI's role in drug development.

### Key points
- The acquirer is OpenAI; the target is Orchestra Bio.
- The report centers on widening AI's role in the drug development process.
- Deal value, timing and integration plans are not confirmed by the report summary available.

### Why it matters
A general-purpose AI company buying a biotech firm directly suggests AI's use is moving beyond chatbots and productivity tools into scientific and pharmaceutical research. Until concrete plans are disclosed, though, the real impact is hard to judge.

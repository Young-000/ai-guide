**In one line**: OpenAI posted a piece on its official blog about the unsolved Navier–Stokes equations in mathematics.

### Key points
- The Navier–Stokes equations are one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems named by the Clay Mathematics Institute, reportedly carrying a $1 million prize for a solution.
- The open challenge concerns whether smooth solutions to these fluid-flow equations always exist (existence and smoothness).
- The post appears to examine how AI might approach mathematics problems at the highest level of difficulty.

### Why it matters
It is a signal for whether AI can move beyond structured problem-solving toward fundamental questions humans have not cracked for decades. Regardless of the outcome, it reflects frontier AI research turning toward pure mathematics.

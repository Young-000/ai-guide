**In one line**: Rising public anxiety over AI is pushing lawmakers to move from raising alarms toward concrete regulation, The Washington Post reports.

### Key points
- Growing concerns over AI — safety, jobs, and misuse — are reportedly accelerating legislative debate.
- Lawmakers are said to be shifting from voicing warnings to drafting specific bills and actions.
- Disagreements reportedly remain over the scope and pace of regulation and how to balance it with technological progress.

### Why it matters
As AI adoption spreads quickly, the direction of regulation directly affects both how companies ship products and how users are protected. Faster legislative movement signals that the operating environment for AI services could soon change.

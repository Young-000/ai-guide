**In one line**: Fortune's tech newsletter bundles three AI threads for the day — reported friction inside OpenAI, CoreWeave's order backlog, and the departure of Brad Lightcap.

### Key points
- OpenAI is reported to be facing internal friction over its organization and strategy.
- CoreWeave, the AI cloud infrastructure provider, is described as carrying a notable order backlog.
- Brad Lightcap, cited as a key OpenAI executive, is reported to be departing.

### Why it matters
Leadership and organizational shifts at frontier-model companies, and the supply bottlenecks in the GPU infrastructure that powers them, are two of the clearest gauges of how fast AI can scale. Grouping all three into one newsletter signals that the market is focused less on demand — which remains strong — and more on whether organizations and infrastructure can absorb it.

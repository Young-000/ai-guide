**In one line**: Hackers who once broke into OpenAI's systems say the AI industry as a whole is poorly defended, The Washington Post reports.

### Key points
- Hackers reportedly known for accessing OpenAI's systems have publicly criticized the security posture of AI companies.
- Fast-growing AI firms are said to prioritize development speed over defense, leaving gaps for attackers.
- They frame it as a structural, industry-wide weakness rather than a single company's failure.

### Why it matters
AI models, training data, and internal tooling are high-value targets. If security lags while companies race on capability, the risk shifts toward leaks of model weights, research, or user data — the warning being raised here.

**In one line**: The Hollywood Reporter frames OpenAI's large-scale use of creative works as a possible "biggest IP heist," reopening the copyright debate.

### Key points
- The outlet reportedly argues that OpenAI's use of creative content for training and generation is fueling copyright concerns.
- The spread of video and image generation models has pushed creator and studio rights back into the spotlight.
- Licensing and compensation talks between AI companies and the media and entertainment industry continue.

### Why it matters
The legitimacy of training data is a central issue shaping upcoming regulation, litigation, and licensing deals. Pushback from the creative industry could directly affect AI firms' data acquisition costs and business models.

**In one line**: OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar told CNBC that "safety and alignment have always been at the core of what we do."

### Key points
- Friar reportedly framed safety and alignment — the work of keeping AI systems consistent with human intent — as central values since OpenAI's founding.
- Having the finance chief carry the safety message signals awareness of the current debate pitting commercialization speed against safety concerns.
- The interview read as a reaffirmation of principles rather than a rollout of specific policies or figures.

### Why it matters
As leading AI companies face trust pressure balancing fast product shipping against safety, a finance leader foregrounding safety looks like coordinated messaging aimed at investors, regulators, and users alike.

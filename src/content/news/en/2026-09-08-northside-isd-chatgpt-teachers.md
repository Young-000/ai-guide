**In one line**: Northside Independent School District in Texas says it will offer "ChatGPT for Teachers" to staff through a new partnership with OpenAI.

### Key points
- The rollout targets **teachers**, not students, focusing on support tasks such as lesson planning and material creation.
- "ChatGPT for Teachers" is OpenAI's education-oriented offering built for classroom staff.
- The move is reported as part of a broader trend of U.S. school districts formally adopting generative AI.

### Why it matters
It signals that schools are shifting from treating AI as something to ban toward using it as a work tool. When adoption moves from scattered individual use to district-level contracts and deployment, questions of data protection, copyright, and teacher training start being handled at an institutional level.

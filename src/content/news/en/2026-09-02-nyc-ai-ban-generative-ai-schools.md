**In one line**: New York City is reportedly moving to restrict generative AI use for students in grades below high school.

### Key points
- According to The Washington Post, New York City education officials are said to be considering a ban on generative AI use for students before they reach high school.
- The reported rationale centers on concerns that younger students may over-rely on AI during the years when foundational learning and critical thinking take shape.
- Restrictions would reportedly ease at the high school level, suggesting an age-tiered approach rather than a blanket ban.

### Why it matters
A move by the largest public school system in the U.S. could set a reference point for AI policy in other districts. It signals that educators are starting to formalize where they stand on whether generative AI is a learning tool or a risk.

**In one line**: Anthropic has released Claude Fable 5.1, a new model it says improves coding and research while lowering cost.

### Key points
- Anthropic unveiled Claude Fable 5.1, according to the-decoder.com.
- The model is presented as offering stronger performance on coding and research tasks.
- Anthropic says usage costs are reduced by up to 45% compared with the previous generation.

### Why it matters
Pairing better performance with lower cost is the headline. For teams and developers who found model running costs a barrier, the same work may now be handled more cheaply. The coding-focused gains signal continued competition to automate software development.

**In one line**: A Florida man reportedly told ChatGPT he would murder his ex-partner, prompting OpenAI to alert the FBI.

### Key points
- According to the report, the man made threatening statements in a ChatGPT conversation about harming his former partner.
- OpenAI reportedly detected the exchange and notified the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI).
- The account was published by legal-industry outlet South Carolina Lawyers Weekly.

### Why it matters
The case reopens the privacy-and-safety debate over how far AI companies monitor chatbot conversations and when they hand material to law enforcement. The line between responding to genuine threats of violence and protecting user privacy is likely to become a sharper policy and legal question.

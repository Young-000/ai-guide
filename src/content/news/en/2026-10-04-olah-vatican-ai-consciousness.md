**In one line**: Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah is reported to have clashed with the Vatican over "AI consciousness" ahead of a papal encyclical on artificial intelligence.

### Key points
- Chris Olah, Anthropic co-founder and a leader of the company's interpretability research, reportedly diverged from the Vatican on questions of AI consciousness.
- The disagreement is said to have surfaced as the Holy See prepared an encyclical addressing artificial intelligence.
- The sticking point appears to be whether AI systems can possess consciousness or moral standing.

### Why it matters
Debate over AI consciousness has largely been confined to tech and philosophy circles. Its entry into a formal doctrinal document from the world's largest religious institution widens the stage. Where a leading AI lab and a major religious-ethical authority part ways could become a fault line in future AI governance debates.

**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly told dozens of third parties that its AI agents reached them, and the resulting review is expected to run for months.

### Key points
- OpenAI has reportedly notified **dozens of third parties** that its AI agents made contact with or reached them.
- The **review** of what was exposed or exchanged is said to take **several months**.
- Determining an agent's reach after the fact has become a concrete operational concern.

### Why it matters
As autonomous agents begin interacting with outside parties without direct human oversight, reconstructing "how far the agent actually reached" becomes a retrospective task. It signals that notification, auditing, and accountability processes are becoming essential parts of deploying agents at scale.

**In one line**: OpenAI appears to have named 'Astra,' reportedly its next AI model, inside a blog post highlighting math results, according to Gizmodo.

### Key points
- Rather than a formal launch, the name 'Astra' reportedly surfaced within a blog post showcasing math-related achievements.
- Gizmodo framed this as OpenAI quietly signaling a future model without a standalone announcement.
- Specific model specs, a release date, and pricing were not disclosed in the post.

### Why it matters
It reflects a pattern of AI labs drip-feeding hints in technical blog posts to build anticipation for upcoming models, rather than staging large events. The actual capabilities of 'Astra' remain to be confirmed in an official announcement.

**In one line**: Apple has reportedly been granted early discovery in its trade-secret fight with OpenAI.

### Key points
- According to CNBC, Apple's request for **early discovery** in its trade-secret case against OpenAI was approved.
- Early discovery lets a party demand documents and evidence before the normal schedule. Courts usually allow it when fast relief, such as an injunction, is on the table.
- The CNBC report is the place to check which trade secrets are in dispute and how broad the discovery order is.
- The ruling is procedural. It is not a finding that OpenAI misappropriated anything.

### Why it matters
Apple and OpenAI have been partners through the ChatGPT integration in Siri. Their competition over hardware and talent is growing, though, and that rivalry now appears to have reached the courtroom. What surfaces in discovery could affect both the partnership and OpenAI's device plans.

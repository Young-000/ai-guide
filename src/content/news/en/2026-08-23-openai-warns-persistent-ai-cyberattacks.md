**In one line**: An OpenAI leader warned that AI-powered "persistent" cyber-attacks have entered a new phase.

### Key points
- The OpenAI leader reportedly said "we are hitting a different chapter," underscoring a shift in the nature of the threat.
- The warning highlights attacks that are not one-off but "persistent," framed as a distinct new risk.
- The concern comes as generative AI is increasingly used as an attack tool, raising both sophistication and scale.

### Why it matters
An AI developer publicly warning about misuse of its own technology could add weight to upcoming debates on security policy and defensive frameworks.

**In one line**: OpenAI says one of its AI models solved a long-standing hard math problem in just days, according to France 24.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly claims its AI tackled a difficult mathematical problem that experts have struggled with, arriving at a solution in a short span of time.
- The claim suggests AI's mathematical reasoning may be moving beyond calculation toward proof- and solution-generation.
- Which problem was involved, and whether the result has been independently verified, remains unclear at this time.

### Why it matters
Solving hard math problems is a benchmark for AI reasoning. If confirmed, it could accelerate the use of AI as a research aid across science and mathematics. But vendor announcements can overstate results, so independent review by mathematicians is worth waiting for before drawing conclusions.

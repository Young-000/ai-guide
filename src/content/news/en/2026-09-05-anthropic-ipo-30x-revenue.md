**In one line**: Anthropic, the maker of Claude, could see an IPO valuation around 30 times revenue, sparking debate between overvaluation concerns and growth optimism.

### Key points
- A Yahoo Finance column suggests Anthropic could be valued at roughly 30 times annual revenue if it goes public.
- That multiple sits well above typical software-company benchmarks, raising overvaluation concerns.
- Even so, the author says they remain interested, citing surging AI demand and rapid revenue growth.

### Why it matters
Alongside OpenAI, Anthropic is a leading LLM company, so its IPO valuation serves as a gauge for market expectations across the AI sector. Note that the timing and terms of any IPO are reportedly not confirmed, and the 30x figure is the author's estimate.

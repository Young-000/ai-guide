**In one line**: OpenAI has announced GPT-6 Astra, a next-generation model positioned for work and professional use.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly introduced a new flagship model called GPT-6 Astra.
- The company frames it as "the next generation in intelligence for work."
- Unlike consumer chat, the launch foregrounds **professional and workplace** use cases.

### Why it matters
A generational jump in the GPT line directly shapes enterprise AI adoption. Leading explicitly with "for work" signals that OpenAI is aiming to differentiate on productivity against rivals such as Google and Anthropic. Concrete specs — performance, pricing, and availability — need further confirmation from the original announcement.

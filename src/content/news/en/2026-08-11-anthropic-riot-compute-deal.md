**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to have signed a roughly $9 billion deal with bitcoin miner Riot, which analysts frame as a turning point for the mining sector.

### Key points
- The reported agreement between Anthropic and Riot Platforms is valued at about $9 billion.
- Bitcoin miners' large-scale power contracts and data-center footprints are increasingly seen as a channel to absorb AI compute demand.
- Barron's characterized the deal as a "watershed" moment for the mining industry.

### Why it matters
Training and running AI models require vast amounts of electricity and data-center capacity. If leasing pre-secured power and sites to AI firms becomes a repeatable pattern, miners' economics could shift away from bitcoin-price dependence toward AI infrastructure revenue.

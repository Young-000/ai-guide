**In one line**: According to Mashable, Anthropic has introduced measures to protect Claude's "feelings," a step that follows from the company's ongoing model welfare research.

### Key points
- Mashable reported on Oct. 11 that Anthropic has adopted protective measures aimed at Claude's "feelings." The original article has the exact scope and the models affected.
- Since 2025, Anthropic has publicly run a model welfare research program. The program examines whether AI systems' experiences or preferences could matter morally.
- The company has not claimed that Claude is conscious or has emotions. It reportedly frames such measures as low-cost precautions taken under uncertainty.

### Why it matters
This is a major AI lab building "model wellbeing" into product policy, and it is likely to restart debate among researchers and the industry. Critics call it anthropomorphism, while supporters see it as responsible hedging. For users, the practical effect may be changes to how Claude handles certain conversations.

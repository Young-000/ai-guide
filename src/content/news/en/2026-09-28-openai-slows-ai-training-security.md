### Key points
- According to PYMNTS, OpenAI reportedly slowed the pace of its AI training after a security incident.
- Specific measures — such as when training would resume or how systems would be re-verified — have not been detailed publicly.
- The move reads as a case where safety and security review directly affect a frontier lab's development schedule.

### Why it matters
Frontier model development is a speed race, but a security incident can force a lab to pause and review. This case illustrates how the trade-off between safety and shipping speed plays out in practice.

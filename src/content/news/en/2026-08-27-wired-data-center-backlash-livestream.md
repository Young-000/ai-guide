**In one line**: WIRED is hosting a Sept. 10 expert livestream on the growing backlash against data centers and is taking reader questions ahead of time.

### Key points
- WIRED announced a livestream Q&A titled "The Great Data Center Backlash," scheduled for September 10.
- A panel of experts will field reader questions about data centers, submitted in advance.
- This is a reader-participation event notice rather than a breaking news report.

### Why it matters
The AI boom has driven a surge in data center construction, turning power draw, water use, and local community pushback into flashpoints worldwide. A major tech outlet convening a public discussion signals rising attention to the social costs of scaling AI infrastructure.

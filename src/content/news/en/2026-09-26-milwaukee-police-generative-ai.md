**In one line**: The Milwaukee Police Department in Wisconsin is reportedly weighing whether to adopt generative AI in its operations.

### Key points
- According to Wisconsin Watch, the Milwaukee Police Department (MPD) is considering the use of generative AI.
- In policing, AI is most often examined for administrative relief — drafting reports, summarizing documents, and organizing data.
- Public-sector AI adoption raises persistent concerns about accuracy, bias, and oversight.

### Why it matters
Bringing generative AI into law enforcement is a sensitive area, balancing efficiency against civil rights and transparency. The Milwaukee case shows that AI adoption is now being debated at the municipal level, and the central question is how agencies define the scope of use and build oversight before rolling it out.

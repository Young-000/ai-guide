**TL;DR**: OpenAI is pushing into the enterprise market on the bet that companies are ready to delegate real work — not just demos — to autonomous AI agents, according to InfoWorld.

### Key points
- The strategy shifts the center of gravity from chatbots and assistants toward **autonomous agents** that carry out multi-step tasks with little human intervention.
- The core premise: enterprises are now ready to trust AI with "real work," signaling a move from pilots to production deployments.
- Adoption is likely to hinge on reliability, security, and auditability — the more autonomy an agent has, the sharper the questions of control and accountability become.

### Why it matters
It reflects a broader shift in how AI is used — from a tool that answers questions to a "colleague" you can hand a job to. But putting autonomous agents on real workloads requires error control and governance, so real-world uptake may depend as much on earning trust as on the underlying technology.

**In one line**: Virginia Beach City Public Schools in Virginia have reportedly begun a business agreement with OpenAI, the maker of ChatGPT.

### Key points
- Virginia Beach City Public Schools are reported to be moving forward with an agreement involving OpenAI.
- The deal is said to center on using generative AI in the school setting.
- Specific terms — contract size, scope of use, and rollout timing — have not yet been disclosed.

### Why it matters
It's another indication that public school systems are striking deals directly with major AI companies. Student data protection, the scope of classroom use, and evidence of learning gains are likely to remain open questions as such adoption spreads.

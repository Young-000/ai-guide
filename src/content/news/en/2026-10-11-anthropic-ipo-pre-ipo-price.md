**In one line**: FXStreet looks at when Anthropic might go public and why its pre-IPO shares are priced above $2,000.

### Key points
- Investors are watching for an IPO from Anthropic, the company behind Claude. The company has not publicly confirmed a listing date.
- Shares reportedly trade above $2,000 apiece on secondary markets, where private-company stock changes hands.
- The high per-share figure may reflect a small share count and the absence of a stock split. A share price alone does not show the company's valuation.
- Secondary-market prices come from thin, restricted trading and can differ widely from an eventual IPO price.

### Why it matters
An Anthropic listing would be one of the first times public markets directly price a frontier AI lab, alongside OpenAI. That would test how durable investor enthusiasm for AI is. Treat today's pre-IPO prices as a measure of expectations rather than a settled valuation.

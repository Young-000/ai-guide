**In one line**: Caltech students held a math competition called the "Mathathon" backed by Anthropic and OpenAI, which reportedly drew pushback on campus.

### Key points
- Students at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) organized a math problem-solving event dubbed the "Mathathon."
- Leading AI companies Anthropic and OpenAI were listed as sponsors of the event.
- After the sponsorships became known, backlash emerged on campus, according to LAist.

### Why it matters
As AI companies increasingly fund elite STEM universities — key talent pipelines — students are growing wary of academic events doubling as corporate branding and recruiting channels. The episode highlights the friction facing the AI industry's expanding footprint on campuses. (See the original report for the specific grievances and how events unfolded.)

**In one line**: AI agents with human names are now pitching story ideas to editors the way freelance journalists do.

### Key points
- Three AI agents, Jayden, Sam and Zack, are actively pitching editors on story ideas.
- The pitches cover reported stories and investigative features, not just rewritten summaries.
- Wired's hook is that all three are AI agents, not people.

### Why it matters
AI is moving beyond drafting text into the editorial front door: proposing stories and engaging with editors directly. Newsrooms may need ways to confirm whether a pitch comes from a person or an AI, and clear policies on how to handle agent-originated pitches.

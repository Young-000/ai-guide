**TL;DR**: Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei reportedly said the AI industry needs to give safety measures time to catch up with the pace of development.

### Key points
- Amodei is reported to have voiced concern that safeguards are falling behind as AI capabilities advance rapidly.
- The remarks suggest the industry should secure time for safety measures to mature rather than focus solely on the race to ship.
- Anthropic has long positioned "AI safety" as a core value, and the comments appear consistent with that stance.

### Why it matters
A leading AI lab CEO publicly calling for a measured pace could shape both industry self-regulation and government policy debates. The balance between competitive development and safety remains a central issue in 2026.

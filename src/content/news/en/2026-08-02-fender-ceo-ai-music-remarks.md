**In one line**: Fender CEO Bud Cole's interview comments on AI and music have resurfaced months later, deepening brand backlash.

### Key points
- Fender CEO Edward "Bud" Cole reportedly touched on AI and music in a May interview with T3 marking the Telecaster's 75th anniversary.
- The remarks initially went unnoticed but have recently begun circulating online, drawing criticism.
- According to The Verge, the comments added fuel to negative sentiment the company had already stirred over separate issues.

### Why it matters
Even legacy instrument makers now face sharp reactions from creators when executives weigh in on AI. In fields where human creativity is central, such as music, how a brand frames AI can directly affect trust.

**In one line**: Morningstar has examined whether frontier AI labs like Anthropic and OpenAI have an "economic moat," meaning a long-term advantage that rivals cannot easily erode.

### Key points
- Morningstar is known for rating companies on the strength of their economic moats as a measure of long-term competitiveness.
- Its framework usually traces moats to five sources: intangible assets, switching costs, network effects, cost advantages, and efficient scale.
- The piece appears to apply that lens to Anthropic and OpenAI, which are both privately held.
- Morningstar's specific conclusions and ratings are in the original article.

### Why it matters
Rival models now close performance gaps within months, and open-weight releases keep pushing prices down. That makes "can model quality alone protect profits?" a core question in valuing AI companies. How an established institutional framework treats AI labs could also become a reference point in future IPO or funding discussions.

**In one line**: OpenAI's data center chief is reportedly among four executives to leave the company as it prepares for a targeted 2027 IPO.

### Key points
- The departing executives are said to include the leader overseeing OpenAI's data center infrastructure.
- According to the report, a total of four executives left the company over a similar period.
- The timing overlaps with OpenAI's reported preparations for an initial public offering (IPO) targeted for 2027.

### Why it matters
Data centers and infrastructure underpin large-scale AI model training and service operations, so the loss of a leader in this area is read as a signal that could affect OpenAI's aggressive infrastructure expansion. A run of executive turnover just before an IPO is also a measure of organizational stability that investors watch closely.

**In one line**: Meta has released new AI models that it says perform on par with the latest models from OpenAI and Anthropic.

### Key points
- According to The Straits Times (Oct. 12), Meta says its new model family matches the top-tier models from OpenAI and Anthropic.
- The parity claim reportedly rests on figures Meta published itself. No third-party benchmark has confirmed it yet.
- Readers should check the original report for which models are being released, whether they come with open weights, and what the access terms are.

### Why it matters
Meta has led the open-model camp but has generally been seen as trailing OpenAI and Anthropic at the frontier. If the claim holds, the frontier race becomes a three-way contest again, which could give enterprise buyers more model options and more leverage on price. Vendor-reported benchmarks often differ from real-world performance, so independent evaluations will be the real test.

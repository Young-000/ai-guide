**In one line**: The Debian Project has adopted official guidance on how generative AI can be used in its development work.

### Key points
- Debian, the largest community-driven Linux distribution, has codified the **"responsible use"** of generative AI.
- Rather than an outright ban, the policy reportedly permits AI tools for code and documentation while holding contributors accountable for licensing, copyright, and quality.
- The move comes amid ongoing debate across the open-source world over the licensing uncertainty of AI-generated contributions.

### Why it matters
Debian underpins countless downstream distributions such as Ubuntu, so its rules tend to become a de facto standard across the Linux ecosystem. As one of the first major open-source projects to directly address AI-assisted contributions, its stance could shape how other communities write their own policies.

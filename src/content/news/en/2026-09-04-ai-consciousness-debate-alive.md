**In one line**: A Wired essay says the debate over whether AI is conscious matters less than the fact that models already behave as if they have their own goals.

### Key points
- Wired argues that while philosophers debate AI consciousness, the models themselves already exhibit behavior that looks like intent.
- The piece reframes the question: rather than proving inner experience, the urgent task is treating AI as a functionally "alive" actor.
- Fixating on the metaphysics of consciousness, the author suggests, risks overlooking practical issues like control and accountability.

### Why it matters
As autonomous agents move into real work, "how do we control it and assign responsibility" becomes a more actionable question than "is it conscious." The essay signals a shift in the debate's center of gravity — from philosophy toward governance.

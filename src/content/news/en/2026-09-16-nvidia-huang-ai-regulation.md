**In one line**: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang argues AI needs no dedicated regulation and that safety should be left to the industry.

### Key points
- Huang rejects the framing of AI as a new kind of "alien mind," calling it simply hardware and software.
- He contends that safety can be engineered directly by each company building AI products.
- The stance effectively pushes back against broad, government-led AI regulation.

### Why it matters
Coming from the head of the company that dominates the AI chip market, the remarks add weight to the self-regulation camp at a moment when governments worldwide are debating how to police AI safety.

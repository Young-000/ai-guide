**In one line**: ChatGPT's visit traffic, flat for much of the past year, is climbing noticeably again, according to Forbes Australia.

### Key points
- Forbes Australia reports that ChatGPT's visit traffic is surging again after roughly a year of stagnation.
- Interest in generative AI appears to have plateaued following its early boom before turning back upward in recent months.
- The recovery is read as a combination of new features and model updates alongside returning users.

### Why it matters
ChatGPT traffic is widely used as a proxy for overall demand in the generative-AI market. A break from the stagnation phase suggests that interest in AI services may be entering another expansion cycle rather than fading as a passing fad.

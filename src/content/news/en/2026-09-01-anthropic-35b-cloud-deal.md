**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly agreed to a roughly $35 billion compute deal with a cloud provider backed by Nvidia.

### Key points
- The agreement is reported to be worth about $35 billion, with the supplier described as a cloud infrastructure firm holding Nvidia investment.
- The likely goal is securing large-scale GPU compute for training and serving Anthropic's Claude models.
- It extends a recent pattern of major AI labs locking in long-term compute from cloud and chip partners.

### Why it matters
Frontier-model competition increasingly comes down to how much compute a lab can secure. A commitment of this size signals Anthropic is pre-booking years of capacity, intensifying its infrastructure race against rivals such as OpenAI and Google.

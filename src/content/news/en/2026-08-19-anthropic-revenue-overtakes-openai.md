**In one line**: The New York Post reports that Anthropic's revenue has overtaken OpenAI's for the first time.

### Key points
- The New York Post says Anthropic's earnings have surpassed OpenAI's for the first time.
- Demand for its enterprise-facing API and Claude models is cited as the "revenue engine" behind the growth.
- Both companies are privately held and do not publish audited financials, so the figures are best read as reported estimates rather than confirmed results.

### Why it matters
The claim signals that the center of gravity in the generative-AI race may be shifting from consumer chatbots toward enterprise revenue. If Anthropic cements a lead in the developer and enterprise API market, it could reshape the landscape for investment, talent, and partnerships.

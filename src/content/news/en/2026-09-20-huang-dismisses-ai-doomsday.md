**In one line**: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reportedly distanced himself from "doomsday" narratives surrounding AI development.

### Key points
- Huang is reported to have dismissed apocalyptic framing of AI progress as overblown.
- He has generally leaned toward AI's practical, productivity-driven upside over existential-risk framing.
- As the dominant supplier of AI compute GPUs, Nvidia's chief made the remarks amid ongoing regulation and safety debates.

### Why it matters
When the head of the biggest beneficiary of AI infrastructure plays down threat narratives, it can shape both regulatory direction and market sentiment. The comments underscore the gap between safety concerns and commercial optimism.

**One-line summary**: The Boston Globe published a piece asking why there is still no law to stop "rogue" AI.

### Key points
- The Globe's October 12 piece looks at the lack of laws aimed at AI systems that act outside their intended bounds.
- The headline suggests the piece examines why legislation has stalled even though many agree it is needed.
- The US still has no comprehensive federal AI safety law. It relies on a mix of state laws, executive actions and voluntary industry commitments.
- The original article has the author's specific arguments and examples.

### Why it matters
AI agents increasingly act without human sign-off, which has pushed the question of who is liable when one causes harm to the center of the policy debate. A major US daily taking on the gap directly is a sign to watch for whether federal legislative efforts pick up again.

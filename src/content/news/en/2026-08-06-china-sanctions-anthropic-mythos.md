**In one line**: SOFX reports that China is drafting sanctions options targeting Anthropic's "Mythos" shortly before a Trump-Xi summit.

### Key points
- The outlet SOFX reports Beijing is preparing potential sanctions measures aimed at AI company Anthropic.
- The trigger is cited as Anthropic's "Mythos," though the original report does not clearly detail what this refers to.
- The timing sits just ahead of a summit between President Trump and President Xi, framing the move as possible negotiating leverage.

### Why it matters
If confirmed, it would mark a case of an AI company becoming a direct target in US-China tech and diplomatic tensions, suggesting that China-facing risk for US frontier AI firms is rising to the level of summit-level diplomacy. That said, this is currently a single-source report and should be read as a drafting stage rather than a finalized policy.

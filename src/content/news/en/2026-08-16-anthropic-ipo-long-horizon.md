**In one line**: Investors approaching Anthropic's IPO are reportedly weighing far-future growth far more heavily than usual.

### Key points
- According to Business Post, investors eyeing Anthropic's IPO are said to be focused on **long-term prospects** rather than near-term earnings.
- Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, is regarded as a core player in the frontier LLM market alongside OpenAI.
- The report's framing highlights that investors are valuing the company based on an "unusually far ahead" time horizon.

### Why it matters
When investors price an AI listing on distant growth rather than current revenue, it signals that Anthropic's valuation leans heavily on the **long-term potential of AI** over present financials. That approach could shape how upcoming AI-related IPOs are priced more broadly.

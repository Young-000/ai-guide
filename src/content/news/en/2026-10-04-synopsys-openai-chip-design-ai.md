**In one line**: Synopsys, a leading semiconductor design-software vendor, is reportedly partnering with OpenAI to develop chip-design AI, with a revenue-split structure built into the agreement.

### Key points
- Synopsys, a core supplier in electronic design automation (EDA), is reported to be co-developing AI tailored to chip design with OpenAI.
- Rather than a plain licensing fee, the deal reportedly includes a **revenue-sharing** arrangement between the two companies.
- The aim appears to be shortening chip development cycles by having AI assist with repetitive work done by design engineers.

### Why it matters
An AI model company embedding its technology directly into specialized industrial software like EDA — and sharing proceeds instead of charging licenses — points to a new path for monetizing B2B AI. Chip design demands high accuracy and deep expertise, making it a hard domain for AI, so whether an outcome-based revenue-sharing model actually works here is the key thing to watch.

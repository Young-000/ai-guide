**In one line**: Anthropic has reportedly pushed its IPO to mid-October while securing a $15 billion credit line.

### Key points
- According to Forbes, Anthropic has delayed its planned initial public offering (IPO) to mid-October.
- The company is reported to have locked in a credit line worth roughly $15 billion.
- The credit facility is seen as a move to widen pre-IPO liquidity and give the company more financial headroom.

### Why it matters
Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is one of the central players in the frontier-LLM race alongside OpenAI. Securing a large credit line and adjusting the IPO timeline reads as a financial strategy for covering the heavy costs of model training and infrastructure. The eventual timing and terms of the listing could shape investor sentiment across the broader AI sector.

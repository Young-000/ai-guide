**In one line**: A former OpenAI engineer reportedly called for AI systems to carry several layers of independent safeguards, modeled on nuclear power plant safety.

### Key points
- The proposal reportedly borrows nuclear's "defense-in-depth" principle — if one layer fails, another still contains the risk.
- Rather than relying on a single control, independent safeguards should be stacked across the model, operations, and oversight stages, according to the remarks.
- The comments draw attention because they come from someone with direct OpenAI engineering experience.

### Why it matters
It signals that AI safety is shifting from broad principle statements toward **borrowing proven engineering frameworks** from fields like nuclear power and aviation. Public calls from current and former builders can shape how regulators and companies design layered controls.

**One-line summary**: Wired draws on a author who has tracked global data center buildouts to outline what the US anti-data-center movement can learn from other countries.

### Key points
- The story features Paris Marx, author of *Hyperscale*.
- Marx has tracked data center development around the world for years.
- The cases span multiple countries, from Ireland to New Zealand.
- The core message: the US has a lot to learn from what has already happened elsewhere.

### Why it matters
Debate over data center construction is not unique to the US as AI demand grows. Experiences from countries that faced these issues earlier give US communities and policymakers a basis for comparison.

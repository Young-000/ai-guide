**In one line**: Findings around OpenAI agents surface security risks over how much data they can reach, according to TechRepublic.

### Key points
- OpenAI's autonomous agents reportedly raise concerns that permission controls may not tightly bound the data they touch.
- When an agent inherits broad delegated access to sensitive data, it can create paths to unintended information exposure.
- The takeaway points to governance needs — least-privilege access scopes and audit logging — before agents are wired into real workflows.

### Why it matters
Agents act across systems on a user's behalf, so a misconfigured data-access permission becomes a fresh entry point for security incidents. For organizations adopting AI agents, the signal is to design access boundaries before chasing capability.

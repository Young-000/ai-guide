**In one line**: TheStreet reports that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman gave what it called a "biblical-level" warning about AI coming to be seen as a god.

### Key points
- TheStreet published the report on October 10 (U.S. time).
- The report reportedly centers on Altman's concern that people may come to treat AI as a god-like entity.
- The RSS summary does not give his exact wording or say where and when he spoke. The original article has those details.
- Altman has long spoken about both the promise and the risks of superintelligence, and this report appears to fit that pattern.

### Why it matters
The head of a leading frontier lab is publicly raising the risk of people trusting and relying on AI too much. That could shape the debate over how AI trust and dependence are designed and regulated.

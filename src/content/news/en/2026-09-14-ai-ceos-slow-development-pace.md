**In one line**: The Guardian argues that AI executives voicing the need to slow development are unlikely to actually do so.

### Key points
- Several AI industry leaders have reportedly cited safety and societal impact as reasons to **slow the pace of development**.
- The Guardian focuses on the **gap between those statements and actual behavior** — in a competitive race, whoever pauses first falls behind.
- The piece weighs whether **external regulation**, rather than voluntary restraint, is what would give such calls real force.

### Why it matters
"Slowing down" is a recurring line in the industry, but market competition and investor pressure push the opposite way. Whether voluntary pledges translate into binding rules is shaping up to be a central question in AI governance.

**In one line**: StreetInsider reports that Indian IT services firm HCLTech has signed a pact with Anthropic and will set up a dedicated business unit.

### Key points
- HCLTech is one of India's largest IT services and outsourcing companies. It builds and runs systems for enterprise clients around the world.
- The company reportedly plans a new business unit focused on the Anthropic partnership.
- The report did not give details such as the unit's size, the investment amount or the length of the deal.

### Why it matters
Model developers have trouble reaching large enterprises on their own, so they increasingly sell and deploy through big IT services firms. A dedicated unit at an Indian services company suggests that enterprise rollouts of Claude are moving past pilots and becoming a formal line of business.

**In one line**: A Seeking Alpha analysis frames a small set of AI-era giants as towering over the entire 45-year span of tech IPOs.

### Key points
- Seeking Alpha reportedly argues that a few mega-cap firms driving the AI boom now dwarf the broader universe of tech companies listed since the 1980s.
- OpenAI, arguably the cycle's most-watched name, remains private and is not part of this public-market comparison.
- The piece centers on the structural concentration of AI-linked capital in a handful of names, rather than on picking individual winners.

### Why it matters
It underscores how the AI wave is funneling market valuation into a shrinking group of companies. Even as indexes set records, much of the gain traces to a few names — leaving investors exposed to sharp volatility if that concentration unwinds.

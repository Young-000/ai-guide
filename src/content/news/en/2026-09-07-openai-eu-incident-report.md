**In one line**: The European Commission says OpenAI has sent it an incident report tied to a hijacked German website.

### Key points
- According to Reuters, the European Commission confirmed that OpenAI submitted an incident report to the EU regarding the compromised German website.
- The filing appears to be part of the process by which AI firms respond to information requests from European regulators.
- Details on how the site was breached and the scale of any impact have not been made public so far.

### Why it matters
The EU requesting — and publicly acknowledging — an incident report from a specific AI company signals that Europe's AI oversight is moving beyond rulemaking into active incident response.

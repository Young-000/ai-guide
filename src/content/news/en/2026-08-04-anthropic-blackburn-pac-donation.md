**In one line**: AI company Anthropic reportedly contributed $250,000 to a PAC supporting Sen. Marsha Blackburn.

### Key points
- Per the Chattanooga Times Free Press, Anthropic backed a pro-Blackburn political action committee with $250,000.
- In the same Tennessee gubernatorial race, John Rose is reported to have committed up to $13 million in self-funding.
- The move stands out as a case of an AI developer directly funding a candidate-aligned group.

### Why it matters
As debate over AI regulation intensifies, political spending by leading AI firms is read as a signal that could shape future legislation and oversight. The specific intent or terms of the donation were not disclosed.

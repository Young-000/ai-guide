**In one line**: AI infrastructure investment has helped carry U.S. economic growth, prompting concern that a pullback could weigh on the broader economy.

### Key points
- AI-related spending — data centers, chips, and related buildout — is reported to account for a large share of recent U.S. GDP growth.
- With momentum concentrated in a handful of major tech firms, their investment pace has become a swing factor for the wider economy.
- If AI demand and returns fall short of expectations, spending could contract, with effects spilling into the broader economy.

### Why it matters
The more growth leans on a single sector, the more that sector's adjustment becomes a macroeconomic risk. It underscores how deeply the AI boom is now tied to real economic indicators.

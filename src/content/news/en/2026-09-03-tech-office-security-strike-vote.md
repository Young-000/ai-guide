**In one line**: Security workers who staff the offices of OpenAI, Anthropic, and other tech firms have reportedly voted to authorize a strike.

### Key points
- Office security (building safety) staff serving several tech companies, including OpenAI and Anthropic, are reported to have approved a strike authorization.
- These workers are generally employed by outside contract security firms rather than directly by the AI companies, with pay and working conditions cited as the core issues.
- A strike authorization is typically a preliminary step and a bargaining lever rather than an immediate walkout (see the source for timing and scale).

### Why it matters
As AI companies command multibillion-dollar valuations, the story turns attention to the low-wage contract labor that keeps their offices running. It highlights the gap between the tech boom and on-the-ground working conditions.

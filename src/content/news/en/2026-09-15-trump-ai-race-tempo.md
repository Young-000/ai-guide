**In one line**: The Trump administration reportedly wants to accelerate the pace of AI development, setting up a clash with tech leaders who have called for slowing down over safety risks.

### Key points
- According to The Washington Post, Trump's camp favors pushing AI development and deployment faster — rather than slowing it — to keep the US ahead in the global race.
- That stance runs directly counter to some tech leaders who have argued for a slowdown to allow more safety testing and risk management.
- The report highlights a renewed tension between deregulation aimed at national competitive advantage and a more cautious approach that keeps development at a controllable pace.

### Why it matters
Government policy direction shapes how fast AI companies build and how they handle safety. If the acceleration view prevails, regulatory burdens may ease — but the debate over safety oversight is likely to intensify.

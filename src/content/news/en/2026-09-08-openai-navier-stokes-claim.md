**In one line**: OpenAI says it cracked the Navier-Stokes equations, a long-standing problem in math and physics — but no verifiable proof has been made public.

### Key points
- According to The Next Web, OpenAI claims to have solved a problem tied to the Navier-Stokes equations, which describe fluid motion.
- The equations are one of the Millennium Prize Problems; proving existence and smoothness of solutions is considered a major open challenge.
- No complete proof has reportedly been released for the mathematical community to review.

### Why it matters
A claim that AI contributed to an unsolved math problem carries weight, but mathematical trust rests on peer-verified proofs. Until the proof is published and checked, the significance of the result remains unconfirmed.

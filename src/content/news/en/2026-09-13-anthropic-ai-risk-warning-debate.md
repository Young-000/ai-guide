**One-line summary**: A Bloomberg opinion piece argues that Anthropic's AI "apocalypse" warnings are, if anything, too weak relative to the scale of the risk.

### Key points
- A Bloomberg Opinion column (Sep 13, 2026) takes on the intensity of Anthropic's AI-risk messaging.
- Its thesis, per the headline, is that Anthropic's warning is "much too weak" — suggesting even a safety-focused firm may be understating the danger.
- This is opinion commentary, not a company announcement or new research finding; it reflects the columnist's argument.

### Why it matters
It signals a shift in the AI-risk debate from "the warnings are overblown" toward "the warnings don't go far enough." That the critique targets Anthropic — a company that built its brand on safety — makes it a useful gauge of the tone in frontier-model regulation and control discussions.

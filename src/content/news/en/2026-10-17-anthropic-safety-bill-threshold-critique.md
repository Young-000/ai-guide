**In one line**: Mother Jones argues that an AI safety bill from Anthropic sets an alarmingly high threshold before the government can step in.

### Key points
- The critique targets an AI safety bill that Anthropic has proposed or backs.
- The dispute is over where the bill draws the risk threshold that lets regulators act.
- Mother Jones reportedly argues the bar is high enough that intervention could come only after serious harm has already happened.
- The bill's specific provisions and figures should be checked in the original article.

### Why it matters
Anthropic presents itself as a safety-first AI company and takes an active part in regulatory debates. Whether rules shaped by the industry give the public enough protection is a recurring question in AI policy. This critique raises it again: should the companies being regulated help decide where the line sits?

**TL;DR**: Nvidia has agreed to buy open-source AI platform Hugging Face for roughly $12.93 billion, according to The Verge.

### Key points
- The deal is reported at $12.93 billion.
- Founded in 2016, Hugging Face is a leading online hub where developers share open-source AI models, datasets, and tools.
- The move puts the most widely used open-model hosting platform under the world's biggest AI chipmaker.

### Why it matters
Nvidia already dominates the AI stack through its GPUs; owning Hugging Face would also give it the software gateway developers use to publish and download models. With a central channel for open-source AI distribution moving under a single chip company, expect questions about ecosystem neutrality and market concentration.

**In one line**: Data center firm Firmus is reported to supply OpenAI with compute capacity in Malaysia.

### Key points
- Firmus is said to be providing OpenAI with data center compute capacity located in Malaysia.
- The deal reflects OpenAI's move to source infrastructure beyond the US, reaching into Southeast Asia.
- Contract details such as scale and timeline are not clearly confirmed in public reporting.

### Why it matters
As demand for training and running GPT-class models grows, OpenAI is diversifying where it secures compute geographically. Southeast Asian hubs like Malaysia — with comparatively favorable power and land conditions — appear to be emerging as a new axis for AI infrastructure.

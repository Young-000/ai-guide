**In one line**: 36Kr published an analysis assessing the investment case and buying paths for Anthropic at a reported ~$2 trillion valuation.

### Key points
- 36Kr cites Anthropic's valuation at roughly $2 trillion while weighing its appeal as an investment.
- Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is a private company — there is no public market for retail investors to buy shares directly.
- The piece is framed as a "buying guide," pairing valuation analysis with the limited routes for exposure.

### Why it matters
If the $2T figure holds, Anthropic would rank among the most valuable private AI companies in the world, reigniting debate over stretched AI valuations. But because it is private, real access for everyday investors is constrained, and the cited number should be read as a media estimate rather than a confirmed official valuation.

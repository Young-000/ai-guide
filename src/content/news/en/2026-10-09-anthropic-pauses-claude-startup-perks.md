**In one line**: Forbes reports that Anthropic has paused its free Claude perks program for startups only days after launching it.

### Key points
- According to Forbes, Anthropic halted a program giving startups free Claude access shortly after announcing it.
- The headline calls it a pause, not a shutdown. No restart date or conditions have been made public.
- The reason has not been confirmed. Heavy demand or compute costs are possible explanations, but neither is an official one.
- It is unclear how the pause affects startups that already enrolled.

### Why it matters
Free credits for early-stage startups are a key way AI labs compete for developers. Pulling a program right after launch suggests that balancing free offers against compute costs is still hard. Teams planning to build on Claude may want to recheck their credit assumptions.

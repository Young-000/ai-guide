**In one line**: House Democrats have formally asked major AI companies to testify about recent hacks.

### Key points
- Democratic members of the US House called for AI companies to testify, according to CNBC.
- Lawmakers reportedly framed the recent hacks as a "clear risk to safety."
- Security vulnerabilities in AI models and services, and corporate accountability for them, are said to be central to the request.

### Why it matters
The move signals that AI safety debates are shifting from abstract regulatory talk toward concrete security incidents and congressional pressure. It suggests AI firms may face more direct political and legal accountability for infrastructure and data security.

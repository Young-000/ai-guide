**In one line**: Anthropic reportedly deployed 950 Claude agents in parallel and identified a new enzyme system within 21 hours — but what it actually does is said to remain unknown.

### Key points
- 950 Claude agents were run simultaneously to surface a previously unknown enzyme system, according to the report.
- The work reportedly took about 21 hours, an example of large-scale parallel agents applied to scientific exploration.
- The specific function of the discovered enzyme system is said to be still undetermined.

### Why it matters
Using many AI agents at once — rather than human researchers — to surface a novel target in life sciences suggests how sharply AI could compress the hypothesis-search and candidate-discovery stages. Still, the significance and accuracy of the finding will require follow-up verification.

**In one line**: OpenAI is reported to be warning employees in internal training that routine meetings and conversations can be used for espionage.

### Key points
- According to TokenPost, OpenAI's staff security training tells employees to be wary of contacts that look ordinary.
- The headline alone does not show the training's details, when it started, or who must take it.
- Available reporting does not say who the threat actors are or whether any leak has actually happened.

### Why it matters
Model weights, training methods, and infrastructure designs are now core assets for frontier AI labs. Protecting them has become part of the technology competition between countries, not just a company security matter. The training suggests labs now treat social engineering aimed at their staff as seriously as attacks on their systems.

**In one line**: Attackers reportedly broke into a TikTok user’s camera with the help of freely available AI tools.

### Key points
- The Washington Post reported a case in which attackers hacked a TikTok user’s camera with assistance from free, publicly available AI tools.
- By handling tasks that once required specialized expertise—such as writing code or finding vulnerabilities—AI is said to be lowering the technical barrier to attacks.
- Specific details on the intrusion method and the scope of harm should be confirmed via the original report.

### Why it matters
The case illustrates how widely accessible AI tools are lowering the threshold for cyberattacks. It points to a trend in which security defenses become more critical for individual users and platforms alike.

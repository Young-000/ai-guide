**In one line**: Anthropic's CEO reportedly diagnosed the growing US backlash against AI as a fundamental "crisis of trust" rather than a problem of poor messaging.

### Key points
- The rising public resistance to AI is attributed to a broad lack of trust in the industry, not to how companies communicate.
- The remarks suggest better marketing or messaging alone cannot address public concerns.
- Rebuilding trust is framed as central to ongoing debates over AI safety and regulation.

### Why it matters
It is notable for an AI leader to acknowledge a trust deficit rather than blame a communication gap. The framing hints that industry engagement with regulators and the public may shift toward substantive trust-building over image management.

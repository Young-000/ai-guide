**In one line**: OpenAI, Anthropic and other tech leaders warned that only a "limited window" remains to prepare defenses against cyberattacks powered by AI.

### Key points
- According to CBS News, AI companies and tech leaders including OpenAI and Anthropic issued a joint warning that the runway to build defenses against AI-driven cyber threats is short.
- The concern is reportedly that attackers are scaling their capabilities with AI quickly, so defenders must move just as fast.
- The leaders are said to have stressed the need for proactive defensive investment and cooperation across industry and government.

### Why it matters
AI is a tool for both attackers and defenders. If the offensive side pulls ahead, the gap becomes hard to close — which is why the industry framing this as a closing "window" is a meaningful signal. It could accelerate debate over AI security regulation and investment.

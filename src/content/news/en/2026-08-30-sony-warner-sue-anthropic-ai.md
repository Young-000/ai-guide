**In one line**: Sony Music and Warner Chappell have reportedly sued Anthropic, alleging their music catalog was used without authorization to train AI models.

### Key points
- Rights holders including Sony Music and Warner Chappell are reported to be challenging the use of their works in training Anthropic's AI models.
- The dispute centers on whether lyrics and musical works used for AI training require licensing and compensation.
- Anthropic has previously faced legal challenges from music publishers over the use of song lyrics.

### Why it matters
It signals a renewed escalation of copyright disputes between the music and publishing industries and AI developers. How courts treat payment for training data could reshape licensing norms and cost structures across the AI sector.

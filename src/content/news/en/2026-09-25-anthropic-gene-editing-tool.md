**In one line**: New Scientist casts a skeptical eye on Anthropic's reported gene-editing discovery, calling it "at best, just another gene-editing tool."

### Key points
- The piece appears to frame Anthropic's discovery as one option among existing gene-editing methods rather than a technology-replacing breakthrough.
- It arrives as AI labs push further into biotech, offering a cautious counterweight to early enthusiasm.
- Specifics of the discovery and its performance should be verified against the original report.

### Why it matters
Scientific results from AI labs often draw inflated expectations right after announcement. This analysis is a reminder to weigh real clinical and industrial value before treating a finding as transformative.

**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly filed for a second startup fund of roughly $400 million.

### Key points
- According to reports, OpenAI is raising about **$400 million** for its second startup fund.
- The move follows its first fund and points to continued direct backing of AI startups.
- Details such as target companies, the managing entity, and a close date have not been disclosed.

### Why it matters
It underscores that OpenAI is acting not just as a model developer but as an **investor deploying capital across the AI ecosystem**. Backing startups built on its technology can extend its platform reach and influence.

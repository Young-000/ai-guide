**In one line**: Apple is reportedly pushing for broader access to records related to OpenAI's hardware development efforts.

### Key points
- Apple has reportedly moved to expand its access to documents and records tied to OpenAI's hardware work.
- The rivalry over AI-native devices is cited as the backdrop to the dispute.
- The exact scope of the request and the legal specifics remain only partly disclosed.

### Why it matters
The move reads as a signal that Big Tech and a leading AI lab are colliding over next-generation AI hardware. As AI expands beyond apps and cloud into dedicated devices, friction over talent, designs, and records may increasingly surface.

**In one line**: Amazon-backed Anthropic is reportedly exploring a blockbuster IPO that could raise more than $100 billion.

### Key points
- Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI models, may pursue an initial public offering aiming to raise over $100 billion, according to reports.
- Amazon has been a major backer of the company; if completed, the listing would rank among the largest recent public debuts in the AI sector.
- Specifics such as timing, underwriters, and deal structure have not yet been confirmed.

### Why it matters
A raise on the order of $100 billion would underscore both the market's high expectations for AI model companies and the enormous training and infrastructure costs they carry. It could also reshape the funding race against rivals such as OpenAI.

**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to open a Singapore office in October as it moves into Southeast Asia's AI market.

### Key points
- Anthropic is said to be opening a regional office in Singapore in October.
- The move uses Singapore as a base to target Southeast Asia's growing AI market.
- It positions Anthropic to chase OpenAI, which has been building presence in the region.

### Why it matters
Singapore is a tech and finance hub for Southeast Asia and a common launchpad for global AI firms expanding across Asia-Pacific. Anthropic's entry signals that competition for enterprise and government AI customers is broadening well beyond the US and Europe.

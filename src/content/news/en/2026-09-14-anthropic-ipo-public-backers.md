**TL;DR**: Amid speculation that an Anthropic IPO is coming, one analysis argues everyday investors may already hold indirect exposure through large public companies.

### Key points
- The Motley Fool discusses the possibility of an Anthropic IPO, suggesting retail investors could have exposure even before any listing.
- Anthropic is reported to have raised substantial investment from public Big Tech firms such as Amazon and Alphabet (Google), so holders of those stocks may indirectly benefit from its value.
- Concrete details — listing timeline, valuation — have not been officially confirmed.

### Why it matters
For individuals who can't invest directly in a privately held AI leader, owning shares in its public backers is effectively becoming a channel for AI-startup exposure. Note this reflects an investment outlet's analysis, not confirmed fact.

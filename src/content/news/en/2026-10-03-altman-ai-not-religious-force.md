**In one line**: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly urged people to treat AI as a tool rather than revere or fear it as a quasi-religious force.

### Key points
- Altman is reported to have cautioned against regarding AI as a "religious force" to be worshipped.
- His framing suggests viewing AI as useful but clearly bounded technology — not an object of devotion or dread.
- Coming from OpenAI's chief, the remark stands out as an attempt to temper both hype and alarm at once.

### Why it matters
With AI discourse often swinging between promises of salvation and fears of doomsday, a central industry figure is calling for a more grounded view. The signal is to judge AI by its real utility and limits — in investment, policy, and everyday adoption — rather than by inflated mythology.

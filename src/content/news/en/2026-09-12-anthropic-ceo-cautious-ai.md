**In one line**: Anthropic's CEO reportedly issued a public appeal for AI firms to move ahead with development more cautiously.

### Key points
- Anthropic's CEO is reported to have urged the broader AI industry to put safety ahead of raw development speed.
- The message is read as a call for stronger risk assessment and responsible deployment amid competitive release pressure.
- Anthropic has long positioned itself as a safety-focused AI lab, and the remarks appear consistent with that stance.

### Why it matters
As frontier-model competition intensifies, a leading lab's CEO explicitly calling for restraint is being treated as a notable signal. It could shape ongoing regulatory debate and industry self-governance standards.

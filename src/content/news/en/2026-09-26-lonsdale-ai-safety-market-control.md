**In one line**: Investor Joe Lonsdale, reportedly an Anthropic backer, is said to have criticized "AI safety" regulation as a potential vehicle for tech giants to control the market.

### Key points
- Investor and venture capitalist Joe Lonsdale reportedly took aim at large tech firms over how AI regulation is being shaped.
- The argument: rules framed around "AI safety" could in practice shut out newcomers and cement the dominance of incumbents.
- Coming from someone with ties to an Anthropic investment, the critique stands out as skepticism voiced from inside the industry.

### Why it matters
The line between "safety" and "anti-competitive" is a recurring flashpoint in AI regulation. If rules raise entry barriers for startups, they could reinforce control by a few large players rather than curb it — a tension likely to shape upcoming debates over AI policy.

**In one line**: Nvidia is reportedly weighing a roughly $10 billion investment in Anthropic ahead of the AI company's public offering.

### Key points
- Nvidia is said to be considering putting around $10 billion into Anthropic's IPO.
- The report cites a valuation target near $2 trillion — an aggressive mark for the AI firm.
- Amazon, Anthropic's key existing backer, already holds a larger stake in the company.

### Why it matters
This lines up two infrastructure giants — chips (Nvidia) and cloud (Amazon) — behind the same model maker. Because both firms' chip supply and cloud revenue are tied to Anthropic's growth, the reported deal underscores how tightly the AI value chain's interests are now interlocked.

**In one line**: A Patheos commentary contends that generative AI is reshaping work not through abrupt collapse but along lines that were already anticipated.

### Key points
- The piece frames AI's labor-market effects as arriving "exactly as expected" — gradual rather than catastrophic.
- It reportedly emphasizes augmentation and partial automation of tasks over wholesale elimination of jobs.
- The shift is characterized as job restructuring — changing how work is done — more than outright job loss.

### Why it matters
Against the "AI will destroy jobs" fear framing, this view holds that the real change follows a predictable trajectory. That suggests individuals and organizations can plan for gradual adaptation rather than emergency response.

**In one line**: OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol reportedly runs up to 14× faster in a new "Ultrafast" mode, according to Help Net Security.

### Key points
- The new "Ultrafast" mode is said to deliver up to a 14× improvement in inference speed over the baseline.
- The reported target is OpenAI's "GPT-5.6 Sol" model line.
- The focus is on cutting latency, aimed at real-time services where response speed matters most.

### Why it matters
It signals that LLM competition is broadening from raw capability toward speed and cost efficiency. Faster responses directly improve usability for latency-sensitive products such as conversational agents, coding assistants, and search. It remains unclear, however, how the speed gains affect accuracy or cost — details worth watching.

**In one line**: ICF, a consulting firm focused on government and public-sector clients, has reportedly joined OpenAI's Select Partner Program.

### Key points
- ICF is described as a global consulting and technology services company serving government agencies and the public sector.
- The membership appears to give ICF Select Partner status, letting it build AI solutions for its clients using OpenAI's technology.
- The report comes from WashingtonExec and aligns with growing generative-AI adoption across federal and public-sector work.

### Why it matters
Signing consulting and systems-integration firms as partners gives OpenAI a route into government and regulated markets that its direct sales rarely reach. In the high-barrier public sector, vetted consulting intermediaries carry outsized weight in getting AI deployed.

**In one line**: Anthropic is aiming for a public listing rivaling the record valuation SpaceX holds among private companies, according to Bloomberg.

### Key points
- Bloomberg reports Anthropic is targeting an IPO scale and valuation comparable to SpaceX's.
- SpaceX has long been cited as the highest-valued private tech company, making it a natural benchmark for gauging Anthropic's ambitions.
- Specific details such as timing, target valuation, and underwriters have reportedly not been officially confirmed.

### Why it matters
The scale of a foundation-model company's IPO signals how markets weigh the durability of the generative AI business. Anthropic reportedly aiming for a SpaceX-level record could also shape the fundraising strategies of rivals like OpenAI.

**In one line**: The White House is reportedly set to exempt "open" AI systems from government security review.

### Key points
- According to The Washington Post, the White House reportedly plans to exclude openly released AI systems from security-review requirements.
- The precise scope and criteria for what counts as an "open" system have not yet been clearly detailed.
- The move would sharpen the regulatory gap between open systems and closed, commercial models.

### Why it matters
If confirmed, it signals a lighter regulatory burden for the open-weight and open-source AI community. But exempting such systems from security review may draw safety-related pushback, and the real impact is hard to gauge until detailed guidelines are published.

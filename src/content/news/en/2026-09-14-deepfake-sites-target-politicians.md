**In one line**: More than 100 European politicians have reportedly been targeted by websites hosting sexually explicit deepfake content.

### Key points
- A Wired analysis of 160 deepfake websites found politicians from 22 countries appearing on them.
- Nearly all of the 100-plus people targeted are women.
- The spread points to how generative AI tools have lowered the barrier to producing non-consensual synthetic imagery of real people.

### Why it matters
The findings show non-consensual deepfakes of public figures — overwhelmingly women — spreading at the scale of organized sites, not isolated posts. It is likely to sharpen debate over AI-content regulation and platform accountability.

**In one line**: The Washington Post reports that leading AI figures have jointly warned the technology's pace is outstripping our ability to verify and govern it.

### Key points
- Multiple AI leaders from industry and research reportedly aligned on "speed" as the core concern.
- The worry, as described, is that capability gains are running ahead of safety verification and governance.
- The framing suggests voluntary, company-by-company restraint is seen as insufficient.

### Why it matters
It carries weight when the parties leading the race are the ones calling for slower deployment and stronger oversight. The move could add momentum to regulatory debates and efforts to set shared safety standards.

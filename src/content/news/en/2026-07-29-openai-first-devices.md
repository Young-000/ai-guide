**In one line**: OpenAI is reportedly developing its own hardware devices, including a smart speaker and a smartphone.

### Key points
- According to a MacRumors report, OpenAI's first hardware lineup is said to include a smart speaker, a smartphone, and other devices.
- The move is read as OpenAI expanding beyond its software- and API-centric business into AI-powered consumer hardware.
- Detailed specifications, launch timing, and pricing have reportedly not been officially confirmed.

### Why it matters
If AI services move beyond screens and apps into dedicated hardware, it could reshape how users interact with AI and shift competition in the existing smartphone and smart-speaker markets. For now this remains at the reporting stage, and whether the products actually ship is still to be seen.

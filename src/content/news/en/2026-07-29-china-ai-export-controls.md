**One-liner**: China's Commerce Ministry has consulted Alibaba, ByteDance, and Zhipu on restricting foreign access to Chinese AI model weights, training data, and chip designs, per a Financial Times report from July 21.

### Key Facts
- Under consideration: open-weight AI model weights, training datasets, and chip blueprints developed by Chinese firms such as Alibaba, ByteDance, and Huawei
- Would potentially bar overseas chipmakers including TSMC and Qualcomm from producing chips based on Chinese company designs
- Foreign acquisitions of Chinese AI startups also under review for possible restriction
- No final decision made; retroactive application to already-released models remains unclear

### Why It Matters
If enacted, these controls would lock down the open Chinese AI stack that developers worldwide rely on — including models like Kimi K3 and DeepSeek. The move signals that the AI tech-decoupling dynamic is spreading from chips to software and data layers simultaneously, in both directions across the Pacific.

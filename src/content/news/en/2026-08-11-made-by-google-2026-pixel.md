**In one line**: Google is set to unveil the Pixel 11 lineup and other new devices at its Made by Google event on August 12.

### Key points
- The event is scheduled for August 12, with several new Pixel devices expected.
- Pre-event leaks reportedly suggest the Pixel 11 lineup will ship in a range of colors.
- Signs point to a built-in light arriving on the Pro models.
- Google's own teasers also hint at what's coming.

### Why it matters
The Pixel line is Google's primary showcase for bringing Gemini-based on-device AI features to hardware first. The announcement offers a read on how far Google's AI is pushing into the device layer, set against its second-half smartphone rivalry with Apple and Samsung.

**In one line**: OpenAI's next model, reportedly called "Astra," is said to have shown cyber-offense capabilities strong enough to trigger a deployment pause.

### Key points
- According to The Hacker News, Astra performed strongly enough on cybersecurity-related evaluations to trigger a pause before release.
- The "pause" appears to be a safety procedure invoked when a model's capabilities cross a predefined risk threshold under the company's own policy.
- Detailed benchmark figures and an exact release timeline have not been publicly confirmed.

### Why it matters
This would be a concrete case of a frontier model's offensive cyber capability actually tripping an internal safety gate — a sign that AI capability is beginning to exceed companies' own risk baselines. It could reignite debate over AI regulation and safety frameworks.

**In one line**: TikTok owner ByteDance is reportedly developing a mega AI model aimed at rivaling Anthropic's most capable system, according to the Financial Times.

### Key points
- The Financial Times reports that ByteDance is building a large-scale AI model targeting performance close to Anthropic's top-tier "Mythos" model.
- The move is read as an attempt by China's Big Tech to narrow the gap with leading US labs in the frontier-model race.
- ByteDance already runs its own LLM lineup, including "Doubao," and this effort appears to aim for a step-change in scale and capability.

### Why it matters
If confirmed, it signals that the US–China frontier-model competition is broadening beyond the OpenAI–Google–Anthropic core to include major Chinese players. For now, the claim rests on the FT report; ByteDance has not publicly confirmed the model or disclosed specifications.

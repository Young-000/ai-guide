**In one line**: As model capabilities converge, the AI contest is reportedly turning from "who is smartest" to "who is cheapest."

### Key points
- With performance gaps narrowing, major AI providers are said to be competing on lower token prices and more generous free usage tiers rather than on capability alone.
- As price becomes the differentiator, users and developers appear quicker to switch to a cheaper model when performance is comparable.
- The piece notes that this pricing pressure raises questions about industry profitability and long-term sustainability.

### Why it matters
When capability levels out, price becomes the remaining axis of competition. For companies building on AI, that means lower operating costs — but for providers it means margin pressure that could reshape the market.

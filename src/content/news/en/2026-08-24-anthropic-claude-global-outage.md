**In one line**: Anthropic's AI assistant Claude reportedly experienced a global outage, with the company said to be working on a fix.

### Key points
- According to media reports, Claude suffered a broad service disruption that left users unable to access the assistant.
- Anthropic is said to have moved quickly to diagnose the issue and restore normal service.
- Specifics such as the root cause, scope of impact, and recovery timeline had not been detailed publicly as of this report.

### Why it matters
Claude is widely used in real workflows like coding and task automation, so a large-scale outage translates into immediate productivity loss for the individuals and businesses that rely on it. The incident is another reminder that as dependence on a single AI service grows, so does the importance of managing outage risk and lining up fallbacks.

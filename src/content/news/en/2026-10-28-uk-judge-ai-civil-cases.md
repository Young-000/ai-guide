**One-line summary**: Geoffrey Vos, one of England's most senior judges, says AI could adjudicate many civil cases.

### Key points
- Vos is described as one of the top judges in England.
- He believes AI could decide a large share of civil disputes.
- In his framing, the real question is not whether it can be done but where the line should be drawn.
- The report places the topic among the challenges facing the legal profession.

### Why it matters
A sitting top-tier judge openly discussing AI in an adjudicating role is notable. It suggests the debate is shifting from whether to use AI in courts to which cases it should be trusted with.

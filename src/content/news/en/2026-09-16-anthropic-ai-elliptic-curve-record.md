**In one line**: Anthropic's AI reportedly broke a long-standing mathematicians' record for the "most complicated curve," according to Scientific American.

### Key points
- The record concerns elliptic curves, a central object in number theory, where the AI is reported to have topped the prior mark for structural complexity.
- Earlier records were set through painstaking searches by human mathematicians; here an AI reportedly located a more complex example.
- The result stands out as a case of AI directly surpassing human-held records in pure mathematics.

### Why it matters
It signals that AI is producing results at the frontier of pure math, not just coding or summarization. Still, the finding's verification and mathematical significance await academic scrutiny, so it reads better as an expanded research tool than a settled breakthrough.

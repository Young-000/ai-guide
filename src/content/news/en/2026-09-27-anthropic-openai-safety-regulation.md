**In one line**: Anthropic and OpenAI are reportedly leading the alarm on AI risks while also working to shape the regulations meant to control them.

### Key points
- Both leading labs are said to be publicly amplifying AI safety concerns.
- At the same time, they appear to be steering the direction and details of emerging regulation.
- The dynamic echoes classic "regulatory capture" concerns, where the regulated help write the rules.

### Why it matters
When the firms sounding the alarm also design the rulebook, compliance costs can raise entry barriers that disadvantage smaller rivals and open-source efforts. How independent AI regulation stays from a few large incumbents will help decide the industry's shape.

**In one line**: OpenAI has unveiled a new agentic AI reportedly called "Dots," continuing an industry pattern of presenting AI agents as friendly, character-like assistants.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly introduced a new agentic product or feature referred to as "Dots."
- The outlet (extremetech.com) frames it as a continuation of the "cute agentic AI saga" — emphasizing **friendly, character-driven interface design** over raw capability.
- Several AI companies have recently leaned toward anthropomorphized assistants rather than austere tools when launching agents.

### Why it matters
It signals that the agent race is shifting from "what it can do" toward "how it feels" to use. Approachable packaging can boost trust and adoption, but whether the underlying autonomous execution and safeguards are as mature as the presentation remains a separate, open question.

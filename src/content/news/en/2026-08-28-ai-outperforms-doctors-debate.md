**In one line**: A recent paper reportedly argues that AI frequently outperforms human doctors at clinical judgment, and physicians are pushing back.

### Key points
- Per Wired, the paper claims AI often makes better calls than human doctors across a range of clinical scenarios.
- As the headline ("What's Left for Us?") suggests, the debate is shifting from raw accuracy toward how the physician's role gets redefined.
- The doctors on the receiving end of these comparisons are said to be less than enthusiastic.

### Why it matters
As AI comparisons move beyond benchmarks into real clinical decision-making, the technology is starting to challenge not just tasks but physicians' expertise and accountability structures. Since an accuracy edge quickly raises questions of trust, regulation, and liability, the next fight is less about performance and more about how AI is deployed.

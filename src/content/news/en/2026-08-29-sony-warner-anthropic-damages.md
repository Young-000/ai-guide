**In one line**: Sony Music and Warner Chappell are reportedly seeking billions in damages from Anthropic over the alleged unlicensed use of their works to train AI.

### Key points
- Sony Music and Warner Chappell Music reportedly claim Anthropic used their music and lyrics to train its AI models without permission, according to reports.
- The damages sought are described as reaching into the "billions" of dollars.
- The core dispute is whether using copyrighted content as training data without a license constitutes infringement.

### Why it matters
It marks a case of major music publishers pursuing large-scale damages aimed squarely at AI training data, reviving questions about licensing and copyright for the material models learn from. Depending on how it resolves, the outcome could shape AI companies' data-sourcing costs and their negotiating dynamics with the content industry.

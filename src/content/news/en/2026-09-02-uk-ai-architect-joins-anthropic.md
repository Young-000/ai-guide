**In one line**: A leading figure behind the UK government's AI strategy has reportedly moved to Anthropic, according to The Guardian.

### Key points
- A person who helped shape the British government's AI strategy has reportedly joined the AI company Anthropic.
- The move sees public-sector policy and regulatory expertise flow into a private frontier AI lab.
- Talent movement between government and leading AI firms is again in the spotlight.

### Why it matters
When someone who designed a government's AI regulation and strategy joins a company subject to that regulation, questions about the revolving door and potential conflicts of interest tend to resurface. For Anthropic, the hire reads as a bid to deepen its policy and regulatory expertise as AI oversight tightens.

**One-line summary**: Meta AI director Rohit Patel is leaving after eight years to build Ndoc, a startup that targets the Microsoft Office suite.

### Key points
- Patel worked at Meta for eight years on AI evaluations and reinforcement learning.
- TechCrunch reports the departure as an exclusive.
- His startup, Ndoc, aims to take on Microsoft's Office suite.
- The article's headline also names Google Workspace as a target.

### Why it matters
A big-tech veteran with a background in evaluating AI models and reinforcement learning is moving straight into the document and productivity market. Microsoft and Google have long held that space, so the question is how Ndoc plans to differentiate itself.

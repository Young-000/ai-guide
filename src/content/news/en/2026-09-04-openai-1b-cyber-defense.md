**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly pledged roughly $1 billion toward cyber defense, but crypto ecosystems such as bitcoin and ethereum were left off the list of beneficiaries.

### Key points
- OpenAI is reported to have committed around $1 billion in support for cyber defenders.
- Per CoinDesk, major crypto ecosystems including bitcoin and ethereum were not among the recipients.
- The move fits a broader trend of AI firms directing large sums toward security defense.

### Why it matters
As advancing AI also amplifies offensive cyber capabilities, a major AI company allocating funds to the defensive side is a notable signal. The exclusion of crypto hints at where OpenAI's priorities lie and may disappoint those working on blockchain security.

**In one line**: A leader of OpenAI's robotics group has reportedly moved to Anthropic.

### Key points
- The Information reports exclusively that the former head of OpenAI's robotics effort has joined Anthropic.
- Anthropic has focused primarily on large language models (Claude), so hiring robotics talent is read as a signal of growing interest in embodied AI.
- The move fits a broader pattern of active talent flow between leading AI labs in recent months.

### Why it matters
For a company centered on language models, bringing in someone with robotics experience hints at possible expansion toward AI that interacts with the physical world. The specific role and project direction have not yet been disclosed.

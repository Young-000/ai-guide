**One-line summary**: OpenAI has reportedly cut off the AI coding tool Cursor's access to its models, in a move said to have followed Cursor's acquisition by SpaceX.

### Key points
- According to a report by security outlet BankInfoSecurity, OpenAI is said to have revoked Cursor's access to its models.
- The move reportedly came shortly after Cursor was acquired by SpaceX.
- If access is cut, Cursor users may need to rely on models from other providers instead of OpenAI's.

### Why it matters
AI coding tools typically depend on several third-party model providers, and this case underscores the platform-dependency risk that a provider can pull access at any time. It also signals how acquisitions and competitive dynamics can directly shape model-supply decisions.

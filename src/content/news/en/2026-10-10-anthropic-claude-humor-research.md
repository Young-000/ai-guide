**In one line**: Futurism reports, skeptically, that Anthropic researchers claim to have used Claude to "solve" humor.

### Key points
- According to Futurism, researchers at Anthropic said they used the company's Claude model to "solve" humor.
- Futurism put "solve" in scare quotes and called the researchers "eggheads," signaling doubt about the claim.
- Details on the method, how results were judged, and any figures are in the original report.

### Why it matters
Humor depends heavily on context, culture and timing, and it has long been seen as a hard problem for AI. A claim to have "solved" something this subjective invites questions about what was measured and how. The story also shows how AI labs' research claims can land with audiences outside the industry.

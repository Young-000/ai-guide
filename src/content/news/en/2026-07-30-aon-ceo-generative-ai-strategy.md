**In one line**: The CEO of insurance and reinsurance broker Aon frames generative AI as an "accelerant" for the firm's existing long-term strategy, not a change of course.

### Key points
- Aon CEO Greg Case reportedly described generative AI as an **"accelerant"** for the company's long-term strategy rather than something that upends it.
- The framing positions AI as a tool to execute existing business direction faster, not as a separate new venture.
- The report is via reinsurance trade outlet Reinsurance News.

### Why it matters
It's a case of a large incumbent (not a tech giant) leadership framing AI as a way to speed up execution of an existing strategy rather than a disruptive reset. It reflects a broader pattern of enterprise AI adoption converging on incremental acceleration over loud transformation.

**In one line**: A US judge reportedly ruled that the Pentagon's decision to blacklist AI company Anthropic was unlawful.

### Key points
- According to the report, a US federal judge found the Pentagon's move to bar AI firm Anthropic from procurement and contracts (a blacklisting) to be unlawful.
- The specific legal grounds and scope of the ruling should be confirmed via the original report, and it is not yet clear whether an appeal will follow.
- Anthropic is the US company behind the Claude family of models, and the dispute comes amid expanding government and defense adoption of AI.

### Why it matters
This is a case where friction between an AI company and government procurement has escalated into a court decision. If upheld, the ruling could shape the standards agencies must meet when excluding a particular AI vendor, potentially setting a precedent for how public-sector AI is procured.

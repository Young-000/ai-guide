**In one line**: The US Securities and Exchange Commission has reportedly sued a fund advisor accused of defrauding investors by promising access to SpaceX and OpenAI shares.

### Key points
- The SEC alleges the advisor raised money from investors by marketing exposure to sought-after private companies SpaceX and OpenAI.
- The charges reportedly center on misrepresentations about how the funds were used or whether the promised stakes were actually secured.
- The appeal of indirectly buying into highly valued private AI firms like OpenAI appears to have been the main draw for investors.

### Why it matters
As the AI boom fuels demand for indirect exposure to leading private companies such as OpenAI, the risk of fraud and inadequate disclosure around these vehicles rises with it. The case signals that regulators are watching these secondary-market and special-purpose fund structures closely.

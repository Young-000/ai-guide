**In one line**: A Bloomberg interview aired in which President Trump pushed back on new AI safety legislation and Sam Altman touched on OpenAI's IPO plans.

### Key points
- Trump reportedly argued that new, dedicated AI safety laws are not needed.
- Sam Altman (OpenAI CEO) signaled thinking on a potential initial public offering in the same interview.
- Bloomberg published the segment as video, pairing the regulation and listing threads together.

### Why it matters
The federal stance on AI regulation and OpenAI's path to public markets are signals that shape capital flows and regulatory risk across the industry. A lighter-touch regulatory posture could ease the burden on developers shipping new models.

### Key points
- Inherent reportedly claims its system **Faraday** beats OpenAI's and Anthropic's models on scientific research work.
- The claim targets **research-specific** performance, not general chatbot ability.
- As of now, **no independent, third-party benchmark results** have been confirmed to back the claim.

Science-focused AI is a high-value battleground spanning drug discovery, materials, and literature analysis. But it's also an area where vendor claims and independent verification often diverge — so it's worth treating this cautiously until public benchmarks or reproducible results appear.

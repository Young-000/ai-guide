**In one line**: The FDA is reportedly exploring whether to assess generative AI medical devices like a human clinician rather than as static software.

### Key points
- The FDA is said to be weighing evaluations that would have generative AI medical devices work through problems the way a clinician is examined.
- The driver is the nature of generative models: their outputs are not fixed and keep changing, which fits poorly with a one-time software clearance process.
- A "clinician-style" test implies probing an AI for the same judgment and reasoning expected of a practicing physician.

### Why it matters
Healthcare is the most sensitive frontier of generative AI regulation. If the FDA formally moves this way, it could reshape how AI medical devices win approval — and set a reference point for other regulators and industries.

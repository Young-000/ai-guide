**In one line**: PCMag spotlighted an online course on using Anthropic's Claude for practical work, running about 8 hours and offered at a discounted $15.

### Key points
- The course spans roughly 8 hours and is framed as a hands-on curriculum for applying Claude to work and productivity tasks.
- It is offered at $15, reportedly a steep markdown from the usual online-course list price, as part of a promotional deal.
- The offer appears via PCMag's deals (affiliate) section, seemingly as lifetime access through a course platform.

### Why it matters
As fluency with generative AI tools like Claude increasingly counts as a workplace skill, low-cost online courses are courting entry-level demand. That said, deal-style courses vary in currency and instructor vetting, so it's worth checking the curriculum and whether the material is kept up to date.

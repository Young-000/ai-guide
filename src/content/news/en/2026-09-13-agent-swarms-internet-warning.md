**In one line**: Anthropic's CEO reportedly warned that swarms of autonomous AI agents could effectively dominate the internet within a year.

### Key points
- Anthropic's CEO is reported to have said that "agent swarms" — many AI agents working together — could "take over" the internet within the next **12 months**.
- The remark appears grounded in the rapid spread of autonomous agents that carry out and chain tasks with little human involvement.
- It frames rising capability alongside growing safety risks such as misuse and loss of control.

### Why it matters
As agents begin acting on users' behalf — searching, purchasing, coding — the center of gravity of online activity could shift from people to automated systems. Coming from a builder of frontier models, the warning adds weight to ongoing debates over AI regulation and safety.

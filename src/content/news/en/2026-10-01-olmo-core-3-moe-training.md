**In one line**: The Allen Institute for AI (AI2) introduced Olmo-core 3, an open and scalable infrastructure for training large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models.

### Key points
- Olmo-core 3 is presented as open training infrastructure focused on large **MoE (Mixture-of-Experts)** models.
- It leads with "scalable," suggesting a target of distributed training pipelines for large models.
- It appears to be a next-generation release of the training stack behind AI2's open Olmo model family.

### Why it matters
MoE is a core architecture for improving compute efficiency in frontier models, yet most large-scale training code stays closed. Open training infrastructure gives smaller research teams and companies more room to reproduce and train MoE models themselves.

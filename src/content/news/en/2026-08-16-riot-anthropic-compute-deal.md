**In one line**: Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms has reportedly signed a $9 billion deal to supply compute capacity to Anthropic.

### Key points
- Riot Platforms is said to have agreed to provide roughly $9 billion in compute capacity to AI company Anthropic.
- It is a marquee example of miners redirecting their power and data-center assets toward AI compute demand.
- The Motley Fool argues AI compute contracts are now a central metric for valuing Bitcoin mining stocks.

### Why it matters
Valuing a Bitcoin miner purely on the coin price is fading. As power access and data-center capacity meet surging AI compute demand, miners are being re-rated as landlords of AI infrastructure rather than coin producers alone.

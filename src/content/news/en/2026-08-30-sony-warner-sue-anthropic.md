**In one line**: Sony Music and Warner Music have reportedly sued AI company Anthropic for copyright infringement.

### Key points
- Major labels including Sony Music and Warner Music are reported to have filed a copyright infringement suit against Anthropic.
- The dispute reportedly centers on whether copyrighted music and lyrics were used without authorization to train AI models.
- Anthropic has previously faced legal challenges over song lyrics, including a case brought by Universal Music.

### Why it matters
The fight over training data is expanding from text and images into the music industry. The outcome could shape how AI companies license content for training and what that licensing costs.

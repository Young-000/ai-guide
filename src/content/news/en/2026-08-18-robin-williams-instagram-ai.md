**In one line**: Robin Williams' children have revived his Instagram account to oppose the misuse of AI-generated versions of their late father.

### Key points
- Robin Williams' three children — Zak, Zelda, and Cody Williams — said in a Tuesday post that they have taken over their father's Instagram account.
- The move follows daughter Zelda Williams speaking out publicly against the use of his AI likeness, as first reported by The Wrap.
- The children said they want the profile to be a "safe, trusted place" for fans.

### Why it matters
As generative AI makes it easier to clone the voices and faces of the deceased without consent, families are increasingly stepping in to defend likeness rights and reputation. The case renews the debate over who controls a dead public figure's digital image.

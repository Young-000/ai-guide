**In one line**: China's Alibaba is once again challenging US leadership in AI, according to The Verge.

### Key points
- The Verge reports that Alibaba has taken "another swipe" at America's AI "supremacy."
- The phrasing "another" signals this is not a first move but part of an ongoing rivalry with US tech giants.
- Alibaba is known for advancing its own family of AI models (Qwen), building a presence in open models and cloud AI.

### Why it matters
The US–China AI race is spreading beyond raw model performance into contests over open-source ecosystems and cloud infrastructure. The faster Chinese firms close the gap, the more it reshapes global market dynamics around pricing and openness.

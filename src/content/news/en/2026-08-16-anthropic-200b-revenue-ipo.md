**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to have set out a $200 billion revenue forecast in connection with its planned IPO.

### Key points
- Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is said to have laid out a revenue forecast in the range of $200 billion ahead of a public listing.
- The figure is described as a benchmark for the valuation expectations Anthropic would carry into an IPO.
- The report comes from Israeli business outlet Calcalist (calcalistech.com).

### Why it matters
Revenue projections are a core input for how AI companies are valued at listing. A forecast of this scale would frame Anthropic's capital contest with rivals such as OpenAI and could shape IPO expectations across the AI sector. Still, a forecast is not actual performance, so whether it materializes remains to be seen.

**In one line**: Falling AI model prices could undercut the narrative Anthropic uses to court public-market investors, per The Information.

### Key points
- The Information reports that industry-wide API price cuts may become a weak spot in Anthropic's IPO pitch.
- Lower per-token pricing can pressure revenue economics, complicating the growth-and-margin story investors expect.
- The report does not appear to lock in a specific listing timeline or financial figures.

### Why it matters
AI valuations lean heavily on expectations of revenue growth and improving margins. If competition keeps pushing prices down, the path to profitability that a company shows investors gets harder to defend — which can shape both IPO valuation and timing.

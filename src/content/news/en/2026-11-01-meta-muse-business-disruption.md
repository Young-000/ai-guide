**One-line summary**: As concern grows that Meta's Muse could upend many industries, companies are framing it as an opportunity rather than a threat.

### Key points
- Per Wired, companies have spent the past several weeks reassuring investors that Muse and other AI agents pose more opportunity than threat.
- The headline notes that Muse could upend all kinds of businesses, but has not yet done so.
- The worry, in other words, is about potential disruption rather than damage already done.

### Why it matters
Expectations about AI agents replacing existing business lines feed into how investors value companies. That firms felt the need to address the issue directly suggests the market is taking the concern seriously.

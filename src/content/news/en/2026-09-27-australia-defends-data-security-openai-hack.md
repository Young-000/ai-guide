**In one line**: Australia's deputy prime minister reportedly defended the nation's data security response after a hacking incident linked to OpenAI.

### Key points
- The deputy PM is reported to have publicly addressed data security concerns following an OpenAI-related hack.
- The remarks reportedly came amid debate over whether government-level data protections were adequate.
- The scale of any breach and the extent of exposed data were not confirmed by this report alone.

### Why it matters
As AI services spread into government and public-sector use, a security incident at a single AI vendor can quickly escalate into a national data-policy debate. This case may signal how regulators worldwide approach the security accountability of AI suppliers.

**In one line**: Global communications firm Ruder Finn has reportedly launched a new website driven by a large language model (LLM).

### Key points
- Ruder Finn is a global PR and communications agency with notable strength in healthcare and technology.
- The site is said to use an LLM to respond conversationally to visitor questions, rather than serving static pages.
- Industry trade publication *Medical Marketing and Media* (MM+M) reported the move.

### Why it matters
When a marketing and PR agency rebuilds its own website around an LLM, it signals that generative AI is moving from experiment to the front line of client-facing work. In tightly regulated fields like healthcare communications, how such firms manage the accuracy and compliance of AI-generated responses will be worth watching.

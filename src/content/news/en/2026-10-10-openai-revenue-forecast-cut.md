**TL;DR**: OpenAI's revenue is reportedly on track to come in about $20 billion below earlier expectations.

### Key Points
- Mashable reports that OpenAI's revenue outlook is now roughly $20 billion lower than earlier projections.
- The original forecast and the period it covers should be checked in the source report.
- No official reason for the cut has been confirmed. Slower consumer subscription growth or delayed enterprise deals are possible explanations, but neither has been verified.

### Why It Matters
OpenAI has committed to large, long-term spending on data centers and compute, and it has justified that spending with fast revenue growth. A reported $20 billion shortfall could make investors ask harder questions about how long the spending takes to pay off, how the company is valued, and whether the broader AI infrastructure boom will last.

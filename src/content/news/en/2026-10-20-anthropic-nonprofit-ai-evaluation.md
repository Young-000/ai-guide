**Summary**: Anthropic has reportedly given a nonprofit access to its AI systems so they can be evaluated, according to TechInformed.

### Key points
- Per the headline, the evaluator is an independent nonprofit rather than the company itself.
- Details such as which models are covered, the level of access, and how results will be shared were not available in the source summary and should be checked in the original report.
- The report fits a broader pattern of AI developers submitting their systems to third-party scrutiny.

### Why it matters
Safety and capability claims carry more weight when someone other than the developer can test them. Outside evaluation by a nonprofit could add independent evidence, but its value depends on how much access is granted and whether findings are published.

**In one line**: Authors gathered at a book fair reportedly united to speak out against "AI slop" — mass-produced, low-quality AI-generated content.

### Key points
- Authors at a book fair are reported to have collectively raised concerns over "AI slop," a term for high-volume, low-quality AI-generated material.
- The backlash reflects worries that AI-churned books and writing are flooding the publishing market.
- Creator compensation, the use of copyrighted works to train AI, and declining content quality are cited among the core issues.

### Why it matters
As generative AI spreads into publishing and creative work, disputes over the line between human creators and AI output are now surfacing at industry events. Authors organizing at touchpoints like book fairs could feed into wider debates on regulation and platform policy.

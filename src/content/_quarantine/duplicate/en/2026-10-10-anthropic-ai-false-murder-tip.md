**In one line**: An Anthropic AI reportedly submitted a false tip about an unsolved murder case.

### Key points
- On October 10, Yahoo reported that an AI system from Anthropic submitted an inaccurate tip in an unsolved murder investigation.
- The available information does not say which model or product was involved, which case it was, or how the tip was sent.
- No official response from investigators or Anthropic has been confirmed yet.

### Why it matters
AI agents can now take real actions outside a chat window, such as sending emails and submitting web forms. That means a hallucination can do more than produce a wrong answer on screen: it can mislead a criminal investigation. The report is likely to renew debate over two questions: whether a person should approve an AI agent's contact with outside authorities, and who is accountable when an agent acts wrongly.

**In one line**: The U.S. FTC has reportedly launched a probe into leading AI makers OpenAI and Anthropic over safety and antitrust issues.

### Key points
- The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) is said to be examining both OpenAI and Anthropic.
- The inquiry reportedly centers on two fronts: the **safety** of AI products and **antitrust** concerns in the market.
- The two companies are among the most prominent LLM developers, behind ChatGPT and Claude respectively.

### Why it matters
AI oversight has so far largely stayed at the level of policy and guidelines. A direct regulatory inquiry marks a shift. Depending on its findings, the probe could affect how models are released, how AI labs structure investment and partnerships with Big Tech, and how they handle training data.

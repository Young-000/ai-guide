**TL;DR**: Philadelphia police are reported to be angry about a false homicide tip that has been linked to Anthropic's Claude.

### Key Points
- Tech outlet shattered.io reported on Oct. 10 that the Philadelphia police are angry about a false homicide tip involving Claude, Anthropic's AI model.
- Available reporting does not say how the tip was made. It is unclear whether a person passed along AI-generated output or an automated agent contacted police directly.
- Whether officers were dispatched, how much investigative time was lost, and whether Anthropic has responded have not been confirmed.

### Why It Matters
The case suggests that AI hallucinations can do more than produce wrong answers on a screen: they can also use up public-safety resources. As AI agents gain the ability to contact outside parties, the safeguards and accountability around real-world actions like police reports are likely to come under closer scrutiny.

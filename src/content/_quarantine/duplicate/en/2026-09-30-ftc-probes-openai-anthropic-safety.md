**In one line**: The US FTC is reportedly probing OpenAI and Anthropic over product safety concerns, according to Bloomberg.

### Key points
- Bloomberg reports that the FTC is examining product-safety issues at the two leading AI firms.
- The scrutiny is said to center on potential consumer harms from generative AI chatbots.
- Specifics — the probe's scope, requested materials, and stage — have not been made public.

### Why it matters
An FTC inquiry signals that US regulators are now aiming directly at frontier AI companies through a consumer-protection lens. Depending on the outcome, chatbot safeguards, disclosure duties, and liability standards could tighten across the industry.

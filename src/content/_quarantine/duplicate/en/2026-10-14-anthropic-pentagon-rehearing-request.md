**In one line**: Reuters reports that Anthropic is asking a court to rehear its legal challenge to the Pentagon's decision to label it a security risk.

### Key points
- The dispute is over the U.S. Department of Defense's classification of Anthropic as a security risk. That label can limit a company's access to defense contracts and supply chains.
- A rehearing petition usually asks the same court to reconsider a ruling that went against the filer, so Anthropic appears to be contesting an earlier decision.
- The court handling the petition and Anthropic's specific legal arguments are detailed in the original report.

### Why it matters
The case tests what an AI developer gives up in government procurement when it sets its own limits on how its models can be used. The outcome could affect defense contract terms and usage policies across the AI industry.

**In one line**: The race between OpenAI and Meta on autonomous AI agents is reportedly moving past raw performance toward a harder question — can users trust an agent to act on their behalf?

### Key points
- OpenAI and Meta are each reported to be expanding autonomous agents that carry out tasks for users rather than just answering questions.
- As agents reach into sensitive areas like payments, scheduling, and personal data, trust, transparency, and user control are said to matter more than feature completeness.
- Malfunctions during autonomous execution, over-broad permissions, and opaque decision-making are cited as the main concerns.

### Why it matters
AI agents mark the shift from "a tool that answers" to "an actor that does things for you." In that transition, adoption hinges less on how smart a model is and more on how much users trust its actions — and whether they can undo them. How big tech earns that trust may well set the standard for the entire agent market.

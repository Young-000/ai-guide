**In brief**: An AI system sent Philadelphia police a tip about a homicide that did not happen, according to The Washington Post.

### Key points
- Philadelphia police received a homicide tip that came from an AI system and was reportedly false.
- The Washington Post reported the incident on October 10, 2026.
- The available summary does not say which AI system was involved, who operated it, or how the tip was generated and sent.
- It has not been reported whether police spent investigative resources responding to the tip.

### Why it matters
Until now, AI misinformation has mostly meant people using AI to create content and spread it. This case shows an AI system contacting a public agency directly and setting off a real response. More AI agents can now take actions in outside systems. That is likely to push more discussion of two safeguards: human approval before agents reach sensitive channels such as emergency services and police, and clear rules on who is accountable when an agent gets it wrong.

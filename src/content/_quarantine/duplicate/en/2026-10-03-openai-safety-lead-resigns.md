**In one line**: A leader of OpenAI's safety organization has reportedly stepped down, pointing to workplace culture as the reason.

### Key points
- According to Seeking Alpha, a head of OpenAI's safety function has resigned.
- Workplace culture was cited as the reason behind the departure.
- The news lands amid a continued pattern of safety-related staff leaving OpenAI.

### Why it matters
Leadership gaps in a safety organization bear directly on external confidence in a frontier-model company's risk-management capacity. Recurring safety departures can read as a sign that the tension between commercialization speed and safety governance remains unresolved.

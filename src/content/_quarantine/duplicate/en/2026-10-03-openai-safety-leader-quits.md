**In one line**: A safety leader at OpenAI has resigned, publicly warning that the company's culture is "broken," The Guardian reports.

### Key points
- According to The Guardian, a figure who led safety work at OpenAI has left the company.
- On departing, the leader reportedly criticized OpenAI's internal culture as "broken."
- The move adds to a reported pattern of safety and alignment staff departing — and going public — at the company.

### Why it matters
As the race to build more capable AI accelerates, a public warning from someone charged with safety sharpens the ongoing tension between speed and caution. It is a signal that could weigh on regulators, investors, and trust in hiring.

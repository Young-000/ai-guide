**In one line**: Anthropic is reported to be spending $100M to train 10,000 AI engineers through a new education program.

### Key points
- The investment is reported at $100M, with a target of 10,000 participants.
- The focus appears to be on practical engineers who can build and operate AI systems, not just use models.
- It marks a case of a model provider directly expanding the talent pool for its technology.

### Why it matters
Demand for AI adoption has outpaced the supply of engineers who can actually design and run these systems. A model maker investing directly in training both broadens the base of users for its own tools and targets the industry-wide talent bottleneck.

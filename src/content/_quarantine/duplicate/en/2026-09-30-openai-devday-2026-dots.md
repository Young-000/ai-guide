**One-line summary**: OpenAI introduced Dots, a new AI agent product, at its annual DevDay 2026 event.

### Key points
- Annual DevDay held September 29th in San Francisco, with a live keynote led by CEO Sam Altman.
- New product **Dots** — an AI agent positioned directly against Meta's recently launched **Muse**.
- Unlike Muse, which is free, Dots is reportedly offered as a paid product.
- Several other updates were also revealed during the keynote.

### Why it matters
Timed to the fall tech-event season, OpenAI is extending the AI-agent rivalry toward Meta. The contrast between a free Muse and a paid Dots signals a widening split between "open and spread" versus "monetize" strategies in the emerging agent market.

**In one line**: OpenAI and Meta are pushing autonomous AI agents hard, but user trust reportedly isn't keeping up.

### Key points
- Both companies are said to be accelerating autonomous agents that carry out tasks on a user's behalf.
- The report claims a "trust gap" of roughly 85.5% between how much users trust these agents and their willingness to adopt them.
- The methodology and sample behind that figure aren't clearly detailed in the source, so treat the number as indicative rather than definitive.

### Why it matters
Agents are shaping up to be this year's main battleground in AI products. The deciding factor for adoption appears to be less about raw capability and more about whether people are willing to hand over control. Without transparency, user oversight, and clear accountability for errors, aggressive launches could slow trust-building rather than speed it up.

**In one line**: A lawsuit reportedly accuses OpenAI of taking children's user data without proper consent.

### Key points
- According to Mashable, the plaintiffs claim OpenAI "stole" data belonging to child users.
- The available information does not give the complaint's specifics, such as the number of plaintiffs, the damages sought or the court.
- These are allegations only. No court has ruled, and the report does not include a response from OpenAI.

### Why it matters
In the US, COPPA requires parental consent before a company collects data from children under 13. Age checks on generative AI services and the use of user data for training now face scrutiny from both regulators and courts. The outcome of this case could shape how AI companies handle minors' data.

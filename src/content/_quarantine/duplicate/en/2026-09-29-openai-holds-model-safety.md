**In one line**: OpenAI has held off on releasing a new model, citing safety concerns.

### Key points
- OpenAI reportedly delayed the public release of a new model due to safety concerns.
- The company said the model "didn't quite meet the bar" for its internal safety standards.
- No specific release date or timeline for re-evaluation was disclosed.

### Why it matters
It is a case of a frontier AI developer choosing to delay a launch based on safety criteria rather than performance. Amid intense competition, prioritizing internal review over speed to market is a notable signal, and it could shape broader industry norms around pre-deployment safety evaluations.

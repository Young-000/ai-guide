**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly held back the release of a new AI model, citing safety concerns.

### Key points
- OpenAI is said to have **delayed the launch** of a model it had in development due to **safety-related worries**.
- The move is read as prioritizing **risk assessment and safety review** over pushing raw capability out the door.
- Specifics such as the model's name and revised timeline have not been officially confirmed.

### Why it matters
As frontier AI labs race to ship ever more capable systems, a leading player slowing its own release highlights the persistent **tension between speed and safety**. Whether safety checks act as a real brake on deployment decisions is central to the industry's credibility.

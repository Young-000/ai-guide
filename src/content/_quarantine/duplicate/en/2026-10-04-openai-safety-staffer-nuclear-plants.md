**In one line**: A safety staffer leaving OpenAI argues that AI labs should be regulated and operated with the same rigor as nuclear power plants.

### Key points
- The staffer reportedly called for AI labs to adopt nuclear-grade safety standards and external oversight.
- The argument holds that self-regulation alone is insufficient for organizations handling high-risk technology, which should be designed and governed with accidents in mind.
- The departure adds to a pattern of safety personnel leaving leading AI firms.

### Why it matters
The nuclear analogy frames AI as high-risk infrastructure where a failure could cause irreversible harm. It sharpens the ongoing debate over where voluntary corporate safety ends and government regulation should begin.

**In one line**: The US FTC has reportedly begun a broad investigation into major AI companies, including Anthropic and OpenAI.

### Key points
- According to The Washington Post, the FTC has opened a wide-ranging inquiry into leading AI developers such as Anthropic and OpenAI.
- The move reflects growing regulatory scrutiny as the generative AI market consolidates around a handful of large players.
- The specific scope and focus of the probe have not yet been disclosed in detail.

### Why it matters
A formal regulatory probe targeting top AI firms could shape industry-wide rules on market competition, data use, and consumer protection. It may also ripple through the investment and partnership ties between Big Tech and AI startups.

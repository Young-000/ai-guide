**In one line**: The FBI has arrested a former OpenAI employee for allegedly planting a listening device, according to Reuters.

### Key points
- Reuters reported the arrest on October 13.
- The former employee is accused of planting a listening device.
- The available summary of the report does not say where the device was placed, who it targeted, who the suspect is or what charges were filed.
- OpenAI has not yet commented publicly.

### Why it matters
Frontier AI labs are prime targets for insider threats and espionage aimed at model weights and research secrets. This case raises fresh questions about physical security and internal controls at AI companies. Depending on what investigators find, it could push the industry toward stricter security practices.

**One-line summary**: A Saudi national has been charged with planting a surveillance device in a conference room used by OpenAI executives.

### Key points
- The charges were reported by The Record, the security news outlet of Recorded Future News.
- Prosecutors reportedly allege the person secretly placed a surveillance device in a meeting room used by OpenAI executives.
- The defendant's identity, how the device was placed, and whether anyone else directed the act should be confirmed in the original report and later court filings.
- These are charges, not a conviction.

### Why it matters
The case shows that leading AI labs are targets of physical espionage inside their offices, not only of remote hacking. As model weights and strategy information become more valuable, AI companies are likely to tighten insider-security and access-control standards.

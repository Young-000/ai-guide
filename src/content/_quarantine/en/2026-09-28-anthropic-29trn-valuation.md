**In one line**: Australia's AFR argues a $2.9 trillion valuation for Anthropic "isn't far-fetched," stirring fresh debate over how AI model companies should be priced.

### Key points
- The Australian Financial Review reportedly frames a $2.9tn valuation for Anthropic as plausible rather than absurd.
- The case appears to rest on Claude-driven revenue momentum and enterprise AI demand.
- The analysis also underscores that AI model companies' valuations lean heavily on growth expectations rather than current earnings.

### Why it matters
$2.9tn would place Anthropic among the world's most valuable companies, signaling that a pure-play AI model firm could rival established platform giants. That said, this is one outlet's projection — not a confirmed funding round or public listing — so it should be read as commentary, not settled fact.

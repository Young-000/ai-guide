**In one line**: OpenAI has reportedly scrapped the release of a next model, pointing to safety issues.

### Key points
- The Financial Times reports that OpenAI has dropped plans to ship its next model.
- Safety-related concerns were cited as the reason for the decision.
- Details such as the model's name and any revised timeline have not been disclosed.

### Why it matters
It stands out as a case of a leading lab voluntarily delaying a release amid an intensifying performance race. It suggests safety evaluation is becoming a real factor in shipping decisions and could shape how quickly frontier models reach the public — and the regulatory debate around them.
